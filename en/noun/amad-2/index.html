What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَمَدُ

    the columns (stupovi) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعَمَدَ

    the columns (stupove) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعَمَدِ

    in the columns (na stupovima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعَمَدِ

    from the columns (od stupova) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْعَمَدِ

    the length of the columns (dužina stupova) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَمَدُهُ

    his/its columns (njegovi stupovi) · Bosnian nominative
