What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْاِخْتِلافُ

    the difference (razlika) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْاِخْتِلافَ

    the difference (razliku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْاِخْتِلافِ

    in the difference (u razlici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْاِخْتِلافِ

    from the difference (od razlike) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ الْاِخْتِلافِ

    the reason of the difference (razlog razlike) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    اِخْتِلافُهُ

    his/its difference (njegova razlika) · Bosnian nominative
