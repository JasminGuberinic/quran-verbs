What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمِيثاقُ

    the pact (ugovor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمِيثَٰقَ

    the pact (ugovor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمِيثاقِ

    in the pact (u ugovoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمِيثاقِ

    from the pact (iz ugovora) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نَصُّ الْمِيثاقِ

    the text of the pact (tekst ugovora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مِيثَٰقَكُمْ from the Quran

    your pact (vaš ugovor) · Bosnian accusative
