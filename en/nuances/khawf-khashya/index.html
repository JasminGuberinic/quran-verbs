Nuances

Fear

خَوْف · خَشْيَة

What they share

Both words mean fear of something that may harm; خَشْيَة adds to that fear reverence and knowledge of the greatness of the one who is feared.

How to tell them apart

Fear of anything dangerous → خَوْف; fear joined with awe, most often toward Allah → خَشْيَة. 13:21 holds both verbs: they “fear (يَخْشَوْنَ) their Lord, and dread (يَخَافُونَ) a woeful reckoning”.

خَوْف fear

26× in the Quran · Noun page →

The general word for fear. Ar-Rāġib describes it as expecting something unwanted from a sign that is supposed or known. Fear of danger, hunger, an enemy; the opposite of security (أَمْن).

خَشْيَة awe, reverent fear

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, fear mixed with reverence, most often born of knowledge of what is feared. Yet the word is also said of fear of a thing itself (17:31), so this is a tendency, not a rule.
