أَمْor; or is it that…?

137× in the Quran

What it does

A conjunction of questions. When with the hamza it asks ‘this or that’, it joins two choices (muttaṣila); when it stands alone, it opens a new question: ‘or is it that…?’ (munqaṭiʿa).

Uses

  1. ‘Or’: this or that
  2. ‘Or is it that…?’: a new question

‘Or’: this or that

conjunction

After the question hamza it asks which of two choices; after the hamza of equalization it makes them equal. The word after it shares the case of the word it is joined to.

‘Or is it that…?’: a new question

conjunction

With no hamza before it, أَمْ breaks off and moves on (al-iḍrāb, like بَلْ). According to Ibn Hišām it sometimes does only that, and sometimes it also asks, mostly reproachfully (بَلْ أَ…).

Sources

  • Ibn Hišām al-Anṣārī, Muġnī al-labīb ʿan kutub al-aʿārīb
