What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجَهالَةُ

    the ignorance (neznanje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجَهالَةَ

    the ignorance (neznanje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْجَهالَةِ

    in the ignorance (u neznanju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْجَهالَةِ

    from the ignorance (od neznanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نَتِيجَةُ الْجَهالَةِ

    the result of the ignorance (posljedica neznanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جَهالَتُهُ

    his/its ignorance (njegovo neznanje) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

    Ignorance causes problems.

    The people were freed from ignorance.

    The result of ignorance is clear.
