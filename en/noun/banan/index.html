What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَنانُ

    the fingertips (jagodice) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَنانَ

    the fingertips (jagodice) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَنانِ

    in the fingertips (na jagodicama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَنانِ

    from the fingertips (od jagodica) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَمْسُ الْبَنانِ

    the touch of the fingertips (dodir jagodica) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَنَانَهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its fingertips (njegove jagodice) · Bosnian accusative
