What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ

    the glass (staklo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الزُّجاجَةَ

    the glass (staklo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الزُّجاجَةِ

    in the glass (u staklu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الزُّجاجَةِ

    from the glass (iz stakla) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ الزُّجاجَةِ

    the color of the glass (boja stakla) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    زُجاجَتُهُ

    his/its glass (njegovo staklo) · Bosnian nominative
