What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَوْتَادُ

    the pegs (klinovi) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَوْتَادَ

    the pegs (klinove) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْأَوْتَادِ

    in the pegs (na klinovima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْأَوْتَادِ

    from the pegs (od klinova) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عَدَدُ ٱلْأَوْتَادِ

    the number of the pegs (broj klinova) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَوْتَادُهُ

    his/its pegs (njegovi klinovi) · Bosnian nominative
