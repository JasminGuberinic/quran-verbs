Nuances

Injustice and aggression

ظُلْم · بَغْي · عُدْوَان

What they share

All three words mean doing wrong; they differ in whether putting a thing in the wrong place, overbearing excess, or crossing a limit in attack is in view.

How to tell them apart

Wrong in the widest sense — towards oneself and towards Allah too → ظُلْم; overbearing excess, oppressing others → بَغْي; overstepping a limit, hostile attack → عُدْوَان.

ظُلْم injustice, wrong

20× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, putting a thing where it does not belong. The widest word; he cites sages who divide it into wrong against Allah (shirk), against people, and against oneself.

بَغْي oppression, transgression

11× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, seeking to go beyond the right measure. ar-Rāġib also names a laudable baghy (going beyond justice to kindness); in the Quran the noun is mostly overbearing wrong against others, and the rivalry out of envy through which those given the Scripture fell into dispute.

عُدْوَان aggression, hostility

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

From a root meaning to go beyond: wrong as overstepping, hostile attack. Often paired with sin (إِثْم).
