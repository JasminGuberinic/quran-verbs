What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْخُسْرَانُ

    the loss (gubitak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخُسْرانَ

    the loss (gubitak) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخُسْرانِ

    in the loss (u gubitku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْخُسْرانِ

    from the loss (od gubitka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ الْخُسْرانِ

    the cause of the loss (uzrok gubitka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خُسْرانُهُ

    his/its loss (njegov gubitak) · Bosnian nominative
