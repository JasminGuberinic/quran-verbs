What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحِكْمَةُ

    the wisdom (mudrost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْحِكْمَةَ

    the wisdom (mudrost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْحِكْمَةِ

    from the wisdom (od mudrosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْحِكْمَةِ

    in the wisdom (u mudrosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلِمَاتُ ٱلْحِكْمَةِ

    the words of the wisdom (riječi mudrosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِكْمَتُهُ

    his/its wisdom (njegova mudrost) · Bosnian nominative
