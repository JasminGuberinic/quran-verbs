Nuances

To send down

أَنْزَلَ · نَزَّلَ

What they share

Both verbs, from one root, mean ‘to send down’ and both are said of revelation; they differ in form: أَنْزَلَ (IV) and نَزَّلَ (II).

How to tell them apart

Sending down in general → أَنْزَلَ; according to ar-Rāġib, when the Quran and the angels are described, sending down piece by piece, one part after another → نَزَّلَ. A tendency, not a strict rule (25:32).

أَنْزَلَ to send down, to reveal

183× in the Quran · Verb page →

The general verb for sending from above: revelation, but also rain, iron, tranquillity. According to ar-Rāġib, inzāl is more general than tanzīl.

نَزَّلَ to send down (in stages)

62× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, when the Quran and the angels are described, tanzīl applies to what is sent down in parts, one after another, while inzāl is general. The Quran's gradual revelation over the years fits that sense well.
