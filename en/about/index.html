About

Fiʿl is a free study aid for the Arabic of the Quran: verbs, nouns, adjectives and particles, with forms, ayat, audio and practice.

Who made it

Fiʿl was made by a programmer together with Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding tool. It is an attempt to use modern technology, AI and AI tools, to make learning the Arabic of the Quran easier.

How it is made

The explanations, the translations into the site's languages, the practice sentences and the reviews are produced with AI. They are checked by automatic tests: grammar, every form against the Quranic Arabic Corpus, licence checks and audio checks. English and Bosnian are the main languages; the other languages are AI translations.

Please note

This site is a learning aid. It is not a religious authority, not tafsir and not a fatwa, and it may contain mistakes. The Arabic text of the Quran itself comes from Tanzil (a verified text); everything around it is a study aid only. For any religious question, consult qualified scholars and trusted translations.

Privacy

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no account.
  • Your progress and settings (the language you chose, the verbs you know) are kept only in your own browser (localStorage). They are never sent anywhere; clearing the site data in your browser deletes them.
  • The pages, the audio and the data are static files served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests, as any web host does.
  • To work offline, the site keeps copies of the pages you open in your browser's cache, on your own device.

A page may contact these third parties and no others: Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) loads the typefaces when a page opens, and the recitation of a whole ayah is streamed from the Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network, behind alquran.cloud) only when you press play on it. Links to quran.com, quranwbw.com and islam.ba are ordinary links: they are contacted only if you click.
