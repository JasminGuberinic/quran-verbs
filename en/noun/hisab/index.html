What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْحِسَابُ

    the account (račun) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْحِسَابَ

    the account (račun) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْحِسَابِ

    from the account (od računa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْحِسَابِ

    in the account (u računu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    رَقْمُ ٱلْحِسَابِ

    the number of the account (broj računa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِسَابُهُمْ from the Quran

    their account (njihov račun) · Bosnian nominative
