What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْوَبالُ

    the consequence (posljedica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْوَبالَ

    the consequence (posljedicu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْوَبالِ

    in the consequence (u posljedici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْوَبالِ

    from the consequence (od posljedice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ثِقَلُ الْوَبالِ

    the weight of the consequence (težina posljedice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    وَبالُهُ

    his/its consequence (njegova posljedica) · Bosnian nominative

    The consequence of laziness was large.

    عَرَفَ الطَّالِبُ وَبَالَ الْكَسَلِ

    The cause of the bad consequence is laziness.
