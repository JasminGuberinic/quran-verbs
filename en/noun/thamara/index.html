What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الثَّمَرَةُ

    the fruit (plod) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الثَّمَرَةَ

    the fruit (plod) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الثَّمَرَةِ

    from the fruit (iz ploda) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الثَّمَرَةِ

    in the fruit (u plodu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَعْمُ الثَّمَرَةِ

    the taste of the fruit (okus ploda) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ثَمَرَتُهُ

    his/its fruit (njegov plod) · Bosnian nominative
