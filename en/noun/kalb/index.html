What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكَلْبُ

    the dog (pas) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكَلْبَ

    the dog (psa) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْكَلْبِ

    in the dog (na psu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْكَلْبِ

    from the dog (od psa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ ٱلْكَلْبِ

    the sound of the dog (glas psa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَلْبُهُمْ from the Quran

    their dog (njihov pas) · Bosnian nominative
