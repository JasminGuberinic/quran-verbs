What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسَّلَٰمُ

    the peace (mir) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسَّلَٰمَ

    the peace (mir) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلسَّلَٰمِ

    with the peace (s mirom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسَّلَٰمِ

    in the peace (u miru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَيَّامُ ٱلسَّلَٰمِ

    the days of the peace (dani mira) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَلَٰمُهُ

    his/its peace (njegov mir) · Bosnian nominative

    The village lived in peace.

    أَيَّامُ السَّلَامِ جَمِيلَةٌ

    The days of peace are beautiful.
