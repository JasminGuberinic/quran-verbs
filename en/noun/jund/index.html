What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجُنْدُ

    the army (vojska) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجُنْدَ

    the army (vojsku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْجُنْدِ

    with the army (s vojskom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْجُنْدِ

    from the army (od vojske) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَائِدُ الْجُنْدِ

    the leader of the army (vođa vojske) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جُنُودُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its armies (njegove vojske) · Bosnian nominative
