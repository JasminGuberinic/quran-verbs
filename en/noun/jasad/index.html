What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجَسَدُ

    the body (tijelo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجَسَدَ

    the body (tijelo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْجَسَدِ

    in the body (u tijelu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْجَسَدِ

    from the body (iz tijela) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    حَرَارَةُ الْجَسَدِ

    the temperature of the body (temperatura tijela) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جَسَدُهُ

    his/its body (njegovo tijelo) · Bosnian nominative
