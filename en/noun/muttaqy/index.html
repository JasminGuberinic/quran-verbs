What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْمُتَّقُونَ

    the pious people (bogobojaznici) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ

    the pious people (bogobojaznike) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ

    on the pious people (na bogobojaznicima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْمُتَّقِينَ

    with the pious people (s bogobojaznicima) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَيْتُ ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ

    the house of the pious people (kuća bogobojaznika) · Bosnian genitive
