What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّنَةُ

    the year (godina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّنَةَ

    the year (godinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّنَةِ

    in the year (u godini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّنَةِ

    from the year (od godine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بِدَايَةُ السَّنَةِ

    the beginning of the year (početak godine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَنَتُهُ

    his/its year (njegova godina) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    مَرَّتِ السَّنَةُ سَرِيعًا

    The year passed quickly.

  • object (accusative)

    بَدَأَ الطُّلَّابُ السَّنَةَ الْجَدِيدَةَ

    The students began the new year.

  • after a preposition (genitive)

    تُسَافِرُ الْعَائِلَةُ مَرَّتَيْنِ فِي السَّنَةِ

    The family travels two times a year.

  • possessor (genitive)

    بِدَايَةُ السَّنَةِ قَرِيبَةٌ

    The beginning of the year is near.
