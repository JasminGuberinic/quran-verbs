Particles · preposition

كَـlike, as

287× in the Quran

What it does

A preposition: the noun after it is in the genitive (كَٱلْفَرَاشِ). It is written joined to the word after it. It does not take a pronoun; then مِثْلُهُ ‘like him’ is said.

See a noun's cases, the genitive after a preposition among them, on: يَوْمَ →

Uses

  1. ‘like’: comparison
  2. Emphasis: ‘nothing is like Him’

‘like’: comparison

preposition

Comparison (at-tašbīh).

The noun after it is genitive.

Emphasis: ‘nothing is like Him’

preposition

Ibn Hišām reports that in this ayah the kāf is, for most (al-akṯarūn), additional (zāʾida) and emphatic: ‘there is nothing like Him’; others explain it differently.

The noun after it is genitive.
