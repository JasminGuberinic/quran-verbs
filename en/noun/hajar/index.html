What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحَجَرُ

    the stone (kamen) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْحَجَرَ

    the stone (kamen) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحَجَرِ

    in the stone (na kamenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحَجَرِ

    from the stone (od kamena) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَزْنُ الْحَجَرِ

    the weight of the stone (težina kamena) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حَجَرُهُ

    his/its stone (njegov kamen) · Bosnian nominative
