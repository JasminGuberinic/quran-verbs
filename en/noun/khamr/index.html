What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْخَمْرُ

    the wine (vino) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَمْرَ

    the wine (vino) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْخَمْرِ

    in the wine (u vinu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْخَمْرِ

    from the wine (iz vina) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    زُجَاجَةُ ٱلْخَمْرِ

    the bottle of the wine (boca vina) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَمْرُهُ

    his/its wine (njegovo vino) · Bosnian nominative
