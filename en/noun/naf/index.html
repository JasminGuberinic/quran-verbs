What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النَّفْعُ

    the benefit (korist) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النَّفْعَ

    the benefit (korist) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ النَّفْعِ

    from the benefit (od koristi) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي النَّفْعِ

    in the benefit (u koristi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مِقْدَارُ النَّفْعِ

    the amount of the benefit (mjera koristi) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَفْعُهُ

    his/its benefit (njegova korist) · Bosnian nominative
