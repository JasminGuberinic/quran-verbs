What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْأَمْنُ

    the security (sigurnost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَمْنَ

    the security (sigurnost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْأَمْنِ

    from the security (od sigurnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْأَمْنِ

    in the security (u sigurnosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَهَمِّيَّةُ ٱلْأَمْنِ

    the importance of the security (važnost sigurnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَمْنُهُ

    his/its security (njegova sigurnost) · Bosnian nominative
