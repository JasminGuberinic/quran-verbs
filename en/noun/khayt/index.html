What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْخَيْطُ

    the thread (nit) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَيْطَ

    the thread (nit) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْخَيْطِ

    from the thread (od niti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْخَيْطِ

    in the thread (na niti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلْخَيْطِ

    the color of the thread (boja niti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَيْطُهُ

    his/its thread (njegova nit) · Bosnian nominative
