What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَرْشُ

    the throne (prijestolje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْعَرْشَ

    the throne (prijestolje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلْعَرْشِ

    on the throne (na prijestolju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْعَرْشِ

    with the throne (prijestoljem) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَارِثُ ٱلْعَرْشِ

    the heir of the throne (nasljednik prijestolja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَرْشُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its throne (njegovo prijestolje) · Bosnian nominative
