What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْخُرُوجُ

    the departure (izlazak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْخُرُوجَ

    the departure (izlazak) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْخُرُوجِ

    to the departure (do izlaska) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْخُرُوجِ

    in the departure (u izlasku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَقْتُ ٱلْخُرُوجِ

    the time of the departure (vrijeme izlaska) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خُرُوجُهُ

    his/its departure (njegov izlazak) · Bosnian nominative
