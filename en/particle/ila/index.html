إِلَىto, towards

742× in the Quran

What it does

A preposition: the noun after it is in the genitive (a kasra at the end: ٱلْأَرْضِ, أَرْضٍ). A pronoun is joined to it as a suffix (إِلَيْهِ ‘to him’ – ى becomes يْ).

Uses

  1. ‘to’: the goal
  2. ‘until’: the end of a time

‘to’: the goal

preposition

The end point of a movement (intihāʾ al-ġāya), paired with مِنْ.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘until’: the end of a time

preposition

The end point in time.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘together with’

preposition

When one thing is added to another, إِلَى can mean ‘together with’ (al-maʿiyya): Ibn Hišām reports this from the Kufans and some Basrans, with 3:52. The Basran school holds that one preposition does not stand for another; in 4:2 Ibn Hišām, after az-Zamaḫšarī, reads ‘consume’ with an implied sense of ‘add’ (at-taḍmīn).

The noun after it is genitive.
