What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمُرْسَلُ

    the envoy (izaslanik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمُرْسَلَ

    the envoy (izaslanika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمُرْسَلِ

    from the envoy (od izaslanika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِ

    on the envoy (na izaslaniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلَامُ الْمُرْسَلِ

    the speech of the envoy (govor izaslanika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مُرْسَلُهُ

    his/its envoy (njegov izaslanik) · Bosnian nominative
