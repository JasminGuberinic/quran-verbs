Nuances

Entering

دَخَلَ · وَلَجَ

What they share

Both words mean to enter. According to ar-Rāġib, دُخُول is the opposite of going out — into a place, a time or deeds — while وُلُوج is entering into something narrow.

How to tell them apart

To enter — the general word: a town, Paradise, the religion → دَخَلَ; to pass into something narrow, to penetrate → وَلَجَ.

دَخَلَ to enter

76× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the opposite of going out; it is said of place, time and deeds. The most general word: entering a town, a house, Paradise, even the religion.

وَلَجَ to pass into, to penetrate

3× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, entering into something narrow. The best-known case: a camel passing through a needle's eye — an image of the impossible.
