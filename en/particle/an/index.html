أَنْthat, to

616× in the Quran

What it does

With the verb after it, it makes a whole that stands as a noun (أَنْ تَصُومُوا = ‘fasting’). A present-tense verb after it is subjunctive.

Uses

  1. ‘that, to’: a verbal noun
  2. ‘namely’: the content of a message

‘that, to’: a verbal noun

conjunction (verbal noun) · corpus: 569×

Ibn Hišām calls it the one that makes a verbal noun and puts the verb in the subjunctive (al-maṣdariyya an-nāṣiba).

The verb after it is subjunctive.

‘namely’: the content of a message

explaining particle · corpus: 47×

After a word that means speech but is not ‘say’ (reveal, call), أَنْ gives the content: ‘…that’. Grammarians who accept it call it the explaining one (al-mufassira) and set conditions for it; the Kufans deny it, and Ibn Hišām finds their view sound. It does not change the verb.
