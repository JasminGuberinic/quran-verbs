What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسَّاعَةُ

    the hour (sat) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسَّاعَةَ

    the hour (sat) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسَّاعَةِ

    in the hour (u satu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلسَّاعَةِ

    with the hour (sa satom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نِصْفُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ

    the half of the hour (pola sata) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ساعَتُهُ

    his/its hour (njegov sat) · Bosnian nominative
