What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَكَانَةُ

    the position (položaj) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَكَانَةَ

    the position (položaj) · Bosnian accusative

    عَلَى الْمَكَانَةِ

    on the position (na položaju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَكَانَةِ

    in the position (u položaju) · Bosnian locative

    مَكَانَتُهُ

    his/its position (njegov položaj) · Bosnian nominative
