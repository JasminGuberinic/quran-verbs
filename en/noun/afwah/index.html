What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَفْوَاهُ

    the mouths (usta) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَفْوَاهَ

    the mouths (usta) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْأَفْوَاهِ

    with the mouths (ustima) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَفْوَاهِ

    from the mouths (iz usta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    شَكْلُ الْأَفْوَاهِ

    the form of the mouths (oblik usta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَفْوَاهُهُ

    his/its mouths (njegova usta) · Bosnian nominative
