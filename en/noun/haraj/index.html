What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحَرَجُ

    the difficulty (teškoća) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحَرَجَ

    the difficulty (teškoću) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحَرَجِ

    from the difficulty (od teškoće) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحَرَجِ

    in the difficulty (u teškoći) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ الْحَرَجِ

    the reason of the difficulty (razlog teškoće) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حَرَجُهُ

    his/its difficulty (njegova teškoća) · Bosnian nominative
