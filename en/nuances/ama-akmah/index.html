Nuances

Blind and born blind

أَعْمَى · أَكْمَه

What they share

Both words mean that a person cannot see. They differ in breadth: one is the general word for losing the sight of the eyes, and also for blindness of the heart; the other a narrow word for one born without sight.

How to tell them apart

Blind — in the eyes or in the heart (insight); in the Quran mostly figurative blindness to the truth → أَعْمَى; born blind — ar-Rāġib: born with the eye effaced, also said of one whose sight goes; only of Jesus' miracle (3:49, 5:110) → أَكْمَه.

أَعْمَى blind

21× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, blindness (ʿamā) is said both of losing the sight of the eyes and of losing insight; indeed, beside the loss of insight, the loss of sight is not even counted as blindness (22:46). In the Quran the word is sometimes literal (no blame on the blind), and most often figurative: blind to the truth as against the one who sees.

أَكْمَه born blind

2× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, akmah is one born with the eye effaced, and it is also said of one whose sight goes. In the Quran it is only twice, both times of Jesus' miracle, which comes about by Allah's leave: the healing of the born-blind and the leper.
