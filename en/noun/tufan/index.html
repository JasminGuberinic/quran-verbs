What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلطُّوفَانُ

    the flood (potop) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلطُّوفَانَ

    the flood (potop) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الطُّوفانِ

    in the flood (u potopu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الطُّوفانِ

    from the flood (od potopa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مَاءُ الطُّوفانِ

    the water of the flood (voda potopa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طُوفانُهُ

    his/its flood (njegov potop) · Bosnian nominative
