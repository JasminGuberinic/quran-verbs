What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَوْعِدُ

    the appointment (termin) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَوْعِدَ

    the appointment (termin) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَوْعِدِ

    in the appointment (u terminu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمَوْعِدِ

    from the appointment (od termina) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَقْتُ الْمَوْعِدِ

    the time of the appointment (vrijeme termina) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَوْعِدُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its appointment (njegov termin) · Bosnian nominative
