What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكَوْكَبُ

    the planet (planeta) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكَوْكَبَ

    the planet (planetu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْكَوْكَبِ

    in the planet (na planeti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْكَوْكَبِ

    from the planet (iz planete) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَطْحُ الْكَوْكَبِ

    the surface of the planet (površina planete) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَوْكَبُهُ

    his/its planet (njegova planeta) · Bosnian nominative
