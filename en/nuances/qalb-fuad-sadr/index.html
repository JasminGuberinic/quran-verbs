Nuances

Heart and breast

قَلْب · فُؤَاد · صَدْر

What they share

All three words name the inner seat of understanding, faith and feeling; they differ in which side of it is in view.

How to tell them apart

The heart that understands and believes → قَلْب; the heart aflame with feeling → فُؤَاد; the breast that holds the heart — expansion, constriction and what is hidden → صَدْر.

قَلْب heart

132× in the Quran · Noun page →

Ar-Rāġib reports that the heart is said to be so named for its frequent turning (taqallub) and that it expresses what belongs to it: spirit, knowledge, courage. In the Quran it is the seat of understanding, faith and remembrance of Allah.

فُؤَاد heart (aflame with feeling)

16× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the same as قَلْب, but called fuʾād when the sense of glowing and burning is in view (from faʾada — to roast). The heart as the place of strong feeling and inner experience; the Quran also names the fuʾād beside hearing and sight, as an organ of perception (17:36).

صَدْر breast, chest

44× in the Quran · Noun page →

The breast, which holds the heart (22:46). It has been said that when the heart is mentioned, intellect and knowledge are meant; when the breast is mentioned, these and the other inner forces too — desire, passion, anger. The word for expansion and constriction, and for what a person hides within.
