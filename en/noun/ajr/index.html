What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَجْرُ

    the reward (nagrada) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَجْرَ

    the reward (nagradu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَجْرِ

    from the reward (od nagrade) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْأَجْرِ

    in the reward (u nagradi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قِيمَةُ الْأَجْرِ

    the value of the reward (vrijednost nagrade) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَجْرِىَ from the Quran

    my reward (moja nagrada) · Bosnian nominative
