Town and city

قَرْيَة · مَدِينَة

What they share

Both words mean a settlement where people live; the Quran sometimes calls the same place by both names.

How to tell them apart

Any settlement, a place where people gather — and its people → قَرْيَة; a city, often a particular city (Medina, Pharaoh's city) → مَدِينَة.

قَرْيَة town, settlement

57× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the place where people gather — and the people themselves. It does not mean only a village: in the Quran Mecca too is called قَرْيَة (47:13). Often said of peoples and their towns that were destroyed (our observation).

مَدِينَة city

17× in the Quran · Noun page →

A city; in the Quran often a particular city: the Prophet's Medina, Pharaoh's city, the city where Moses found two men fighting.
