Nuances

Dawn, morning and forenoon

فَجْر · صُبْح · ضُحًى

What they share

All three words name the beginning of the day, but each a different moment in it.

How to tell them apart

Dawn — the light that ‘breaks through’ the night → فَجْر; morning, when day has broken and grown light → صُبْح; forenoon, when the sun has risen and its light spreads → ضُحًى.

فَجْر dawn

6× in the Quran · Noun page →

Dawn, the first light that breaks the dark of night (from the root ‘to split, to burst forth’). The fast and the dawn prayer begin with it.

صُبْح morning, daybreak

5× in the Quran · Noun page →

Morning, when day has broken. The Quran gives it pictures: when it ‘brightens’, when it ‘breathes’; and morning as the hour of punishment.

ضُحًى forenoon, morning brightness

6× in the Quran · Noun page →

The time when the sun has risen and its light spreads; the Quran swears by it.
