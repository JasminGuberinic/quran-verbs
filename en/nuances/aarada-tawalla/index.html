Turning away

أَعْرَضَ · تَوَلَّى

What they share

Both words mean turning away from something. According to ar-Rāġib, أَعْرَضَ is turning away showing one's side, while تَوَلَّى with ‘from’ (عَن) is turning away and leaving — with the body or by not obeying.

How to tell them apart

To turn aside, to pay no heed — even when that is praised (from the ignorant) → أَعْرَضَ; to turn one's back, to leave; and without ‘from’: to take someone as ally → تَوَلَّى.

أَعْرَضَ to turn away, to disregard

32× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, turning away showing one's side (عُرْض) — paying no heed. It can be blamed (turning from Allah's reminder), but also commanded: the Prophet is told to turn away from the ignorant.

تَوَلَّى to turn away; to take as ally

78× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, with ‘from’ (said or implied) it means turning away and leaving — with the body or by not listening and not obeying. Without ‘from’ it means drawing near: turning one's face towards something (2:144) or taking someone as ally and friend.

Sources
