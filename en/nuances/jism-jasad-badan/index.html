The body

جِسْم · جَسَد · بَدَن

What they share

All three words mean body. According to ar-Rāġib, ǧasad is like ǧism, but narrower. In the Quran (our observation) each stands in a different picture.

How to tell them apart

The body as build and strength, what is seen and admired → جِسْم; the bare bodily form — the lifeless figure of the golden calf, a body that needs food → جَسَد; the body, the bare frame — Pharaoh's body saved as a lesson → بَدَن.

جِسْم body, physique

2× in the Quran · Noun page →

The body as build and strength. Twice in the Quran: Saul's bodily strength, and the hypocrites' build that pleases the eye.

جَسَد body, lifeless form

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

The bare bodily form: in 7:148 the lifeless figure of the golden calf that only lowed; in 21:8 the body — the prophets were not bodies that need no food.

بَدَن body

1× in the Quran

The body, the trunk; this noun once in the Quran — Pharaoh's body saved from the sea as a lesson to those after him (بُدْن, sacrificial camels, 22:36, is another word).
