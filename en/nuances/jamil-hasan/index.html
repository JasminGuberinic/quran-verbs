Beautiful and good

جَمِيل · حَسَن

What they share

Both words mean something fine and desirable. They differ in measure and in what they describe: abundant beauty, in the Quran always the beauty of a way of acting, or good in the widest sense — what reason, the soul or the eye find fine.

How to tell them apart

Abundant beauty; the adjective in the Quran always of a way of acting: beautiful patience, beautiful forbearance, a beautiful parting → جَمِيل; good, fine in any respect — a goodly loan, good provision, a good promise → حَسَن.

جَمِيل beautiful, comely

7× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ǧamāl is abundant beauty (al-ḥusn al-kaṯīr), whether the beauty in a person himself — in his soul, body or deed — or the beauty that reaches others from him. The adjective jamīl comes in the Quran in 7 places, and always describes a way of acting: patience, forbearance, parting.

حَسَن good, goodly

21× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ḥusn is everything that delights and is desired, whether fine to reason, to desire or to the senses. It is said of things and of events; in people's speech mostly of what is fine to the eye, but in the Quran, he says, mostly of what is fine to insight. So too in Quranic usage: a goodly loan, good provision, a good promise.
