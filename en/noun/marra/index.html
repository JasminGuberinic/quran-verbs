What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَرَّةُ

    the time (put) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَرَّةَ

    the time (put) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَرَّةِ

    in the time (u putu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمَرَّةِ

    from the time (od puta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نِهَايَةُ الْمَرَّةِ

    the end of the time (kraj puta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَرَّتُهُ

    his/its time (njegov put) · Bosnian nominative
