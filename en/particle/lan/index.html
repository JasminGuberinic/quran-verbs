Particles · negation

لَنْwill not

106× in the Quran

What it does

It negates the future and puts the present-tense verb in the subjunctive: لَن تَنَالُوا ‘you will not attain’.

Uses

‘will not’: negating the future

negation · corpus: 106×

Ibn Hišām says that لَنْ by itself does not mean ‘never’, against az-Zamaḫšarī; ‘for ever’ comes from the rest of the ayah when it is there.

The verb after it is subjunctive.

Sources
