Particles · vocative

ٱللَّهُمَّO Allah!

5× in the Quran

What it does

Calling on Allah: ٱللَّهُمَّ is ‘يَا ٱللَّهُ’, with ـمَّ at the end in place of يَا. So Sībawayh explains it: ‘they dropped يَا and added the mīm in its place’, and al-Ḫalīl: the mīm here is at the end of the word what يَا is at its beginning. That is why يَا and ـمَّ are normally not put together.

Uses

‘O Allah!’ in a supplication

vocative · corpus: 5×

When a description of Allah in the accusative follows ٱللَّهُمَّ (مَٰلِكَ ٱلْمُلْكِ، فَاطِرَ), Sībawayh, following al-Ḫalīl, reads it as a second call with يَا understood, not as an adjective, since ٱللَّهُمَّ takes no adjective.
