What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلصَّلَوٰةُ

    the prayer (namaz) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ

    the prayer (namaz) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ

    on the prayer (na namazu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ

    with the prayer (namazom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَقْتُ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ

    the time of the prayer (vrijeme namaza) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    صَلَاتُهُمْ from the Quran

    their prayer (njihov namaz) · Bosnian nominative
