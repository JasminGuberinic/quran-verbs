What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعامِلُ

    the worker (radnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعامِلَ

    the worker (radnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعامِلِ

    in the worker (u radniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعامِلِ

    from the worker (od radnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أُجْرَةُ الْعامِلِ

    the wage of the worker (plata radnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَٰمِلُهُ

    his/its worker (njegov radnik) · Bosnian nominative
