Nuances

Eye and sight

عَيْن · بَصَر

What they share

Both words concern seeing; they differ in whether they name the organ or the faculty.

How to tell them apart

The eye as an organ, also in idioms (the eye's comfort), and a spring of water → عَيْن; sight — the faculty of seeing, and the organ that sees — often beside hearing (السَّمْعُ وَالْبَصَرُ) → بَصَر.

عَيْن eye; spring

57× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the organ of sight; it is also said of a spring of water. In the Quran also in idioms such as “that her eyes might be refreshed” (20:40).

بَصَر sight, vision

48× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, said of the organ that sees and of the faculty of sight in it; the heart's perception is called بَصِيرَة. In the Quran it often stands with hearing and the heart as a means of knowing.
