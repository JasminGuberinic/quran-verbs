What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأُفُقُ

    the horizon (obzor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأُفُقَ

    the horizon (obzor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْأُفُقِ

    with the horizon (obzorom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْأُفُقِ

    in the horizon (na obzoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلْأُفُقِ

    the color of the horizon (boja obzora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أُفُقُهُ

    his/its horizon (njegov obzor) · Bosnian nominative
