What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السُّنْبُلَةُ

    the ear (klas) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السُّنْبُلَةَ

    the ear (klas) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السُّنْبُلَةِ

    in the ear (u klasu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السُّنْبُلَةِ

    from the ear (iz klasa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ السُّنْبُلَةِ

    the length of the ear (dužina klasa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سُنْبُلَتُهُ

    his/its ear (njegov klas) · Bosnian nominative
