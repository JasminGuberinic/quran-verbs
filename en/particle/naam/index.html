نَعَمْyes

4× in the Quran

What it does

A word of answer confirming what was said or asked: to ‘did…?’ it answers ‘yes’. To a negative question it would confirm the negation; that is why بَلَى is said there.

Uses

‘Yes’

answer · corpus: 4×

It confirms the question as a whole.
