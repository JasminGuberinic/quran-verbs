What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السِّدْرُ

    the lote trees (lotosi) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السِّدْرَ

    the lote trees (lotose) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السِّدْرِ

    from the lote trees (od lotosa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السِّدْرِ

    in the lote trees (u lotosima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ظِلُّ السِّدْرِ

    the shade of the lote trees (hlad lotosa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سِدْرُهُ

    his/its lote trees (njegovi lotosi) · Bosnian nominative
