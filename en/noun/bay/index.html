What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْبَيْعُ

    the sale (prodaja) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَيْعَ

    the sale (prodaju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَيْعِ

    in the sale (u prodaji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَيْعِ

    from the sale (od prodaje) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عَقْدُ الْبَيْعِ

    the contract of the sale (ugovor prodaje) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَيْعُهُ

    his/its sale (njegova prodaja) · Bosnian nominative
