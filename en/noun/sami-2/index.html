What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّمِيُّ

    the namesake (imenjak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّمِيَّ

    the namesake (imenjaka) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّمِيِّ

    in the namesake (u imenjaku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّمِيِّ

    from the namesake (od imenjaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَيْتُ السَّمِيِّ

    the house of the namesake (kuća imenjaka) · Bosnian genitive
