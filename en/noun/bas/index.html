What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَأْسُ

    the might (sila) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَأْسَ

    the might (silu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْبَأْسِ

    from the might (od sile) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْبَأْسِ

    in the might (u sili) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    شِدَّةُ ٱلْبَأْسِ

    the intensity of the might (žestina sile) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَأْسُهُ

    his/its might (njegova sila) · Bosnian nominative
