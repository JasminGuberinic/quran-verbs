Vision and dream

رُؤْيَا · حُلُم

What they share

Both words speak of what is seen in sleep. In the Quran رُؤْيَا is always a true dream that comes to pass, while as ‘dreams’ أَحْلَام comes only in the phrase أَضْغَاثُ أَحْلَامٍ, ‘confused dreams’ (12:44, 21:5).

How to tell them apart

What is seen in sleep and has a meaning — in the Quran always true → رُؤْيَا; dreams (only in ‘confused dreams’), and the same word also means maturity and, by extension, minds → حُلُم.

رُؤْيَا dream, vision

7× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what is seen in sleep; he cites the report that nothing of the heralds of prophethood remains but the dream (ruʾyā). In the Quran every رُؤْيَا comes to pass: Joseph's, the king's, Abraham's, the Prophet's.

حُلُم dreams; maturity; minds

6× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, from a root meaning self-restraint from anger: ‘minds’ (52:32), since forbearance springs from reason, and ‘maturity’ (24:59), when one becomes worthy of forbearance; the same root gives ‘to dream’. As dreams it comes in the Quran only in ‘confused dreams’, said by those who dismiss a vision (12:44) or the revelation (21:5).
