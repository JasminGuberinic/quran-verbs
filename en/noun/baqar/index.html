What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَقَرُ

    the cows (krave) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَقَرَ

    the cows (krave) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْبَقَرِ

    from the cows (od krava) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْبَقَرِ

    in the cows (u kravama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    حَلِيبُ ٱلْبَقَرِ

    the milk of the cows (mlijeko krava) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَقَرُهُ

    his/its cows (njegove krave) · Bosnian nominative
