Nuances

Concealing, hiding, keeping secret

كَتَمَ · أَخْفَى · أَسَرَّ

What they share

All three words speak of not showing something. According to ar-Rāġib, أَخْفَى is the opposite of showing and declaring, أَسَرَّ the opposite of doing openly, and كَتَمَ is concealing speech.

How to tell them apart

To withhold what one knows and should tell (testimony, the truth) → كَتَمَ; to hide anything, the opposite of showing → أَخْفَى; to keep secret, or to confide to someone while hiding it from others → أَسَرَّ.

كَتَمَ to conceal, to withhold

21× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, concealing speech. In the Quran it is most often withholding the truth, a testimony, or knowledge owed to others, and then it is blamed as a sin (2:283); but it can also be hiding one's own faith (40:28).

أَخْفَى to hide

18× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, to put a thing under cover; it is the opposite of showing (أَبْدَى) and declaring (أَعْلَنَ). A general word: one hides a good deed, a feeling, a thought.

أَسَرَّ to keep secret; to confide

18× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the opposite of doing openly; and to confide a secret to someone is to reveal it to him while hiding it from others. So the word goes with what is shared in private, with one person or a few (71:9).
