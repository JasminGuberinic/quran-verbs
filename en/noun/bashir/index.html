What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْبَشِيرُ

    the herald (vjesnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَشِيرَ

    the herald (vjesnika) · Bosnian accusative

    مِنَ الْبَشِيرِ

    from the herald (od vjesnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    in the herald (u vjesniku) · Bosnian locative

    his/its herald (njegov vjesnik) · Bosnian nominative
