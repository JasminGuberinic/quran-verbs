Particles · time adverb

إِذْwhen (in the past)

309× in the Quran

What it does

An adverb of past time: ‘when’. A whole sentence (verbal or nominal) follows it and stays as it is. At the head of a story (وَإِذْ قَالَ…) commentators usually explain it with an understood ‘remember (when)…’. In يَوْمَئِذٍ and حِينَئِذٍ the tanwīn stands in for a sentence left out.

Uses

  1. ‘When…’: telling of the past
  2. يَوْمَئِذٍ: ‘that Day’

‘When…’: telling of the past

time adverb

It most often introduces an event of the past, often at the start of a prophet's story.

يَوْمَئِذٍ: ‘that Day’

time adverb

In يَوْمَئِذٍ (‘the day when…’) the tanwīn on إِذٍ stands in for the sentence said before (tanwīn al-ʿiwaḍ): ‘that Day’, most often of the Day of Judgement.
