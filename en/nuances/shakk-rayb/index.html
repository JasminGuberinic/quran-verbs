Nuances

Doubt

شَكّ · رَيْب

What they share

Both words mean doubt; they tend to differ in whether it is a bare wavering between two possibilities or doubt that brings unease and suspicion.

How to tell them apart

Wavering between two possibilities with no preponderance → شَكّ; doubt with unease and suspicion — ‘no doubt about it’ → رَيْب.

شَكّ doubt

15× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the state in which two opposites are equal in the mind and neither tips the scale. Often described as a doubt ‘that disquiets’ (مُرِيب).

رَيْب doubt

18× in the Quran · Noun page →

Doubt joined with unease and suspicion; so tafsir often explains it at the opening of al-Baqarah. The Quran uses it to say there is ‘no doubt’ about the Book and the Last Day.

Sources

  • ar-Rāġib al-Iṣfahānī, Mufradāt alfāẓ al-Qurʾān — entries شكك
  • Tafsir on 2:2 — the sense of rayb
