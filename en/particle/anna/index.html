أَنَّthat

362× in the Quran

What it does

A sister of إِنَّ: the subject after it is accusative. But while إِنَّ starts a sentence, أَنَّ turns it into part of a larger one (‘know that…’), usually after a verb of knowing.

Uses

‘that’

particle of the إِنَّ family · corpus: 362×

It introduces what is known or told; its subject is accusative.

The word after it is accusative.
