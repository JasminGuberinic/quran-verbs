Particles · question word

هَلْis…?, has…?

93× in the Quran

What it does

A word asking a yes-or-no question. Unlike the hamza, according to Ibn Hišām it does not ask ‘this or that’ and does not stand before a negation. It changes no case or mood.

Uses

  1. ‘Has…?’, ‘Is…?’
  2. A question meaning a denial: ‘is … anything but…?’
  3. Meaning قَدْ (76:1)

‘Has…?’, ‘Is…?’

question word

It asks about the whole sentence. In “Hath there come unto thee the history…” (79:15) the question draws attention to the story that follows.

A question meaning a denial: ‘is … anything but…?’

question word

With إِلَّا the question means a negation: ‘there is nothing but…’.

Meaning قَدْ (76:1)

question word

Ibn Hišām reports that some, Ibn ʿAbbās and al-Kisāʾī among them, explained هَلْ in 76:1 as قَدْ: ‘there has indeed come…’, rather than as a question.
