What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحِينُ

    the time (vrijeme) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحِينَ

    the time (vrijeme) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى الْحِينِ

    to the time (do vremena) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْحِينِ

    on the time (na vremenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْحِينِ

    the length of the time (dužina vremena) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِينُهُ

    his/its time (njegovo vrijeme) · Bosnian nominative
