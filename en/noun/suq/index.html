What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السُّوقُ

    the market (pijaca) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السُّوقَ

    the market (pijacu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسُّوقِ

    in the market (na pijaci) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلسُّوقِ

    with the market (pijacom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَسَطُ ٱلسُّوقِ

    the middle of the market (sredina pijace) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سُوقُهُ

    his/its market (njegova pijaca) · Bosnian nominative
