What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْعِلْمُ

    the knowledge (znanje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْعِلْمَ

    the knowledge (znanje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْعِلْمِ

    from the knowledge (od znanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلْعِلْمِ

    on the knowledge (na znanju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَالِبُ ٱلْعِلْمِ

    the student of the knowledge (učenik znanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عِلْمُهَا from the Quran

    her/its knowledge (njeno znanje) · Bosnian nominative
