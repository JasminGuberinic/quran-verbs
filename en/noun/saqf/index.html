What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسَّقْفُ

    the roof (krov) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّقْفَ

    the roof (krov) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسَّقْفِ

    in the roof (na krovu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلسَّقْفِ

    from the roof (iz krova) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلسَّقْفِ

    the color of the roof (boja krova) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَقْفُهُ

    his/its roof (njegov krov) · Bosnian nominative
