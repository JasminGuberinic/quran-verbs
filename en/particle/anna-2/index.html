أَنَّىhow?; from where?

28× in the Quran

What it does

A question word with more than one meaning: ‘how?’ and ‘from where?’ (Sībawayh glosses it with كَيْفَ and أَيْنَ). It changes no case or mood. It often expresses wonder or denial.

Uses

‘How?’, ‘From where?’

question word · corpus: 28×

It asks about the way or the origin; sometimes it wonders and rejects: ‘how, then, are you deluded?’

Sources

  • Ibn Hišām al-Anṣārī, Muġnī al-labīb ʿan kutub al-aʿārīb
