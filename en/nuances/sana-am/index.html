Year

سَنَة · عَام

What they share

Both words mean ‘year’; ar-Rāġib records a difference in what kind of year each most often names, while the Quran uses both neutrally as well.

How to tell them apart

According to ar-Rāġib, a year that is often hard and barren → سَنَة; a year often of plenty → عَام. A tendency, not a rule: in the Quran both words are often neutral (12:47, 9:28).

سَنَة year

19× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, sana is often used of a year of hardship or drought; hence drought itself is called ‘a year’. In the Quran it is nonetheless mostly a neutral measure of time.

عَام year

9× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a year, often one of ease and plenty, like the year in which people are given rain (12:49). In the Quran it also comes neutrally (9:28).
