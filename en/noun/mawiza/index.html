What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَوْعِظَةُ

    the admonition (pouka) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَوْعِظَةَ

    the admonition (pouku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمَوْعِظَةِ

    in the admonition (u pouci) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمَوْعِظَةِ

    from the admonition (od pouke) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَثَرُ ٱلْمَوْعِظَةِ

    the effect of the admonition (utjecaj pouke) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَوْعِظَتُهُ

    his/its admonition (njegova pouka) · Bosnian nominative
