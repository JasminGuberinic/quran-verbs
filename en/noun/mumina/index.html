What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    مُؤْمِنَةٌ

    a believer (vjernica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    مُؤْمِنَةً

    a believer (vjernicu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنْ مُؤْمِنَةٍ

    from a believer (od vjernice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي مُؤْمِنَةٍ

    in a believer (u vjernici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَلْبُ مُؤْمِنَةٍ

    the heart of a believer (srce vjernice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مُؤْمِنَتُهُ

    his/its believer (njegova vjernica) · Bosnian nominative
