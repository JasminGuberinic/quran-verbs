What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلنَّارُ

    the fire (vatra) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلنَّارَ

    the fire (vatru) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلنَّارِ

    in the fire (u vatri) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ

    from the fire (iz vatre) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    حَرُّ ٱلنَّارِ

    the heat of the fire (vrelina vatre) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَارُهُ

    his/its fire (njegova vatra) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

    The fire flared up.

    أَشْعَلَ الْكَشَّافُ النَّارَ
