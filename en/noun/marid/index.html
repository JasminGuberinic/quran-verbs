What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَرِيضُ

    the sick person (bolesnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَرِيضَ

    the sick person (bolesnika) · Bosnian accusative

    عَلَى ٱلْمَرِيضِ

    on the sick person (na bolesniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مَرِيضُهُ

    his/its sick person (njegov bolesnik) · Bosnian nominative
