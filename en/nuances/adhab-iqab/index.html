Nuances

Punishment

عَذَاب · عِقَاب

What they share

Both words are rendered ‘punishment’; they differ in what is in view: the pain, or the consequence of the deed.

How to tell them apart

Severe pain, torment — even torment people inflict → عَذَاب; penalty that follows the deed as its consequence → عِقَاب.

عَذَاب punishment, torment

322× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ‘severe pain’. The Quran's most frequent word for punishment; it is also said of torment people inflict, such as Pharaoh's tormenting of the Children of Israel.

عِقَاب penalty

20× in the Quran · Noun page →

From the root ʿ-q-b — what comes after (ʿaqib: heel; ʿāqiba: outcome): punishment seen as the consequence that follows a deed. The noun ʿiqāb is always Allah's in the Quran (the verb ʿāqaba is said of people too), most often in شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ.

Sources
