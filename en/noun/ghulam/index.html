What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْغُلَٰمُ

    the boy (dječak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْغُلامَ

    the boy (dječaka) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْغُلامِ

    in the boy (u dječaku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْغُلامِ

    from the boy (od dječaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أُمُّ الْغُلامِ

    the mother of the boy (majka dječaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    غُلَٰمُهُ

    his/its boy (njegov dječak) · Bosnian nominative
