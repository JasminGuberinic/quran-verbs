What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النَّعِيمُ

    the comfort (blagostanje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النَّعِيمَ

    the comfort (blagostanje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلنَّعِيمِ

    in the comfort (u blagostanju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    from the comfort (od blagostanja) · Bosnian genitive

    نَعِيمُهُ
