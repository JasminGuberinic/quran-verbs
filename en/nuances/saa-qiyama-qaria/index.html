Nuances

Names of the Day: the Hour, the Rising, the Striking, the Sure Reality

سَاعَة · قِيَامَة · قَارِعَة · حَاقَّة

What they share

All four words name the Day of Judgement. They differ in the side they bring out: the suddenness and shortness of a moment, everyone rising at once, a blow that shakes, and the reality in which reward and punishment become due.

How to tell them apart

The Hour — a part of time, and a name of the Day; it comes ‘suddenly’ → سَاعَة; the Rising — always in ‘the Day of Rising’ (yawm al-qiyāma); ar-Rāġib: a rising all at once → قِيَامَة; the Striking — what strikes one thing on another; 69:4 and 101:1–3 → قَارِعَة; the Sure Reality — only 69:1–3; ar-Rāġib: the day on which recompense becomes due → حَاقَّة.

سَاعَة the Hour

48× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, sāʿa is a part of time, and the Day of Judgement is expressed by it — for the swiftness of Allah's reckoning, or because life will seem to people as short as an hour. In 30:55 the word is there twice: first the Day, then ‘a short time’. In the Quran the Hour comes suddenly, and its knowledge is with Allah.

قِيَامَة the Rising

70× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, qiyāma expresses ‘the rising of the Hour’, and its origin is what a man does when he stands up all at once; the ending -a points to its happening all at once. In the Quran it is in all 70 places in the phrase yawm al-qiyāma, ‘the Day of Rising’.

قَارِعَة the Striking Calamity

5× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, qarʿ is striking one thing on another, and he cites 69:4 and 101:1. Mufassirun often explain the name as a blow that shakes the hearts. In 13:31 the same word means a calamity that strikes the disbelievers in this world.

حَاقَّة the Sure Reality

3× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, ‘al-ḥāqqa’ in 69:1 points to the Day of Judgement, because on it reward and punishment come true and become due (yaḥiqqu). In the Quran it is only at the opening of the surah al-Ḥāqqa (69:1–3).
