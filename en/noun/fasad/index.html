What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْفَسَادُ

    the corruption (korupcija) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْفَسَادَ

    the corruption (korupciju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْفَسَادِ

    in the corruption (u korupciji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْفَسَادِ

    from the corruption (od korupcije) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ ٱلْفَسَادِ

    the cause of the corruption (uzrok korupcije) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَسَادُهُ

    his/its corruption (njegova korupcija) · Bosnian nominative
