What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْفَتْحُ

    the opening (otvaranje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفَتْحَ

    the opening (otvaranje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْفَتْحِ

    in the opening (u otvaranju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْفَتْحِ

    from the opening (od otvaranja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    يَوْمُ ٱلْفَتْحِ

    the day of the opening (dan otvaranja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَتْحُهُ

    his/its opening (njegovo otvaranje) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    فَتْحُ الْبَابِ سَهْلٌ

    Opening the door is easy.

  • object (accusative)

    انْتَظَرَ النَّاسُ فَتْحَ الْمَتْجَرِ

    The people waited for the opening of the store.

  • after a preposition (genitive)

    فَرِحَ التُّجَّارُ بِفَتْحِ السُّوقِ

    The merchants were glad about the opening of the market.

  • possessor (genitive)

    وَقْتُ فَتْحِ الْمَكْتَبَةِ مُبَكِّرٌ

    The opening time of the library is early.
