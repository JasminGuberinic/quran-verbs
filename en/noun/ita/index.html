What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْإِيتَاءُ

    the giving (davanje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْإِيتَاءَ

    the giving (davanje) · Bosnian accusative

    فِي الْإِيتَاءِ

    in the giving (u davanju) · Bosnian locative
