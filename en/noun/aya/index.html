What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْآيَةُ

    the sign (znak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْءَايَةَ

    the sign (znak) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْآيَةِ

    from the sign (od znaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْآيَةِ

    in the sign (u znaku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مَعْنَى الْآيَةِ

    the meaning of the sign (značenje znaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ءَايَٰتُنَا from the Quran

    our signs (naši znaci) · Bosnian nominative
