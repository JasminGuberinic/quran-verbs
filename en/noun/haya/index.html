What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْحَيَوٰةُ

    the life (život) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ

    the life (život) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْحَيَوٰةِ

    in the life (u životu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْحَيَوٰةِ

    with the life (životom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَعْمُ ٱلْحَيَوٰةِ

    the taste of the life (okus života) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حَيَاتُنَا from the Quran

    our life (naš život) · Bosnian nominative
