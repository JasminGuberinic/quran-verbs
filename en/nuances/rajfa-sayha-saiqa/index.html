Quake, shriek and thunderbolt

رَجْفَة · صَيْحَة · صَاعِقَة

What they share

All three words name the punishment that seized earlier peoples. According to ar-Rāġib, رَجْفَة is violent shaking, صَيْحَة is raising the voice, and صَاعِقَة is a mighty sound from the sky that brings fire, punishment or death. One people, Thamud, is described with each of the three. Mufassirun often explain that this is one punishment described from different sides: the dreadful sound from above and the quaking of the ground.

How to tell them apart

Quake — the ground shakes violently (Thamud, Midian) → رَجْفَة; shriek — a sound that seizes with terror; also the blast of the Horn → صَيْحَة; thunderbolt — a mighty crash from the sky, the bolt that strikes → صَاعِقَة.

رَجْفَة quake, tremor

4× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, violent shaking, said of the earth and the sea. In the Quran (4 times) the punishment that seized Thamud and Midian, and the quake that seized the seventy chosen from Moses' people.

صَيْحَة shriek, blast

13× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, raising the voice; the root is the cracking of sound, and since a shout terrifies, it is said of terror itself (15:73). In the Quran the punishment of Thamud, Midian and Lot's people, and also the blast of the Horn at the resurrection (50:42).

صَاعِقَة thunderbolt

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a mighty sound from the sky; from it come fire, punishment or death — these are its effects, while it is itself one thing. Some linguists, he reports, distinguished three senses: death, punishment and fire. In the Quran the punishment of Thamud and ʿĀd, and also the bolts in a storm (13:13).
