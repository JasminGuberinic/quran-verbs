What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السُّلَّمُ

    the ladder (ljestve) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السُّلَّمَ

    the ladder (ljestve) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السُّلَّمِ

    in the ladder (na ljestvama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السُّلَّمِ

    from the ladder (iz ljestava) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ السُّلَّمِ

    the length of the ladder (dužina ljestava) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سُلَّمُهُ

    his/its ladder (njegove ljestve) · Bosnian nominative
