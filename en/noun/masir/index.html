What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْمَصِيرُ

    the fate (sudbina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَصِيرَ

    the fate (sudbinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَصِيرِ

    in the fate (u sudbini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمَصِيرِ

    from the fate (iz sudbine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سُؤَالُ الْمَصِيرِ

    the question of the fate (pitanje sudbine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَصِيرُهُ

    his/its fate (njegova sudbina) · Bosnian nominative
