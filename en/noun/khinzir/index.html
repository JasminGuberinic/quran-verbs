What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخِنْزِيرُ

    the pig (svinja) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخِنْزِيرَ

    the pig (svinju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْخِنزِيرِ

    in the pig (u svinji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْخِنزِيرِ

    from the pig (od svinje) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَحْمُ ٱلْخِنزِيرِ

    the meat of the pig (meso svinje) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خِنْزِيرُهُ

    his/its pig (njegova svinja) · Bosnian nominative
