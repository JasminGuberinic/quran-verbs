What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلصُّبْحُ

    the morning (jutro) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الصُّبْحَ

    the morning (jutro) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلصُّبْحِ

    in the morning (u jutru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلصُّبْحِ

    from the morning (od jutra) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ضَوْءُ ٱلصُّبْحِ

    the light of the morning (svjetlost jutra) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    صُبْحُهُ

    his/its morning (njegovo jutro) · Bosnian nominative
