What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْيَتِيمُ

    the orphan (jetim) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْيَتِيمَ

    the orphan (jetima) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْيَتِيمِ

    in the orphan (u jetimu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    from the orphan (od jetima) · Bosnian genitive

    يَتِيمُهُ

    his/its orphan (njegov jetim) · Bosnian nominative
