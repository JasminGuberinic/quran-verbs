Cleansing and purifying

طَهَّرَ · زَكَّى

What they share

Both words mean to purify. According to ar-Rāġib, purity (طَهَارَة) is of two kinds — of the body and of the soul — while the root of زَكَاة is growth that comes from Allah's blessing: تَزْكِيَة is a purifying that makes a person grow in good.

How to tell them apart

To cleanse body, place or soul of impurity → طَهَّرَ; to purify the soul so that it grows in good; also to call oneself pure (blamed) → زَكَّى.

طَهَّرَ to cleanse

9× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, purity is of the body and of the soul, and most ayat are taken to mean both. In the Quran water cleanses the body (8:11), the Kaʿba is cleansed (2:125), and the soul — Allah purified Mary (3:42).

زَكَّى to purify (cause to grow in good)

12× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a purifying of the soul that brings growth and blessing. It is ascribed to man who strives for it (91:9), to Allah who truly does it (4:49), and to the Prophet as the means (62:2). Purifying oneself by deeds is praised; praising oneself in words is blamed (53:32).
