Pronouns · demonstrative

تِلْكَthat (f.)

43× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The demonstrative of the far, feminine singular: تِـ + the lām of distance + the كَ of address. Like هَٰذِهِ, it is also used for a plural of things: تِلْكَ ءَايَٰتُ – ‘those are the verses’.

Uses

‘That …’, ‘those are …’

demonstrative · corpus: 43×

At the start of some surahs it points to the verses of the Book (‘those / these are’); with persons and things in the feminine it means ‘that’.
