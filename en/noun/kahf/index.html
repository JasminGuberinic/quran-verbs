What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكَهْفُ

    the cave (pećina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكَهْفَ

    the cave (pećinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْكَهْفِ

    to the cave (do pećine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْكَهْفِ

    in the cave (u pećini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مَدْخَلُ ٱلْكَهْفِ

    the entrance of the cave (ulaz pećine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَهْفُهُ

    his/its cave (njegova pećina) · Bosnian nominative
