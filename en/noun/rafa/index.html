What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الرَّأْفَةُ

    the compassion (samilost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الرَّأْفَةَ

    the compassion (samilost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الرَّأْفَةِ

    in the compassion (u samilosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الرَّأْفَةِ

    from the compassion (od samilosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عَلَامَةُ الرَّأْفَةِ

    the sign of the compassion (znak samilosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    رَأْفَتُهُ

    his/its compassion (njegova samilost) · Bosnian nominative
