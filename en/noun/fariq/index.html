What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْفَرِيقُ

    the team (tim) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفَرِيقَ

    the team (tim) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْفَرِيقِ

    in the team (u timu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْفَرِيقِ

    from the team (od tima) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَائِدُ الْفَرِيقِ

    the leader of the team (vođa tima) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَرِيقُهُ

    his/its team (njegov tim) · Bosnian nominative
