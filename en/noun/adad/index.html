What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَدَدُ

    the number (broj) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعَدَدَ

    the number (broj) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعَدَدِ

    in the number (u broju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعَدَدِ

    from the number (od broja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    زِيَادَةُ الْعَدَدِ

    the increase of the number (povećanje broja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَدَدُهُ

    his/its number (njegov broj) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    زَادَ عَدَدُ الطُّلَّابِ

    The number of students increased.

  • object (accusative)

    كَتَبَ الطِّفْلُ الْعَدَدَ

    The child wrote the number.

  • after a preposition (genitive)

    بَدَأَتِ الْبِنْتُ بِالْعَدَدِ الْأَوَّلِ

    The girl started with the first number.

  • possessor (genitive)

    قِيمَةُ الْعَدَدِ كَبِيرَةٌ

    The value of the number is large.
