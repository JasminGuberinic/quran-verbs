Nuances

Joy

فَرِحَ · سُرُور · مَسْرُور

What they share

Words of joy; they differ in whether the joy shows and in what people rejoice at.

How to tell them apart

Joy at something, often fleeting — in the Quran mostly blamed → فَرِحَ; gladness, at root (ar-Rāġib) joy concealed in the heart → سُرُور, مَسْرُور.

فَرِحَ to rejoice

16× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the expansion of the breast at an immediate pleasure, mostly bodily; hence in the Quran it is mostly blamed, and he names only 10:58 and 30:4 as allowing it (3:170 is another approving ayah).

سُرُور joy, gladness

1× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, ‘what is concealed of joy’ — from the root of سِرّ, secret: joy in the heart, not display. It occurs once in the Quran, of the reward of the righteous.

مَسْرُور joyful

2× in the Quran

The passive participle of the same root: gladdened, filled with joy. Said of the joy of the one given his record in his right hand, and also of the denier's careless joy in this world.
