What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الذَّهَبُ

    the gold (zlato) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلذَّهَبَ

    the gold (zlato) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلذَّهَبِ

    from the gold (iz zlata) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلذَّهَبِ

    in the gold (u zlatu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلذَّهَبِ

    the color of the gold (boja zlata) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ذَهَبُهُ

    his/its gold (njegovo zlato) · Bosnian nominative
