أَيْنَwhere?; wherever

17× in the Quran

What it does

It asks about place: ‘where?’. With مَا (أَيْنَمَا) it is the condition ‘wherever’ and puts the verbs in the jussive. It does not change itself.

Uses

  1. ‘Where?’
  2. أَيْنَمَا: ‘wherever’
