What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَبُ

    the father (otac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَبَ

    the father (oca) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى الْأَبِ

    to the father (k ocu) · Bosnian dative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَبِ

    from the father (od oca) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَيْتُ الْأَبِ

    the house of the father (kuća oca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَبِيكُمْ from the Quran

    of your father (vašeg oca) · Bosnian genitive
