What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحُسْبانُ

    the calculation (račun) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحُسْبانَ

    the calculation (račun) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحُسْبانِ

    in the calculation (na računu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحُسْبانِ

    from the calculation (od računa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    دِقَّةُ الْحُسْبانِ

    the correctness of the calculation (tačnost računa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حُسْبانُهُ

    his/its calculation (njegov račun) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    كَانَ الْحُسْبَانُ دَقِيقًا

    The calculation was precise.

  • object (accusative)

    فَحَصَ الْمُعَلِّمُ الْحُسْبَانَ

    The teacher examined the calculation.

  • possessor (genitive)

    دِقَّةُ الْحُسْبَانِ مُهِمَّةٌ

    The precision of the calculation is important.
