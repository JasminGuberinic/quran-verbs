What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْحُوتُ

    the whale (kit) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْحُوتَ

    the whale (kita) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْحُوتِ

    in the whale (u kitu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْحُوتِ

    from the whale (iz kita) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    حَجْمُ ٱلْحُوتِ

    the size of the whale (veličina kita) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِيتَانُهُمْ from the Quran

    their whales (njihovi kitovi) · Bosnian nominative
