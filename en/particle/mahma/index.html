Particles · condition

مَهْمَاwhatever

1× in the Quran

What it does

A conditional word: ‘whatever’. Ibn Hišām shows it is a noun, since a pronoun returns to it in 7:132 (بِهِۦ), and a simple word, not a compound (against those who derive it from مَهْ + مَا or مَا + مَا). It makes the verb of the condition and of the answer jussive.

Uses

‘Whatever…’

condition · corpus: 1×

Ibn Hišām says that in 7:132 it means ‘whatever’ of a thing (not of time), and that مِنْ ءَايَةٍ explains it – ‘whatever sign’.

The verb after it is jussive.
