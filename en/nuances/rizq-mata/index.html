Nuances

Provision and enjoyment

رِزْق · مَتَاع

What they share

Both words speak of what a person benefits from in life. They differ in what they stress: one what is given, the other a use that lasts a while.

How to tell them apart

What is given and allotted — in this world and the next, a share, food, and knowledge too → رِزْق; benefit that lasts for a time, passing enjoyment, and also the goods one uses → مَتَاع.

رِزْق provision

55× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a gift that keeps coming — of this world or of the next; then a share, and what is eaten and nourishes. In “spend of that wherewith We have provided you” (2:254) it covers, he says, wealth, standing and knowledge.

مَتَاع enjoyment, goods

35× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a benefit that stretches over time. So this world is مَتَاع — enjoyment for a set span, little beside the Hereafter; and مَتَاع is also the goods one uses, such as baggage and food.

Sources

  • ar-Rāġib al-Iṣfahānī, Mufradāt alfāẓ al-Qurʾān — entries رزق, متع
