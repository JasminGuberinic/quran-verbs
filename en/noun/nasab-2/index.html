What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النَّسَبُ

    the lineage (porijeklo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النَّسَبَ

    the lineage (porijeklo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي النَّسَبِ

    in the lineage (u porijeklu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ النَّسَبِ

    from the lineage (od porijekla) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    شَرَفُ النَّسَبِ

    the honor of the lineage (čast porijekla) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَسَبُهُ

    his/its lineage (njegovo porijeklo) · Bosnian nominative
