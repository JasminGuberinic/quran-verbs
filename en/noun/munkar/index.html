What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمُنْكَرُ

    the wrongdoing (zlo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمُنكَرَ

    the wrongdoing (zlo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمُنكَرِ

    in the wrongdoing (u zlu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمُنكَرِ

    from the wrongdoing (od zla) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    تَرْكُ ٱلْمُنكَرِ

    the giving up of the wrongdoing (ostavljanje zla) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مُنْكَرُهُ

    his/its wrongdoing (njegovo zlo) · Bosnian nominative
