What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلظَّالِمُ

    the wrongdoer (nasilnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الظَّالِمَ

    the wrongdoer (nasilnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلظَّالِمِ

    from the wrongdoer (od nasilnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلظَّالِمِ

    on the wrongdoer (na nasilniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عِقَابُ ٱلظَّالِمِ

    the punishment of the wrongdoer (kazna nasilnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ظَالِمُهُ

    his/its wrongdoer (njegov nasilnik) · Bosnian nominative
