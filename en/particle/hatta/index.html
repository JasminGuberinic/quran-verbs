Particles · preposition · opening a new sentence

حَتَّىuntil; till; up to

142× in the Quran

What it does

It marks a limit: ‘until’, ‘up to’. Before a noun it is a preposition (genitive); before a present-tense verb it puts it in the subjunctive; and it can open a new sentence (most often حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا), when it changes nothing.

Uses

  1. ‘Until’ with a noun
  2. ‘Until…’ with a verb
  3. Opening a new sentence: ‘until, when…’

‘Until’ with a noun

preposition

As a preposition it takes the genitive and marks the end of a time.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘Until…’ with a verb

preposition

A present-tense verb after it is subjunctive. According to Ibn Hišām an understood أَنْ makes it subjunctive and حَتَّى stays a preposition; according to the Kufans حَتَّى itself makes it subjunctive.

The verb after it is subjunctive.

Opening a new sentence: ‘until, when…’

opening a new sentence · corpus: 47×

According to Ibn Hišām, حَتَّى can be a word of beginning (ibtidāʾiyya): a whole sentence follows it, which it does not change, and the sense of a limit remains.

Sources

  • Ibn Hišām al-Anṣārī, Muġnī al-labīb ʿan kutub al-aʿārīb
