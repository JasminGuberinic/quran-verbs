What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السُّلْطانُ

    the authority (vlast) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السُّلْطانَ

    the authority (vlast) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السُّلْطانِ

    from the authority (od vlasti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السُّلْطانِ

    in the authority (u vlasti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ السُّلْطانِ

    the strength of the authority (snaga vlasti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سُلْطَٰنُهُ

    his/its authority (njegova vlast) · Bosnian nominative
