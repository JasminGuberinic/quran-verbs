What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجِذْعُ

    the trunk (deblo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجِذْعَ

    the trunk (deblo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى الْجِذْعِ

    to the trunk (do debla) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْجِذْعِ

    with the trunk (deblom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْجِذْعِ

    the length of the trunk (dužina debla) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جِذْعُهُ

    his/its trunk (njegovo deblo) · Bosnian nominative
