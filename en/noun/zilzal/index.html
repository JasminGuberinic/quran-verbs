What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الزِّلْزالُ

    the earthquake (zemljotres) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الزِّلْزالَ

    the earthquake (zemljotres) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الزِّلْزالِ

    in the earthquake (u zemljotresu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الزِّلْزالِ

    from the earthquake (od zemljotresa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ الزِّلْزالِ

    the strength of the earthquake (snaga zemljotresa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    زِلْزَالَهَا from the Quran

    her/its earthquake (njen zemljotres) · Bosnian accusative
