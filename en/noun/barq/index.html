What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْبَرْقُ

    the lightning (munja) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَرْقَ

    the lightning (munju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَرْقِ

    in the lightning (u munji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَرْقِ

    from the lightning (od munje) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ضَوْءُ الْبَرْقِ

    the light of the lightning (svjetlo munje) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَرْقِهِۦ from the Quran

    of his/its lightning (njegove munje) · Bosnian genitive
