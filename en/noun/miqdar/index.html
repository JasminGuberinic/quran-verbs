What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمِقْدارُ

    the amount (količina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمِقْدارَ

    the amount (količinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمِقْدارِ

    in the amount (u količini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمِقْدارِ

    from the amount (od količine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    زِيَادَةُ الْمِقْدارِ

    the increase of the amount (povećanje količine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مِقْدَارُهُۥٓ from the Quran

    his/its amount (njegova količina) · Bosnian nominative
