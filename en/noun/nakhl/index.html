What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلنَّخْلُ

    the date palms (palme) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلنَّخْلَ

    the date palms (palme) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلنَّخْلِ

    from the date palms (od palmi) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلنَّخْلِ

    with the date palms (palmama) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ثَمَرُ ٱلنَّخْلِ

    the fruit of the date palms (plod palmi) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَخْلُهُ

    his/its date palms (njegove palme) · Bosnian nominative
