What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجانِبُ

    the side (strana) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجانِبَ

    the side (stranu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْجانِبِ

    with the side (stranom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْجانِبِ

    from the side (od strane) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْجانِبِ

    the length of the side (dužina strane) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جانِبُهُ

    his/its side (njegova strana) · Bosnian nominative
