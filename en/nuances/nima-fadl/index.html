Nuances

Favour and bounty

نِعْمَة · فَضْل

What they share

Both words mean the good Allah gives. According to ar-Rāġib, نِعْمَة is the good state a person finds himself in, while فَضْل is what goes beyond what suffices — a gift the giver is not bound to give.

How to tell them apart

The good state, a favour enjoyed and beyond counting → نِعْمَة; the surplus, a gift of bounty beyond what is due, and what is sought by effort → فَضْل.

نِعْمَة favour, blessing

50× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the good state a person is in; as a genus it is said of little and of much. The verb أَنْعَمَ is, he says, to bring good to another, and is said only when the receiver is a rational being.

فَضْل bounty

84× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the surplus over what suffices; every gift the giver is not bound to give is called فَضْل. In “that ye may seek bounty (فَضْل) from your Lord” (17:12) it is, he says, wealth and what is earned.
