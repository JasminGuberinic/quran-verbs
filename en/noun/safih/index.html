What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّفِيهُ

    the fool (bezumnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّفِيهَ

    the fool (bezumnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّفِيهِ

    in the fool (u bezumniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّفِيهِ

    from the fool (od bezumnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلَامُ السَّفِيهِ

    the speech of the fool (govor bezumnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَفِيهُنَا from the Quran

    our fool (naš bezumnik) · Bosnian nominative
