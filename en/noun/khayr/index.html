What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْخَيْرُ

    the good (dobro) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْخَيْرَ

    the good (dobro) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْخَيْرِ

    from the good (od dobra) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْخَيْرِ

    to the good (k dobru) · Bosnian dative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَرِيقُ ٱلْخَيْرِ

    the road of the good (put dobra) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَيْرُهُ

    his/its good (njegovo dobro) · Bosnian nominative
