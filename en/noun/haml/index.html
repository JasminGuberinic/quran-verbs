What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحَمْلُ

    the burden (breme) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحَمْلَ

    the burden (breme) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحَمْلِ

    in the burden (na bremenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحَمْلِ

    from the burden (od bremena) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَزْنُ الْحَمْلِ

    the weight of the burden (težina bremena) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حَمْلُهُ

    his/its burden (njegovo breme) · Bosnian nominative
