Nuances

Tilth and crops

حَرْث · زَرْع

What they share

Both words speak of cultivated land and what grows on it. Ar-Rāġib sets them apart by whose act each is: man's part is to prepare the soil and cast the seed, while making it grow is Allah's act.

How to tell them apart

Casting seed and the prepared, tilled land, and so the yield of one's labour → حَرْث; what sprouts and grows, the crop → زَرْع.

حَرْث tilth, tilled land

13× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, casting seed into the earth and preparing it for the crop; then the tilled land itself, and also what labour yields. In 56:63–64 Allah ascribes the tilling (تَحْرُثُونَ) to people and the growing to Himself.

زَرْع crops

10× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, zarʿ is making to grow, and that in its reality is Allah's act, not man's; it is said of a man only because he does the causes. At root a verbal noun, it is said of what is sown: the crop.
