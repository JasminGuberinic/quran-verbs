What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكَنْزُ

    the treasure (blago) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكَنْزَ

    the treasure (blago) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْكَنْزِ

    from the treasure (iz blaga) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْكَنْزِ

    in the treasure (u blagu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مَكَانُ الْكَنْزِ

    the place of the treasure (mjesto blaga) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَنزَهُمَا from the Quran

    their treasure (njihovo blago) · Bosnian accusative
