What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّعْيُ

    the endeavor (trud) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسَّعْىَ

    the endeavor (trud) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّعْيِ

    in the endeavor (u trudu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّعْيِ

    from the endeavor (od truda) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ثَمَرَةُ السَّعْيِ

    the fruit of the endeavor (plod truda) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَعْيَهَا from the Quran

    her/its endeavor (njen trud) · Bosnian accusative
