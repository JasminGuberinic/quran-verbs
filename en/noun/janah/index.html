What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجَناحُ

    the wing (krilo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجَناحَ

    the wing (krilo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْجَناحِ

    with the wing (krilom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى الْجَناحِ

    to the wing (do krila) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    رِيشُ الْجَناحِ

    the feathers of the wing (perje krila) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جَنَاحَكَ from the Quran

    your wing (tvoje krilo) · Bosnian accusative
