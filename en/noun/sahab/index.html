What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّحابُ

    the clouds (oblaci) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسَّحَابَ

    the clouds (oblake) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسَّحَابِ

    in the clouds (u oblacima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلسَّحَابِ

    from the clouds (od oblaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلسَّحَابِ

    the color of the clouds (boja oblaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَحَابُهُ

    his/its clouds (njegovi oblaci) · Bosnian nominative
