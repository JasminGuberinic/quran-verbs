What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقِسْمَةُ

    the division (podjela) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْقِسْمَةَ

    the division (podjelu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْقِسْمَةِ

    in the division (u podjeli) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْقِسْمَةِ

    from the division (od podjele) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَرِيقَةُ الْقِسْمَةِ

    the method of the division (način podjele) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قِسْمَتُهُ

    his/its division (njegova podjela) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

    The method of the division is clear.
