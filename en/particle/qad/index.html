Particles · particle of certainty

قَدْalready, indeed

406× in the Quran

What it does

It stands only before a verb. With the past tense it confirms that something really happened (often ‘already’). With the present tense it can mean ‘sometimes’, but in the Quran, of Allah's knowledge, it confirms. It does not change the verb.

Uses

  1. With the past: ‘indeed, already’
  2. With the present: confirming

With the past: ‘indeed, already’

particle of certainty

Confirming a completed action (at-taḥqīq), often with لَـ: لَقَدْ.

With the present: confirming

particle of certainty

With Allah's knowledge and seeing قَدْ confirms (at-taḥqīq), it does not lessen; the verb stays indicative.

The verb after it stays indicative.
