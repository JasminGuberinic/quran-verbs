What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَسْكَنُ

    the residence (dom) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَسْكَنَ

    the residence (dom) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَسْكَنِ

    in the residence (u domu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمَسْكَنِ

    from the residence (iz doma) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ الْمَسْكَنِ

    the door of the residence (vrata doma) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَسَٰكِنُهُمْ from the Quran

    their residences (njihovi domovi) · Bosnian nominative
