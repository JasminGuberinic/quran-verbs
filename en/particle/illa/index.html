Particles · exception · restriction

إِلَّاexcept; only

659× in the Quran

What it does

It excepts. In an affirmative sentence what it excepts is accusative. After a negation it restricts: ‘not … except’ = ‘only’, and the word after it keeps the case its sentence gives it.

Uses

  1. ‘except’: exception
  2. ‘only’: restriction after a negation

‘except’: exception

exception · corpus: 102×

It excepts from an affirmative sentence; what is excepted is accusative.

The word after it is accusative.

‘only’: restriction after a negation

restriction · corpus: 557×

After a negation (مَا, إِنْ, لَا) it restricts the statement to one thing: ‘nothing but’.
