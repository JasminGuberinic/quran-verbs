What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْفَتَى

    the young man (mladić) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفَتَى

    the young man (mladića) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْفَتَى

    in the young man (u mladiću) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْفَتَى

    from the young man (od mladića) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الْفَتَى

    the name of the young man (ime mladića) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَتَىٰهَا from the Quran

    of her/its young man (njenog mladića) · Bosnian genitive
