كَأَنَّas if, as though

31× in the Quran

What it does

Of the إِنَّ family: it puts the noun after it in the accusative, while the predicate stays nominative. It expresses likeness: ‘as if they were…’. In the Quran a pronoun most often follows it.

Uses

‘As if…’: likeness

particle of the إِنَّ family · corpus: 31×

The pronoun is its noun, and what it is likened to is the predicate, in the nominative.
