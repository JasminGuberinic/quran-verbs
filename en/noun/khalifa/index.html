What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخَلِيفَةُ

    the successor (nasljednik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَلِيفَةَ

    the successor (nasljednika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخَلِيفَةِ

    in the successor (u nasljedniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْخَلِيفَةِ

    from the successor (od nasljednika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الْخَلِيفَةِ

    the name of the successor (ime nasljednika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَلِيفَتُهُ

    his/its successor (njegov nasljednik) · Bosnian nominative
