What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النُّفُورُ

    the aversion (odbojnost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النُّفُورَ

    the aversion (odbojnost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي النُّفُورِ

    in the aversion (u odbojnosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ النُّفُورِ

    from the aversion (od odbojnosti) · Bosnian genitive

    his/its aversion (njegova odbojnost) · Bosnian nominative
