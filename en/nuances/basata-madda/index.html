Nuances

To spread out and to draw out

بَسَطَ · مَدَّ

What they share

Both verbs mean that a thing reaches out beyond itself, and both are said of the hand and of the earth. Ar-Rāġib himself explains reaching out the hand (بَسْط) as its ‘madd’, so the meanings touch; by his definitions (spreading vs. pulling) they differ in direction: one is spreading in breadth, the other drawing out in length.

How to tell them apart

To spread and widen — a carpet, the clouds, the hand, provision → بَسَطَ; to pull, to draw out in length — the shade, the earth, a span of time, punishment → مَدَّ.

بَسَطَ to spread out, to reach out, to widen

16× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, to spread a thing out and widen it. To reach out the hand is to extend it — to ask, take, attack or give. When Allah widens provision, it means He makes it ample, as befits Him.

مَدَّ to draw out, to extend

13× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, at root to pull, to draw out; hence مُدَّة, time that stretches on. When it means supplying someone, he notes that Form IV (أَمَدَّ) comes mostly of what is liked, and مَدَّ of what is disliked — such as punishment drawn out.
