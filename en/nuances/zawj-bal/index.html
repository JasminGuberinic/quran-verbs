Nuances

Spouse and husband

زَوْج · بَعْل

What they share

Both words are said of a marriage partner. زَوْج is each of the two who make a pair — husband or wife, and more than marriage; بَعْل is the husband, since he was seen as standing over the woman, and from that anything that stands over another.

How to tell them apart

One of a pair — a spouse, husband or wife, and also a pair, a kind → زَوْج; the husband, the male of the two spouses → بَعْل.

زَوْج spouse, pair

76× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, each of the two who make a pair, male or female, and each of any two that go together — alike or opposite. The word is the same for husband and wife (the form زَوْجَة he calls poor usage), and it also means a kind.

بَعْل husband

7× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the male of the two spouses; since the husband was seen as standing over the woman, as the one who looks after her and is in charge of her (4:34). So whatever stood over another was called بَعْل, and so the idol Baʿl (37:125), which is also counted under this word.
