What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْفاسِقُ

    the transgressor (prijestupnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفاسِقَ

    the transgressor (prijestupnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْفاسِقِ

    in the transgressor (u prijestupniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْفاسِقِ

    from the transgressor (od prijestupnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلَامُ الْفاسِقِ

    the speech of the transgressor (govor prijestupnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَاسِقُهُ

    his/its transgressor (njegov prijestupnik) · Bosnian nominative
