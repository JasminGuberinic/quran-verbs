Nuances

To think

ظَنَّ · حَسِبَ · زَعَمَ

What they share

All three verbs mean ‘to think, to suppose’; they differ in how firm the belief is and how far it can be trusted.

How to tell them apart

An opinion resting on a sign, from guess to certainty → ظَنَّ; to think, without the opposite even coming to mind → حَسِبَ; to claim what is likely false — in the Quran always blamed → زَعَمَ.

ظَنَّ to think, to assume

47× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what arises from a sign; when strong it leads to knowledge, when very weak it goes no further than imagining. Hence in 2:46 it means conviction, and in 45:32 mere supposition.

حَسِبَ to think, to reckon

44× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, judging for one of two opposites without the other even coming to mind — unlike ẓann, where both are in mind and one prevails. In the Quran often of a mistaken belief.

زَعَمَ to claim

13× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, reporting a claim open to suspicion of falsehood; which is why, he says, it comes in the Quran everywhere in blame of those who claim so.
