What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسِّجْنُ

    the prison (zatvor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسِّجْنَ

    the prison (zatvor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسِّجْنِ

    in the prison (u zatvoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلسِّجْنِ

    from the prison (iz zatvora) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ ٱلسِّجْنِ

    the door of the prison (vrata zatvora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سِجْنُهُ

    his/its prison (njegov zatvor) · Bosnian nominative
