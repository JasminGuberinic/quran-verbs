What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْغُرْفَةُ

    the room (soba) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْغُرْفَةَ

    the room (sobu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْغُرْفَةِ

    in the room (u sobi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْغُرْفَةِ

    from the room (iz sobe) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ الْغُرْفَةِ

    the door of the room (vrata sobe) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    غُرْفَتُهُ

    his/its room (njegova soba) · Bosnian nominative
