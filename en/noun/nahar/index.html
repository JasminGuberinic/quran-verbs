What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلنَّهَارُ

    the daytime (dan) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلنَّهَارَ

    the daytime (dan) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ

    in the daytime (u danu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلنَّهَارِ

    with the daytime (danom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ضَوْءُ ٱلنَّهَارِ

    the light of the daytime (svjetlo dana) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَهارُهُ

    his/its daytime (njegov dan) · Bosnian nominative
