Nuances

To eat

أَكَلَ · طَعِمَ

What they share

Both verbs mean ‘to eat’; they differ in range and in figurative use.

How to tell them apart

To eat, and figuratively to ‘devour’ others' wealth → أَكَلَ; to taste, to take food — said even of water and drink → طَعِمَ.

أَكَلَ to eat

93× in the Quran · Verb page →

The general verb for eating. In the Quran it is also used figuratively for seizing wealth: ‘eating’ the property of orphans or of one another.

طَعِمَ to eat, to taste

5× in the Quran · Verb page →

To take food, to taste. Tafsir on 2:249 notes that it is said there even of water, and 5:93 is explained of drink as well.

Sources
