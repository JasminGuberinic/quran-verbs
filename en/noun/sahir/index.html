What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسَّاحِرُ

    the magician (čarobnjak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّاحِرَ

    the magician (čarobnjaka) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّاحِرِ

    in the magician (u čarobnjaku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّاحِرِ

    from the magician (od čarobnjaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عَصَا السَّاحِرِ

    the stick of the magician (štap čarobnjaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَٰحِرُهُ

    his/its magician (njegov čarobnjak) · Bosnian nominative
