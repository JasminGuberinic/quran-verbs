What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْمَلِكُ

    the king (kralj) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَلِكَ

    the king (kralja) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمَلِكِ

    in the king (u kralju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمَلِكِ

    from the king (od kralja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَصْرُ ٱلْمَلِكِ

    the palace of the king (dvorac kralja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَلِكُهُ

    his/its king (njegov kralj) · Bosnian nominative
