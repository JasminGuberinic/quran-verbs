Nuances

Understanding and perceiving

فَقِهَ · شَعَرَ

What they share

Both words mean coming to know something. They differ in the way: by the mind, from the seen to the hidden, or by fine noticing, as with the senses.

How to tell them apart

To understand in depth, reaching from what is seen to what is hidden → فَقِهَ; to perceive, to notice finely, as by the touch of the senses — in the Quran always negated or under ‘if only you perceived’ → شَعَرَ.

فَقِهَ to understand (in depth)

19× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, fiqh is reaching knowledge of the hidden through knowledge of the seen; so it is narrower than knowledge (عِلْم). In the Quran it is mostly negated — they do not understand speech, nor the praise of all creation — but Moses prays to be understood (20:28).

شَعَرَ to perceive

25× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, šuʿūr is fine knowing, as when one touches a hair; مَشَاعِر are the senses. ‘You do not perceive’ is: you do not sense it. He says that in many places ‘they do not reason’ could not stand for ‘they do not perceive’, since the mind knows much that the senses do not catch.
