What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلرُّءْيَا

    the dream (san) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الرُّؤْيَا

    the dream (san) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الرُّؤْيَا

    in the dream (u snu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الرُّؤْيَا

    from the dream (od sna) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    تَفْسِيرُ الرُّؤْيَا

    the interpretation of the dream (tumačenje sna) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    رُءْيَاكَ from the Quran

    your dream (tvoj san) · Bosnian nominative

    The explanation of the dream is difficult.
