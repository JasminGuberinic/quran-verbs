What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْقَرِينُ

    the companion (pratilac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقَرِينَ

    the companion (pratioca) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْقَرِينِ

    in the companion (u pratiocu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْقَرِينِ

    from the companion (od pratioca) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الْقَرِينِ

    the name of the companion (ime pratioca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قَرِينُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its companion (njegov pratilac) · Bosnian nominative
