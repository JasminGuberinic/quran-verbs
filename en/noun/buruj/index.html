What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبُرُوجُ

    the towers (kule) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبُرُوجَ

    the towers (kule) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْبُرُوجِ

    in the towers (u kulama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْبُرُوجِ

    from the towers (iz kula) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ارْتِفَاعُ ٱلْبُرُوجِ

    the height of the towers (visina kula) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بُرُوجُهُ

    his/its towers (njegove kule) · Bosnian nominative
