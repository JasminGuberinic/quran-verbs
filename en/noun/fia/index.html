What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْفِئَةُ

    the group (grupa) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفِئَةَ

    the group (grupu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْفِئَةِ

    from the group (iz grupe) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    in the group (u grupi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَائِدُ الْفِئَةِ

    the leader of the group (vođa grupe) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فِئَتُهُ

    his/its group (njegova grupa) · Bosnian nominative
