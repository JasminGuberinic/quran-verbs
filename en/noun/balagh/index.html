What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْبَلَٰغُ

    the message (poruka) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَلاغَ

    the message (poruku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَلاغِ

    in the message (u poruci) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَلاغِ

    from the message (od poruke) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نَصُّ الْبَلاغِ

    the text of the message (tekst poruke) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَلَٰغُهُ

    his/its message (njegova poruka) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    الْبَلَاغُ وَصَلَ إِلَى الشُّرْطَةِ

    The message reached the police.

  • object (accusative)

    قَرَأَ الْمُذِيعُ الْبَلَاغَ

    The announcer read the message.

  • after a preposition (genitive)

    عَلِمَ الْوَزِيرُ بِالْبَلَاغِ

    The minister found out about the message.

  • possessor (genitive)

    نَصُّ الْبَلَاغِ قَصِيرٌ

    The text of the message is short.
