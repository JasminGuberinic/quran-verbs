What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    اللَّبَنُ

    the milk (mlijeko) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    اللَّبَنَ

    the milk (mlijeko) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ اللَّبَنِ

    from the milk (od mlijeka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي اللَّبَنِ

    in the milk (u mlijeku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ اللَّبَنِ

    the colour of the milk (boja mlijeka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    لَبَنُهُ

    his/its milk (njegovo mlijeko) · Bosnian nominative
