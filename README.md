# Fiʿl — learn Quranic Arabic: verbs, conjugation, words and roots of the Quran

### 🟢 Live and free to use: **[jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/)**

**Fiʿl** (فِعْل, “verb”) is a free website for learning the Arabic of the Quran. Each word is
taught as it stands in the Quran, with its ayat, translation, audio and exercises. No sign-up,
no ads, no payments, and it stays that way.

## What you can learn

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**Verbs of the Quran**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/) | The 600 most frequent Quranic verbs. For each: root and verb form (I–X), meaning, the full conjugation table (past, present, command), every form the Quran uses with its meaning and grammar, example ayat, audio and practice sentences. |
| [**Nouns**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/noun/) | 700 Quranic nouns with singular, plural, ayat, audio and pictures. |
| [**Adjectives**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/adjective/) | 105 adjectives with gender, number and case agreement. |
| [**Near-synonyms**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/nuances/) | 255 groups of words that look alike in translation, and why the Quran uses one rather than another (e.g. *insān*, *bashar*, *rajul*). |
| [**Particles**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/particle/) and [**pronouns**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/pronoun/) | 64 particles and 17 pronouns: the small words that hold an ayah together. |
| [**Roots**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/root/) | 735 Arabic roots, each with its core idea and the Quranic words built from it. |
| [**Surahs**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/surah/) | The vocabulary of all 114 surahs, with how much of each surah the site covers. |
| [**Exercises**](https://jasminguberinic.github.io/quran-verbs/en/exercises/) | 10 kinds of exercises: listen and choose, picture, verb form ↔ meaning, find the form in an ayah, cases, prepositions, agreement, near-synonyms… |

Every Arabic word can be heard. A synthetic voice reads the forms and sentences from a
vowel-checked text, and real recitation plays wherever the word occurs in the Quran. The site
works on a phone and in light or dark mode, and your browser can translate the page into any
other language. The Arabic and the ayat are never machine-translated.

**Who it is for:** anyone who recites the Quran and wants to understand it. That includes
beginners, students in a madrasa or maktab, and teachers looking for word lists, conjugation
tables and exercises.

## For developers

The site is generated by `fil`, a Python content engine with a hexagonal architecture, golden
tests and strict quality gates. The gates cover the grammar of every practice sentence, a blind
AI review, ayah-quote and licence checks, an audio check of every clip, broken links and SEO.
The engine writes static pages that are published to GitHub Pages.

```sh
pip install -e .          # Python 3.11+
fil pages                 # build the whole site into build/pages
pytest -q && node --test tests/js
```

Code and documentation are on the
[`study-site`](https://github.com/JasminGuberinic/quran-verbs/tree/study-site) branch. Start with
[CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/JasminGuberinic/quran-verbs/blob/study-site/CLAUDE.md) and
[docs/](https://github.com/JasminGuberinic/quran-verbs/tree/study-site/docs).
