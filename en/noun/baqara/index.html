What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَقَرَةُ

    the cow (krava) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَقَرَةَ

    the cow (kravu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَقَرَةِ

    in the cow (u kravi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَقَرَةِ

    from the cow (od krave) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَبَنُ الْبَقَرَةِ

    the milk of the cow (mlijeko krave) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَقَرَتُهُ

    his/its cow (njegova krava) · Bosnian nominative
