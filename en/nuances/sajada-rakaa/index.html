Prostrating and bowing

سَجَدَ · رَكَعَ

What they share

Both verbs are postures of humility and parts of the prayer. According to ar-Rāġib, سُجُود is at root lowering and humbling oneself, and is said of people, animals and lifeless things, while رُكُوع is bending — the posture in prayer, or humility in worship and outside it.

How to tell them apart

To prostrate — the forehead to the ground; also the submission of all creation to Allah, willingly or not → سَجَدَ; to bow — the posture in prayer; in the Quran the verb is used only of people → رَكَعَ.

سَجَدَ to prostrate

35× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root is lowering oneself and humility. Prostration is of two kinds: by choice — only of man, and it is rewarded — and the prostration of subjection, by which all creation, people, animals and plants, testifies that it is created. In the Sharia it is the known pillar of the prayer.

رَكَعَ to bow

5× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, bending: sometimes the special posture in the prayer, sometimes humility and submission, in worship or outside it. In the Quran the verb is said only of people, and mostly together with the prayer and prostration.
