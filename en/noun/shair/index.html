What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الشَّاعِرُ

    the poet (pjesnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الشَّاعِرَ

    the poet (pjesnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الشَّاعِرِ

    in the poet (u pjesniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الشَّاعِرِ

    from the poet (od pjesnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    دِيوَانُ الشَّاعِرِ

    the collection of poems of the poet (zbirka pjesama pjesnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    شَاعِرُهُ

    his/its poet (njegov pjesnik) · Bosnian nominative
