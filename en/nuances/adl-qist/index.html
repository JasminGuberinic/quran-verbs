Nuances

Justice

عَدْل · قِسْط

What they share

Both words mean justice; they differ in whether equality and balance or the fair share due to each is in view.

How to tell them apart

Justice as equality, a fair judgement, even an ‘equivalent’ → عَدْل; the fair share, right measure and weight → قِسْط.

عَدْل justice, equity

14× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, equality: dividing into equal shares, said of what is grasped by insight, such as rulings. Hence also an ‘equivalent’: fasting equal to the food of an expiation (5:95, as he explains it); in 2:48 mufassirun take it as a ransom of equal value.

قِسْط fairness, the just share

15× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the share given justly. So قِسْط often stands with measure and weighing (our observation), and from the same root a قَاسِط is the unjust who takes another's share, a مُقْسِط the just who gives it.
