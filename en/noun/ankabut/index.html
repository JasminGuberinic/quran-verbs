What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَنْكَبُوتُ

    the spider (pauk) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعَنْكَبُوتَ

    the spider (pauka) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْعَنكَبُوتِ

    in the spider (u pauku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْعَنكَبُوتِ

    from the spider (od pauka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    خَيْطُ ٱلْعَنكَبُوتِ

    the thread of the spider (nit pauka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَنْكَبُوتُهُ

    his/its spider (njegov pauk) · Bosnian nominative
