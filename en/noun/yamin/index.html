What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْيَمِينُ

    the right hand (desnica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْيَمِينَ

    the right hand (desnicu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْيَمِينِ

    with the right hand (desnicom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْيَمِينِ

    in the right hand (u desnici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    جِهَةُ ٱلْيَمِينِ

    the side of the right hand (strana desnice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    يَمِينُهُ

    his/its right hand (njegova desnica) · Bosnian nominative
