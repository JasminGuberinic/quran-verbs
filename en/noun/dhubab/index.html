What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلذُّبَابُ

    the flies (muhe) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الذُّبابَ

    the flies (muhe) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الذُّبابِ

    in the flies (u muhama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الذُّبابِ

    from the flies (od muha) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ الذُّبابِ

    the sound of the flies (zvuk muha) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ذُبابُهُ

    his/its flies (njegove muhe) · Bosnian nominative
