What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الشِّهابُ

    the meteor (meteor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الشِّهابَ

    the meteor (meteor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالشِّهابِ

    with the meteor (meteorom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الشِّهابِ

    in the meteor (u meteoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ضَوْءُ الشِّهابِ

    the light of the meteor (svjetlost meteora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    شِهَابُهُ

    his/its meteor (njegov meteor) · Bosnian nominative
