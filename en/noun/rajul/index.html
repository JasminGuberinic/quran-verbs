What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الرَّجُلُ

    the man (muškarac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الرَّجُلَ

    the man (muškarca) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الرَّجُلِ

    on the man (na muškarcu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى الرَّجُلِ

    to the man (do muškarca) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الرَّجُلِ

    the name of the man (ime muškarca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    رَجُلُهُ

    his/its man (njegov muškarac) · Bosnian nominative
