What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الطَّائِرُ

    the bird (ptica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الطَّائِرَ

    the bird (pticu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الطَّائِرِ

    in the bird (na ptici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الطَّائِرِ

    from the bird (od ptice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    جَنَاحُ الطَّائِرِ

    the wing of the bird (krilo ptice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طائِرُهُ

    his/its bird (njegova ptica) · Bosnian nominative
