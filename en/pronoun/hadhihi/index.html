Pronouns · demonstrative

هَٰذِهِthis (f.)

47× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The demonstrative of the near, feminine singular (هَا + ذِهِ). It is also used for a plural of things and animals, which in Arabic agrees as a feminine singular.

Uses

‘This …’, ‘this is …’

demonstrative · corpus: 47×

As with هَٰذَا: a noun with the article after it usually names what is pointed at, a noun without it is the predicate; but a definite noun with a relative clause can also be the predicate (52:14).
