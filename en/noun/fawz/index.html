What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْفَوْزُ

    the victory (pobjeda) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفَوْزَ

    the victory (pobjedu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْفَوْزِ

    in the victory (u pobjedi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْفَوْزِ

    from the victory (od pobjede) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    فَرْحَةُ الْفَوْزِ

    the joy of the victory (radost pobjede) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَوْزُهُ

    his/its victory (njegova pobjeda) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    الْفَوْزُ قَرِيبٌ

    Victory is near.

  • object (accusative)

    حَقَّقَ الْفَرِيقُ الْفَوْزَ

  • after a preposition (genitive)

  • possessor (genitive)

    The joy of the victory is great.
