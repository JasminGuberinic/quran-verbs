What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْكِبَرُ

    the old age (starost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْكِبَرَ

    the old age (starost) · Bosnian accusative

    عَلَى ٱلْكِبَرِ

    on the old age (na starosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْكِبَرِ

    from the old age (od starosti) · Bosnian genitive

    سِنُّ ٱلْكِبَرِ

    the age of the old age (dob starosti) · Bosnian genitive

    كِبَرُهُ

    his/its old age (njegova starost) · Bosnian nominative
