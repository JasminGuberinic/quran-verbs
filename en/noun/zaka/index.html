What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الزَّكاةُ

    the charity (zekat) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ

    the charity (zekat) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةِ

    from the charity (od zekata) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    in the charity (u zekatu) · Bosnian locative

    زَكاتُهُ

    his/its charity (njegov zekat) · Bosnian nominative
