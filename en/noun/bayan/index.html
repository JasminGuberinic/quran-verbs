What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَيانُ

    the statement (izjava) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَيَانَ

    the statement (izjavu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَيانِ

    in the statement (u izjavi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَيانِ

    from the statement (od izjave) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نَصُّ الْبَيانِ

    the text of the statement (tekst izjave) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَيَانَهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its statement (njegovu izjavu) · Bosnian accusative
