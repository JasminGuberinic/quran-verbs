What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَسَاوِرُ

    the bracelets (narukvice) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَسَاوِرَ

    the bracelets (narukvice) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَسَاوِرِ

    from the bracelets (iz narukvica) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْأَسَاوِرِ

    in the bracelets (u narukvicama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    the price of the bracelets (cijena narukvica) · Bosnian genitive
