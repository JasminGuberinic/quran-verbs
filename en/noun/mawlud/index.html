What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَوْلُودُ

    the newborn (novorođenče) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَوْلُودَ

    the newborn (novorođenče) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلْمَوْلُودِ

    on the newborn (na novorođenčetu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    in the newborn (u novorođenčetu) · Bosnian locative
