Voice. Every form, noun and sentence is spoken by a synthetic voice (tts_arabic) from a reading checked vowel by vowel. The “as recited” button plays a real reciter wherever the Quran contains that form.

Translations: Bosnian is read by a person, English is checked against a lexicon (CAMeL), other languages are unreviewed AI translations.
