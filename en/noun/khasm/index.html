What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخَصْمُ

    the adversary (protivnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَصْمَ

    the adversary (protivnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْخَصْمِ

    in the adversary (u protivniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْخَصْمِ

    from the adversary (od protivnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ ٱلْخَصْمِ

    the strength of the adversary (snaga protivnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَصْمُهُ

    his/its adversary (njegov protivnik) · Bosnian nominative
