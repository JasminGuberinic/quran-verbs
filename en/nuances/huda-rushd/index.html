Nuances

Guidance and right conduct

هُدًى · رُشْد

What they share

Both words speak of the right way; they differ in whether showing the way or the soundness and maturity of the one who walks it is in view.

How to tell them apart

Guidance Allah gives, the Book as guidance → هُدًى; rightness, maturity and good sense, the opposite of error (غَيّ) → رُشْد.

هُدًى guidance

85× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, guiding with gentleness. Allah's guidance is fourfold: general guidance to every creature, the call through the prophets, help (tawfīq) to the guided, and leading into Paradise. He notes that the word هُدًى is reserved for guidance Allah Himself grants — so the Book itself is called هُدًى.

رُشْد right course, maturity

11× in the Quran · Noun page →

Soundness of mind and conduct, the opposite of error (غَيّ). It is said even of an orphan's maturity to manage property. ar-Rāġib reports the view that رُشْد is said of worldly and otherworldly matters, and رَشَد of otherworldly matters only.

Sources
