What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْوَادِي

    the valley (dolina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْوَادِيَ

    the valley (dolinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْوَادِ

    with the valley (dolinom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلْوَادِ

    on the valley (na dolini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَرِيقُ ٱلْوَادِ

    the road of the valley (put doline) · Bosnian genitive

Practice sentences

    الْوَادِي أَخْضَرُ

  • object (accusative)

    عَبَرَ الْمُسَافِرُونَ الْوَادِيَ
