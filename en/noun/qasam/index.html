What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقَسَمُ

    the oath (zakletva) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقَسَمَ

    the oath (zakletvu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْقَسَمِ

    in the oath (u zakletvi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْقَسَمِ

    from the oath (od zakletve) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صِدْقُ الْقَسَمِ

    the sincerity of the oath (iskrenost zakletve) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قَسَمُهُ

    his/its oath (njegova zakletva) · Bosnian nominative
