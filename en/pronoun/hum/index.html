هُمْthey

442× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The personal pronoun of the third person, masculine plural (also for a mixed group). Before a connecting hamza it is said هُمُ (هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ).

Uses

  1. Subject: ‘they…’
  2. The pronoun of separation (ḍamīr al-faṣl)

Subject: ‘they…’

personal pronoun

Often in describing a group, before a verb or a predicate.

The pronoun of separation (ḍamīr al-faṣl)

personal pronoun

Between a subject and a definite predicate the pronoun says that what follows is the predicate, not an adjective or a follower – hence its name ‘separation’ (faṣl), and also ‘support’ (ʿimād). Ibn Hišām names two more benefits, emphasis and restriction, and az-Zamaḫšarī says all three on 2:5 – ‘they, and they alone’ (of هُمْ in 2:5).

Sources

  • Ibn Hišām al-Anṣārī, Muġnī al-labīb ʿan kutub al-aʿārīb
