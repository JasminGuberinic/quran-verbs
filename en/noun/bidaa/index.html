What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبِضاعَةُ

    the merchandise (roba) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبِضاعَةَ

    the merchandise (robu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْبِضاعَةِ

    with the merchandise (s robom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبِضاعَةِ

    in the merchandise (u robi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ثَمَنُ الْبِضاعَةِ

    the price of the merchandise (cijena robe) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بِضَٰعَتَهُمْ from the Quran

    their merchandise (njihovu robu) · Bosnian accusative
