Particles · preposition

بِـwith, by; in

2544× in the Quran

What it does

A preposition: the noun after it is in the genitive (a kasra at the end: ٱلْأَرْضِ, أَرْضٍ). A pronoun is joined to it as a suffix (بِهِ ‘with it’). It is written joined to the word after it (بِسْمِ). Its basic meaning is ‘contact, attachment’ (al-ilṣāq; Sībawayh's own word is al-ilzāq).

  1. ‘with, by means of’: the instrument

‘with, by means of’: the instrument

preposition

The means by which something is done (al-istiʿāna).

The noun after it is genitive.

‘in, at’: place and time

preposition

Like فِي: a place or a time (aẓ-ẓarfiyya).

The noun after it is genitive.

With a verb: ‘take away’

preposition

With a verb of motion it makes the subject carry something along (at-taʿdiya): ذَهَبَ ‘went’, ذَهَبَ بِـ ‘took away’. Ibn Hišām cites this very ayah.

The noun after it is genitive.

Emphasis in a negation

preposition

In the predicate after لَيْسَ or مَا it strengthens the negation; Ibn Hišām calls it additional (zāʾida). It is not translated.

The noun after it is genitive.
