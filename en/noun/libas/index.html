What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    اللِّباسُ

    the clothing (odjeća) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    اللِّباسَ

    the clothing (odjeću) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي اللِّباسِ

    in the clothing (u odjeći) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ اللِّباسِ

    from the clothing (od odjeće) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ اللِّباسِ

    the color of the clothing (boja odjeće) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    لِبَاسَهُمَا from the Quran

    their clothing (njihovu odjeću) · Bosnian accusative
