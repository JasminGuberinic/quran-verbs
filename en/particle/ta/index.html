Particles · oath

تَـby (Allah)

9× in the Quran

What it does

The preposition of an oath, like وَ and بِـ: the noun after it is genitive. Ibn Hišām says it comes almost only with Allah's name (rarely with رَبّ) and carries a note of wonder; az-Zamaḫšarī says on 21:57 that بِـ is the root of the oath, وَ stands in for it, and تَـ stands in for وَ, with wonder added.

Uses

تَٱللَّهِ – ‘by Allah’

oath · corpus: 9×

All nine times in the Quran: تَٱللَّهِ, followed by the answer to the oath, mostly with لَـ.

The noun after it is genitive.
