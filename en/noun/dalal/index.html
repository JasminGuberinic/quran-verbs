What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلضَّلَٰلُ

    the error (zabluda) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الضَّلالَ

    the error (zabludu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلضَّلَٰلِ

    in the error (u zabludi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلضَّلَٰلِ

    from the error (od zablude) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَرِيقُ ٱلضَّلَٰلِ

    the way of the error (put zablude) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ضَلالُهُ

    his/its error (njegova zabluda) · Bosnian nominative
