Dominion and authority

مُلْك · سُلْطَان

What they share

Both words mean power over something. According to ar-Rāġib, مُلْك is controlling a thing by one's rule, and the true, lasting one belongs to Allah; سُلْطَان is the power to overpower — and also proof, since it storms the hearts.

How to tell them apart

Dominion, kingship: control of what one rules; the true and lasting one only Allah's → مُلْك; the power to overpower, authority over someone; and proof that prevails → سُلْطَان.

مُلْك dominion, kingship

48× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the controlling of a thing by one's rule; every مُلْك is ownership, but not every ownership is مُلْك. The true and lasting مُلْك belongs to Allah, and He gives it to whom He wills.

سُلْطَان authority, power; proof

37× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, from سَلَاطَة, the power to overpower: سُلْطَان is that power, and more often the one who holds it. Proof, he says, is called سُلْطَان because it storms the hearts — though its force falls mostly on the people of knowledge and wisdom among the believers.
