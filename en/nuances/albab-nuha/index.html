Nuances

Understanding and intelligence

أَلْبَاب · نُهَى

What they share

Both words mean the intellect, and both come in the phrase ‘those who have…’ (أُولُو). Going by ar-Rāġib's definitions, they differ in what they stress about the mind: its purity, or its holding back from what is vile.

How to tell them apart

The intellect cleansed of admixture, the kernel of a person's mind, to which lessons and reminders are addressed → أَلْبَاب; the intelligence that restrains from what is vile, beside signs in nature and history → نُهَى.

أَلْبَاب understanding, minds

16× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, lubb is the intellect pure of admixture, the kernel of a person; some say: the intellect that has grown pure, so every lubb is an intellect but not every intellect a lubb. That is why, he says, Allah ties what only purified minds grasp to ‘those of understanding’. In the Quran they are mostly told to take heed or to fear Allah.

نُهَى intelligence (that restrains)

2× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, from the root of forbidding and holding back: the intelligence that restrains from vile deeds. It comes twice in the Quran, both times in signs “for men of thought” (20:54, 20:128), beside what the eyes can see: pasture on the earth and the ruins of destroyed peoples.
