What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    اللَّحْمُ

    the meat (meso) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    اللَّحْمَ

    the meat (meso) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي اللَّحْمِ

    in the meat (u mesu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ اللَّحْمِ

    from the meat (od mesa) · Bosnian genitive

    the taste of the meat (okus mesa) · Bosnian genitive
