What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعُنُقُ

    the neck (vrat) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعُنُقَ

    the neck (vrat) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعُنُقِ

    in the neck (u vratu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى الْعُنُقِ

    to the neck (do vrata) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَلَمُ الْعُنُقِ

    the pain of the neck (bol vrata) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَعْنَٰقُهُمْ from the Quran

    their necks (njihovi vratovi) · Bosnian nominative
