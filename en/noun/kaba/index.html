What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكَعْبَةُ

    the Kaaba (Kaba) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْكَعْبَةَ

    the Kaaba (Kabu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْكَعْبَةِ

    in the Kaaba (u Kabi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْكَعْبَةِ

    from the Kaaba (od Kabe) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ ٱلْكَعْبَةِ

    the door of the Kaaba (vrata Kabe) · Bosnian genitive

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    الْكَعْبَةُ فِي مَكَّةَ

    The Kaaba is in Mecca.

  • object (accusative)

    رَأَى الطِّفْلُ الْكَعْبَةَ

    The child saw the Kaaba.

  • after a preposition (genitive)

    مَشَى الْحُجَّاجُ إِلَى الْكَعْبَةِ

    The pilgrims walked to the Kaaba.

  • possessor (genitive)

    بَابُ الْكَعْبَةِ جَمِيلٌ

    The door of the Kaaba is beautiful.
