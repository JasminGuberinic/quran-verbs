What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحَبْلُ

    the rope (uže) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحَبْلَ

    the rope (uže) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْحَبْلِ

    with the rope (užetom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحَبْلِ

    from the rope (od užeta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَرَفُ الْحَبْلِ

    the end of the rope (kraj užeta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِبَالُهُمْ from the Quran

    their ropes (njihova užeta) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

    The rope is long.

  • object (accusative)

    يَرْبُطُ الْبَحَّارُ الْحَبْلَ

    جَرَّ الْفَلَّاحُ الْبَقَرَةَ بِالْحَبْلِ

    The farmer pulled the cow with the rope.

  • possessor (genitive)

    لَوْنُ الْحَبْلِ أَصْفَرُ

    The color of the rope is yellow.
