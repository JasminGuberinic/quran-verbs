Offspring and progeny

ذُرِّيَّة · نَسْل

What they share

Both words mean those born of a person. They differ in what the word brings out: offspring as a line and children, or progeny as what separates off from the parent.

How to tell them apart

Descendants, originally the young children, though in use both young and old, one or many — the word of the prophets' lines and of prayers for children → ذُرِّيَّة; progeny as what ‘issues’ from the father, a word of the same root as ‘to come away’ → نَسْل.

ذُرِّيَّة offspring, descendants

32× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ḏurriyya are originally the young among descendants, but in use it is said of young and old alike, of one and of many. In the Quran it is the word of the prophets' lines and of prayers for children.

نَسْل progeny

2× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, the root means separating from a thing (as fur comes off a camel), and nasl is the child because it ‘issues’ from its father. In the Quran it comes twice: the progeny a corrupter destroys, and man's progeny made from a worthless fluid.
