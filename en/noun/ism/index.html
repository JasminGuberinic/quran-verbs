What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلِٱسْمُ

    the name (ime) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْاِسْمَ

    the name (ime) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْاِسْمِ

    in the name (u imenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْاِسْمِ

    from the name (od imena) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كِتَابَةُ الْاِسْمِ

    the writing of the name (pisanje imena) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ٱسْمُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its name (njegovo ime) · Bosnian nominative
