بَلَىyes (indeed)!

22× in the Quran

What it does

A word of answer that affirms what was negated: it answers a negative question or a denial and turns it into an affirmation. Unlike نَعَمْ, which confirms what was said.

Uses

‘Yes!’ to a negative question

answer · corpus: 22×

To “Am I not your Lord?” (7:172) the answer بَلَىٰ means ‘Yes, You are!’.
