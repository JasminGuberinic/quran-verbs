What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجَبَلُ

    the mountain (planina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْجَبَلَ

    the mountain (planinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْجَبَلِ

    from the mountain (iz planine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْجَبَلِ

    to the mountain (do planine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    رَأْسُ ٱلْجَبَلِ

    the top of the mountain (vrh planine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جَبَلُهُ

    his/its mountain (njegova planina) · Bosnian nominative
