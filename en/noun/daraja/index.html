What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الدَّرَجَةُ

    the degree (stepen) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الدَّرَجَةَ

    the degree (stepen) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الدَّرَجَةِ

    in the degree (u stepenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الدَّرَجَةِ

    from the degree (iz stepena) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اِرْتِفَاعُ الدَّرَجَةِ

    the rise of the degree (porast stepena) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    دَرَجَتُهُ

    his/its degree (njegov stepen) · Bosnian nominative
