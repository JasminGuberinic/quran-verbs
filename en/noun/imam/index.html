What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْإِمَامُ

    the leader (vođa) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْإِمَامَ

    the leader (vođu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْإِمَامِ

    in the leader (u vođi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْإِمَامِ

    from the leader (od vođe) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ الْإِمَامِ

    the voice of the leader (glas vođe) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    إِمَامُهُ

    his/its leader (njegov vođa) · Bosnian nominative
