إِذًاthen, in that case

31× in the Quran

What it does

A word of answer and consequence: ‘then (it is so)…’. Sībawayh says it means answer and requital (al-jawāb wa-l-jazāʾ). Grammarians say it puts a present-tense verb in the subjunctive when it heads its clause, the verb means the future and nothing stands between them; in the ayat cited here it changes nothing.

Uses

‘Then…’: answering

answer · corpus: 31×

It draws the conclusion of what was said before.
