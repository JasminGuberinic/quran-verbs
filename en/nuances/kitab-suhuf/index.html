Nuances

Book and scrolls

كِتَاب · صُحُف

What they share

Both words name what is written; they differ in whether it is a gathered whole or leaves.

How to tell them apart

A written work gathered into a whole, the revealed Scripture, and also decree and ordinance → كِتَاب; leaves one writes on — the scrolls of Abraham and Moses, the records of deeds → صُحُف.

كِتَاب book, decree

260× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root sense is joining leather to leather by sewing, and in usage joining letters in writing. In the Quran: the revealed Scripture, the record of deeds, and also what is ordained and decreed (8:68).

صُحُف scrolls, pages

8× in the Quran

Plural of ṣaḥīfa; according to ar-Rāġib, ‘what is spread flat’ — the leaf one writes on. In the Quran: the scrolls revealed to Abraham and Moses, and the records of deeds that will be spread open.
