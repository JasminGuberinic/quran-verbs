What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَيْنُ

    the eye (oko) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْعَيْنَ

    the eye (oko) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْعَيْنِ

    with the eye (okom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْعَيْنِ

    in the eye (u oku) · Bosnian locative

    لَوْنُ ٱلْعَيْنِ

    the colour of the eye (boja oka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَيْنُهُ

    his/its eye (njegovo oko) · Bosnian nominative
