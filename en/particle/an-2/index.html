Particles · preposition

عَنْfrom, away from; about

465× in the Quran

What it does

A preposition: the noun after it is in the genitive (a kasra at the end: ٱلْأَرْضِ, أَرْضٍ). A pronoun is joined to it as a suffix (عَنْهُ ‘from him, about him’).

Uses

  1. ‘away from’: moving off
  2. ‘about’: with ask, speak
  3. ‘after’: one after another

‘away from’: moving off

preposition

Moving away from something (al-mujāwaza) – the basic meaning.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘about’: with ask, speak

preposition

With verbs of asking and speaking: about what.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘after’: one after another

preposition

Ibn Hišām also gives the meaning ‘after’ (al-baʿdiyya), with this ayah: ‘stage after stage’.

The noun after it is genitive.
