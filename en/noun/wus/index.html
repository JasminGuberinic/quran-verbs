What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْوُسْعُ

    the capability (sposobnost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْوُسْعَ

    the capability (sposobnost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْوُسْعِ

    in the capability (u sposobnosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْوُسْعِ

    from the capability (od sposobnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    حُدُودُ الْوُسْعِ

    the limits of the capability (granice sposobnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    وُسْعَهَا from the Quran

    her/its capability (njenu sposobnost) · Bosnian accusative
