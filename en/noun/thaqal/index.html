What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَثْقَالُ

    the loads (tereti) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَثْقَالَ

    the loads (terete) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْأَثْقَالِ

    in the loads (u teretima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَثْقَالِ

    from the loads (iz tereta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَزْنُ الْأَثْقَالِ

    the weight of the loads (težina tereta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَثْقَالُهُ

    his/its loads (njegovi tereti) · Bosnian nominative
