Nuances

Error and straying

ضَلَال · غَيّ

What they share

Both words mean leaving the right way; they differ in the cause: any straying, or error that springs from a corrupt belief.

How to tell them apart

Any straying from the straight path, intended or not, small or great → ضَلَال; error rooted in a corrupt belief, the opposite of رُشْد → غَيّ.

ضَلَال error, straying

38× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, turning aside from the straight path — any such turning, intended or not, small or great. Hence, he notes, it is said both of a mere oversight or forgetting and of the far error of disbelief (ضَلَالًا بَعِيدًا).

غَيّ error, deviation

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ignorance that springs from a corrupt belief. It stands against رُشْد, and the Quran uses it of those who choose the wrong way (7:146) and follow their desires.
