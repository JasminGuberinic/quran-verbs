Nuances

Enmity and hatred

عَدَاوَة · بَغْضَاء

What they share

Both words speak of what divides people. In the Quran they stand together four times: enmity and hatred.

How to tell them apart

A breach and overstepping, when hearts do not fit together — a state between people → عَدَاوَة; the soul's turning away from something, the opposite of love → بَغْضَاء.

عَدَاوَة enmity

6× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root means overstepping and not fitting together; when it concerns the heart it is called ʿadāwa. In Quranic usage it is a state between two sides (“between whom and thee”, 41:34), which a good deed can turn into friendship.

بَغْضَاء hatred

5× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, buġḍ is the soul's aversion from what it turns away from — the opposite of love, which is the soul's attraction to what it desires. It is a feeling in the soul; in one ayah the Quran says hatred has already shown in speech, and what the breasts conceal is greater.
