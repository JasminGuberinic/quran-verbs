What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْحَقُّ

    the truth (istina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْحَقَّ

    the truth (istinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْحَقِّ

    with the truth (s istinom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْحَقِّ

    from the truth (od istine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نُورُ ٱلْحَقِّ

    the light of the truth (svjetlo istine) · Bosnian genitive

Practice sentences

  • subject (nominative)

    الْحَقُّ وَاضِحٌ

    The truth is clear.

  • object (accusative)

    قَالَ الشَّاهِدُ الْحَقَّ

    The witness told the truth.

    دَافَعَ الْمُحَامِي عَنِ الْحَقِّ

    The lawyer defended the truth.

  • possessor (genitive)

    صَوْتُ الْحَقِّ قَوِيٌّ

    The voice of truth is strong.
