What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّبَبُ

    the cause (uzrok) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّبَبَ

    the cause (uzrok) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّبَبِ

    in the cause (u uzroku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّبَبِ

    from the cause (od uzroka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَيَانُ السَّبَبِ

    the explanation of the cause (objašnjenje uzroka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَبَبُهُ

    his/its cause (njegov uzrok) · Bosnian nominative
