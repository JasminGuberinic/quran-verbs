Nuances

To touch

مَسَّ · لَمَسَ

What they share

Both verbs mean to touch. According to ar-Rāġib, lams is perception by the surface of the skin, like mass, but is also said of seeking; and mass is said of whatever harm befalls a person.

How to tell them apart

To touch, to befall — most often harm, evil or punishment that ‘touches’ a person; and a gentle word for marital relations → مَسَّ; to feel with the hand, and to seek → لَمَسَ.

مَسَّ to touch, to befall

56× in the Quran · Verb page →

To touch; in the Quran most often of harm, evil or punishment that befalls a person — good may ‘touch’ him too. It is also a gentle word for marital relations.

لَمَسَ to touch, to feel; to seek

4× in the Quran · Verb page →

To touch and feel with the hand, perceiving by touch. According to ar-Rāġib, it is said of seeking too — the jinn ‘touched’ the heaven, i.e. sought to reach it.
