Stone and rock

حَجَر · صَخْرَة

What they share

Both words mean the hard matter of the ground and the hills. They differ in degree: stone as such, or especially hard stone — rock.

How to tell them apart

Stone, the general word — stones that fall as punishment, that fuel the Fire, to which hard hearts are likened → حَجَر (plural حِجَارَة); especially hard stone, rock that is hewn → صَخْرَة.

حَجَر stone, stones

12× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, حَجَر is the well-known hard substance; its plurals are أَحْجَار and حِجَارَة. In the Quran it is mostly the plural حِجَارَة: stones that fall on peoples punished, stones that fuel the Fire, and stones to which hard hearts are likened (2:74).

صَخْرَة rock

3× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, صَخْر is hard stone. In the Quran it comes three times: the rock in which not even a mustard seed is hidden from Allah (31:16), the rocks Thamud hewed in the valley (89:9), and the rock where Moses and his young man took shelter (18:63).

Sources
