What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَصْلُ

    the root (korijen) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَصْلَ

    the root (korijen) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْأَصْلِ

    in the root (u korijenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْأَصْلِ

    on the root (na korijenu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْأَصْلِ

    the length of the root (dužina korijena) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَصْلُهَا from the Quran

    her/its root (njen korijen) · Bosnian nominative
