What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخَيْرَةُ

    the good deed (dobro djelo) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَيْرَةَ

    the good deed (dobro djelo) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخَيْرَةِ

    in the good deed (u dobrom djelu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْخَيْرَةِ

    from the good deed (od dobrog djela) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    فِعْلُ الْخَيْرَةِ

    the doing of the good deed (činjenje dobrog djela) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَيْرَتُهُ

    his/its good deed (njegovo dobro djelo) · Bosnian nominative

    فِعْلُ الْخَيْرَاتِ جَمِيلٌ

    Doing good deeds is beautiful.
