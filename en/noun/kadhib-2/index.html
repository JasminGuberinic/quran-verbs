What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكاذِبُ

    the liar (lažac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكاذِبَ

    the liar (lašca) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْكاذِبِ

    from the liar (od lašca) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْكاذِبِ

    on the liar (na lašcu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلَامُ الْكاذِبِ

    the speech of the liar (govor lašca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَٰذِبُهُ

    his/its liar (njegov lažac) · Bosnian nominative
