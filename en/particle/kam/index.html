Particles · quantity (“how many a…”) · question word

كَمْhow many?; how many a…

21× in the Quran

What it does

A noun of quantity. Ibn Hišām tells two apart: the كَمْ of asking (‘how many?’, seeking a number) and the كَمْ of report (‘how many a…’, saying there was much of something). Both are nouns, vague, need a word saying of what (tamyīz), never change and come first. In the Quran what there was much of is mostly brought in with مِنْ: كَم مِّن قَرْيَةٍ – ‘how many a city’.

Uses

  1. ‘How many a…!’ – a great number
  2. Asking ‘how many?’

‘How many a…!’ – a great number

quantity (“how many a…”) · corpus: 17×

It does not ask but says there was much of something: how many peoples were destroyed, how many gardens left behind. Ibn Hišām says that in 36:31 كَمْ is the object of أَهْلَكْنَا, not something the clause after it stands in for. The corpus files this use with no question tag.

Asking ‘how many?’

question word · corpus: 4×

It asks for a number or measure; the answer is a number.
