What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْفاكِهَةُ

    the fruit (voće) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْفاكِهَةَ

    the fruit (voće) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْفاكِهَةِ

    with the fruit (voćem) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْفاكِهَةِ

    in the fruit (u voću) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَبَقُ الْفاكِهَةِ

    the plate of the fruit (tanjir voća) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فَٰكِهَتُهُ

    his/its fruit (njegovo voće) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences

  • possessor (genitive)

    The dish of fruit is on the table.
