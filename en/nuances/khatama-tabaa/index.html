Nuances

Sealing

خَتَمَ · طَبَعَ

What they share

The Quran says both of the hearts of those who disbelieve. According to ar-Rāġib, both are the impress of a seal, and طَبْع is the wider: shaping a thing into a form (hence also طَبِيعَة, nature).

How to tell them apart

To seal, close and complete — the seal that shuts (and the “Seal of the Prophets”, 33:40) → خَتَمَ; to imprint, to give a shape — a heart for which sin has become nature → طَبَعَ.

خَتَمَ to seal

5× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the seal that shuts and bars, and that by which a thing is finished. Allah seals hearts, he says, by the way He has set: when a person persists in falsehood and sin and never turns to the truth, it leaves him in a state where sins seem good to him — as if his heart were sealed.

طَبَعَ to imprint, to seal

11× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, giving a thing its shape, as when coins are minted; it is wider than خَتْم, and from the same word comes طَبِيعَة, nature. Nature itself, he says, is a form imprinted on the soul, by birth or by habit; some take it as ‘soiled’, like رَانَ (83:14).
