What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ

    the proof (dokaz) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَيِّنَةَ

    the proof (dokaz) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْبَيِّنَةِ

    with the proof (dokazom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْبَيِّنَةِ

    on the proof (na dokazu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ الْبَيِّنَةِ

    the strength of the proof (snaga dokaza) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَيِّنَتُهُ

    his/its proof (njegov dokaz) · Bosnian nominative
