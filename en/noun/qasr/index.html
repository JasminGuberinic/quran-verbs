What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقَصْرُ

    the palace (dvorac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقَصْرَ

    the palace (dvorac) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْقَصْرِ

    in the palace (u dvorcu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْقَصْرِ

    from the palace (iz dvorca) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ ٱلْقَصْرِ

    the door of the palace (vrata dvorca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قَصْرُهُ

    his/its palace (njegov dvorac) · Bosnian nominative
