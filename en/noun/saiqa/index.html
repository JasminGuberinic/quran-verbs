What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلصَّٰعِقَةُ

    the thunderbolt (grom) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الصَّاعِقَةَ

    the thunderbolt (grom) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الصَّاعِقَةِ

    from the thunderbolt (iz groma) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الصَّاعِقَةِ

    in the thunderbolt (u gromu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ الصَّاعِقَةِ

    the sound of the thunderbolt (zvuk groma) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    صاعِقَتُهُ

    his/its thunderbolt (njegov grom) · Bosnian nominative
