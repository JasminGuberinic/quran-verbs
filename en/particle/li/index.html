Particles · preposition · purpose · command

لِـfor, belongs to; so that; let

2848× in the Quran

What it does

It is written joined to the word after it. Before a noun it is a preposition (genitive). Before a present-tense verb it means ‘so that’ (subjunctive) or, as the lām of command, ‘let’ (jussive).

Uses

  1. ‘for, belongs to’: the preposition
  2. ‘so that’: purpose
  3. ‘let’: the command (lām al-amr)

‘for, belongs to’: the preposition

preposition · corpus: 2451×

Belonging, possession, purpose: ‘praise belongs to Allah’. The noun after it is genitive. With a pronoun: لَهُ ‘his, for him’.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘so that’: purpose

purpose · corpus: 319×

Before a verb it gives the purpose (lām at-taʿlīl, or lām kay); the verb is subjunctive. According to most grammarians (al-jumhūr), an implied أَنْ after it makes it so; most Kufans say the lām itself does.

The verb after it is subjunctive.

‘let’: the command (lām al-amr)

command · corpus: 78×

Before a present-tense verb it gives a command to a third person: ‘let…’; the verb is jussive. After وَ or فَ it is said without a vowel: فَلْيَعْبُدُوا.

The verb after it is jussive.
