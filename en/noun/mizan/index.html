What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمِيزانُ

    the scale (vaga) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمِيزَانَ

    the scale (vagu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمِيزَانِ

    in the scale (u vagi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمِيزَانِ

    from the scale (iz vage) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَاحِبُ ٱلْمِيزَانِ

    the owner of the scale (vlasnik vage) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its scales (njegove vage) · Bosnian nominative
