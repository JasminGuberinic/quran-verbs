Nuances

Father and parent

أَب · وَالِد

What they share

Both words can be rendered ‘father’; they differ in whether fatherhood in a wider sense or begetting is in view.

How to tell them apart

Father, also grandfather, forefather, even an uncle — the fathers as a tradition followed → أَب; the parent who begot, most often ‘both parents’ to be treated well → وَالِد.

أَب father, forefather

117× in the Quran · Noun page →

Father; according to ar-Rāġib, also anyone who is a cause of a thing's coming to be or its betterment; and alongside the father, a grandfather or even an uncle is counted among one's fathers (2:133). In the plural (آبَاء) often the ‘forefathers’ whose way people follow.

وَالِد parent, father

24× in the Quran · Noun page →

The one who begot — from the verb وَلَدَ, ‘to beget’. Most often in the dual, ‘both parents’, in the command to treat them well and in the laws of inheritance.
