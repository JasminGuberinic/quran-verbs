What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسَّيْلُ

    the torrent (bujica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّيْلَ

    the torrent (bujicu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّيْلِ

    in the torrent (u bujici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّيْلِ

    from the torrent (od bujice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ السَّيْلِ

    the strength of the torrent (snaga bujice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَيْلُهُ

    his/its torrent (njegova bujica) · Bosnian nominative
