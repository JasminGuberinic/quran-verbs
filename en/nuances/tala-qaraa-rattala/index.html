Nuances

Reciting, reading and measured recitation

تَلَا · قَرَأَ · رَتَّلَ

What they share

All three words concern voicing a written or revealed text. According to ar-Rāġib, تِلَاوَة is narrower than قِرَاءَة: every تِلَاوَة is a قِرَاءَة, but not every قِرَاءَة is a تِلَاوَة, while تَرْتِيل is a manner of voicing — with ease and evenness.

How to tell them apart

To recite Allah's revelation and follow it (root: to follow after) → تَلَا; to read, to voice letters and words joined together, any text → قَرَأَ; to voice with ease and evenness, word after word in order → رَتَّلَ.

تَلَا to recite; to follow

61× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root is to follow a thing with nothing between. تِلَاوَة, he says, belongs to Allah's revealed books — both reading them and keeping to what they command — so it is not said of an ordinary letter. In Quranic usage its object is almost always Allah's revelation.

قَرَأَ to read

16× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, reading is joining letters and words one to another in measured delivery; a single uttered letter is not reading. It is wider than تِلَاوَة: it is said of any text, and in Quranic usage even of the record of deeds on the Day of Judgement.

رَتَّلَ to intone evenly, in measure

2× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, the root means that a thing is even and orderly, and تَرْتِيل is sending the words out with ease and evenness. Only twice in the Quran, both times of the Quran.
