What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقَبْضَةُ

    the handful (šaka) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقَبْضَةَ

    the handful (šaku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْقَبْضَةِ

    in the handful (u šaci) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْقَبْضَةِ

    from the handful (iz šake) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَزْنُ الْقَبْضَةِ

    the weight of the handful (težina šake) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قَبْضَتُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its handful (njegova šaka) · Bosnian nominative
