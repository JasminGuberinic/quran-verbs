What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْإِمْلَاقُ

    the poverty (neimaština) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْإِمْلَاقَ

    the poverty (neimaštinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْإِمْلَاقِ

    from the poverty (od neimaštine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْإِمْلَاقِ

    in the poverty (u neimaštini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ الْإِمْلَاقِ

    the reason of the poverty (razlog neimaštine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    إِمْلَاقُهُ

    his/its poverty (njegova neimaština) · Bosnian nominative
