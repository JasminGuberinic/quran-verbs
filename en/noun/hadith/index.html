What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحَدِيثُ

    the story (priča) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحَدِيثَ

    the story (priču) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْحَدِيثِ

    in the story (u priči) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْحَدِيثِ

    from the story (iz priče) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نِهَايَةُ ٱلْحَدِيثِ

    the end of the story (kraj priče) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حَدِيثُهُ

    his/its story (njegova priča) · Bosnian nominative

Practice sentences
