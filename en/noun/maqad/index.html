What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَقْعَدُ

    the seat (sjedište) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَقْعَدَ

    the seat (sjedište) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْمَقْعَدِ

    with the seat (sjedištem) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَقْعَدِ

    in the seat (na sjedištu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    رَقْمُ الْمَقْعَدِ

    the number of the seat (broj sjedišta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَقْعَدُهُ

    his/its seat (njegovo sjedište) · Bosnian nominative
