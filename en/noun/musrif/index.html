What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمُسْرِفُ

    the spendthrift (rasipnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمُسْرِفَ

    the spendthrift (rasipnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمُسْرِفِ

    from the spendthrift (od rasipnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمُسْرِفِ

    in the spendthrift (u rasipniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مَالُ الْمُسْرِفِ

    the money of the spendthrift (novac rasipnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مُسْرِفُهُ

    his/its spendthrift (njegov rasipnik) · Bosnian nominative
