Nuances

Produce and fruit

ثَمَرَة · فَاكِهَة

What they share

Both words mean what is eaten from plants and trees. They differ in whether it is seen as yield and provision, or as fruit set out to eat, in the Quran mostly in Paradise.

How to tell them apart

What a tree yields, as produce and provision, and figuratively any benefit that comes from a thing → ثَمَرَة; fruit — the singular occurs eleven times, nine of them describing Paradise → فَاكِهَة.

ثَمَرَة produce, yield

22× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a name for all that is eaten of what trees yield. It is carried over to wealth (so Ibn ʿAbbās understood 18:34) and to any benefit that comes from a thing — ‘the fruit of knowledge is good deeds’. In the Quran produce is mostly provision (رِزْق) that Allah brings forth with rain.

فَاكِهَة fruit

11× in the Quran · Noun page →

Ar-Rāġib reports two views: that it is all fruits, or all except grapes and pomegranates, the second because these two are named apart beside it — in the Quran in 55:68. The singular ‘fruit’ occurs eleven times in the Quran, nine of them in the description of Paradise.
