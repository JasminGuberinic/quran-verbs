What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقَلَمُ

    the pen (pero) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقَلَمَ

    the pen (pero) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْقَلَمِ

    with the pen (perom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْقَلَمِ

    in the pen (na peru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلْقَلَمِ

    the color of the pen (boja pera) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَقْلَٰمَهُمْ from the Quran

    their pens (njihova pera) · Bosnian accusative
