Nuances

To hear and to listen

سَمِعَ · اِسْتَمَعَ · أَنْصَتَ

What they share

All three verbs concern taking in speech by hearing; they differ in how much attention and intent is in it.

How to tell them apart

To hear, and also to understand and accept → سَمِعَ; to listen attentively, on purpose → اِسْتَمَعَ; to fall silent in order to listen → أَنْصَتَ.

سَمِعَ to hear

78× in the Quran · Verb page →

The general verb for the sense of hearing. According to ar-Rāġib, ‘hearing’ is sometimes said for understanding and sometimes for obedience — hence the reproach of those who say they hear and do not heed.

اِسْتَمَعَ to listen

16× in the Quran · Verb page →

Form VIII of the same root: listening with attention and intent, turning one's hearing toward the speech.

أَنْصَتَ to listen in silence

2× in the Quran

In the classical dictionaries: to fall silent in order to listen. It occurs only twice in the Quran, both times with listening to the Quran.
