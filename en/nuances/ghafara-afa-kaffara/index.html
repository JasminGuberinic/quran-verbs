Nuances

To forgive

غَفَرَ · عَفَا · كَفَّرَ

What they share

All three verbs speak of forgiving sins; they differ in the image: covering and shielding, erasing and forgoing the penalty, or covering the sin as though it were not done.

How to tell them apart

To forgive and shield from punishment → غَفَرَ; to pardon, let pass unpunished — Allah's pardon, and often pardon between people (in retaliation, bridal due, offence) → عَفَا; to remove, cover over bad deeds → كَفَّرَ.

غَفَرَ to forgive

65× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root sense is covering with what shields (hence مِغْفَر, the helmet); Allah's forgiveness is shielding the servant from punishment. Said also of people who forgive.

عَفَا to pardon, to let pass

27× in the Quran · Verb page →

To pass over a sin, to forgo its penalty; tafsir links it to ‘effacing’ a trace. Often of people among themselves: pardon in retaliation, in the bridal due, in an offence.

كَفَّرَ to remove (sins), to expiate

14× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, covering a sin until it is as though it were not done. Its object is almost always ‘bad deeds’ (سَيِّئَات); from the same root is كَفَّارَة, expiation.

Sources

  • ar-Rāġib al-Iṣfahānī, Mufradāt alfāẓ al-Qurʾān — entries غفر, عفا, كفر
