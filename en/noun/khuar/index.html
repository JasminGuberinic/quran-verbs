What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخُوارُ

    the lowing (mukanje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخُوارَ

    the lowing (mukanje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخُوارِ

    in the lowing (u mukanju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْخُوارِ

    from the lowing (od mukanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ الْخُوارِ

    the sound of the lowing (zvuk mukanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خُوَارُهُ

    his/its lowing (njegovo mukanje) · Bosnian nominative
