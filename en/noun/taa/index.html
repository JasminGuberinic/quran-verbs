What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الطَّاعَةُ

    the obedience (poslušnost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الطَّاعَةَ

    the obedience (poslušnost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الطَّاعَةِ

    in the obedience (u poslušnosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الطَّاعَةِ

    from the obedience (od poslušnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قِيمَةُ الطَّاعَةِ

    the value of the obedience (vrijednost poslušnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طَاعَتُهُ

    his/its obedience (njegova poslušnost) · Bosnian nominative
