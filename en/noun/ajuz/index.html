What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَجُوزُ

    the old woman (starica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعَجُوزَ

    the old woman (staricu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعَجُوزِ

    in the old woman (u starici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعَجُوزِ

    from the old woman (od starice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَيْتُ الْعَجُوزِ

    the house of the old woman (kuća starice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَجُوزُهُ

    his/its old woman (njegova starica) · Bosnian nominative

    The old woman's house is small.
