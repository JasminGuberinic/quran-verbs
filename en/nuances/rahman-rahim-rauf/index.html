The Merciful and the Compassionate

رَحْمٰن · رَحِيم · رَءُوف

What they share

All three words speak of mercy; in the Quran they are above all attributes of Allah. They differ in how far the mercy reaches and to whom the word is applied.

How to tell them apart

Mercy that embraces all, a name for Allah alone → رَحْمٰن; merciful — said of Allah and also of a human being → رَحِيم; tender compassion, almost always beside رَحِيم → رَءُوف.

رَحْمٰن the Most Merciful

57× in the Quran

A name and attribute of Allah, never said of anyone else. According to ar-Rāġib this is because His mercy “embraceth all things” (7:156). In the Quran it also stands as a proper name, on a par with the name Allah.

رَحِيم merciful

116× in the Quran · Adjective page →

The most frequent of the three, usually at the close of an ayah with a name of Allah. Unlike رَحْمٰن it is said of creatures too: the Prophet is رَءُوف رَحِيم towards the believers.

رَءُوف kind, compassionate

11× in the Quran

ar-Rāġib glosses raʾfa simply as mercy (raḥma); tafsir describes it as an especially tender, intense compassion. In the Quran (our observation) it stands almost always beside رَحِيم and completes it.
