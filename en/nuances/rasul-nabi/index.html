Nuances

Messenger and prophet

رَسُول · نَبِيّ

What they share

Both words speak of those Allah sends to people. The Quran calls some prophets by both names (19:51), and in 22:52 names the two apart — so they differ.

How to tell them apart

One sent, the bearer of a message — a prophet or an angel → رَسُول; a prophet, one who brings news from Allah (and, for some, one raised high) → نَبِيّ.

رَسُول messenger

332× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, one who is sent; the word is said both of the message and of the one who bears it. Allah's messengers are sometimes angels and sometimes prophets.

نَبِيّ prophet

75× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, from نَبَأ — weighty news: the prophet informs from Allah and is himself informed; some scholars, he reports, derive it from ‘height’ (19:57). The Quran says it only of humans, never of angels.
