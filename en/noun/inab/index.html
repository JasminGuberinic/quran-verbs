What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعِنَبُ

    the grapes (grožđe) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعِنَبَ

    the grapes (grožđe) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعِنَبِ

    from the grapes (od grožđa) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعِنَبِ

    in the grapes (u grožđu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَعْمُ الْعِنَبِ

    the taste of the grapes (okus grožđa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عِنَبُهُ

    his/its grapes (njegovo grožđe) · Bosnian nominative
