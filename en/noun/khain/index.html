What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخائِنُ

    the traitor (izdajnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخائِنَ

    the traitor (izdajnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخائِنِ

    in the traitor (u izdajniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْخائِنِ

    from the traitor (od izdajnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الْخائِنِ

    the name of the traitor (ime izdajnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خائِنُهُ

    his/its traitor (njegov izdajnik) · Bosnian nominative
