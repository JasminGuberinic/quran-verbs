Particles · particle of the إِنَّ family

لَعَلَّperhaps; so that (perhaps)

129× in the Quran

What it does

Of the إِنَّ family: it puts the noun after it in the accusative, the predicate stays nominative. It expresses hope for, or fear of, something possible. In the Quran a pronoun most often follows it: لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ.

Uses

  1. ‘So that you may…’: hope
  2. The noun after it is accusative

‘So that you may…’: hope

particle of the إِنَّ family

Allah does not hope as a man hopes. Ibn Hišām gives two explanations: that the hope is on the side of those addressed, and, according to al-Aḫfaš and al-Kisāʾī, that لَعَلَّ means purpose here: ‘so that you may’.

The noun after it is accusative

particle of the إِنَّ family

When a noun rather than a pronoun follows لَعَلَّ, the accusative shows.

The word after it is accusative.
