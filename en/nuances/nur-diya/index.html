Nuances

Light

نُور · ضِيَاء

What they share

Both words mean ‘light’; the Quran sets them side by side in 10:5.

How to tell them apart

Light that spreads and helps sight, and figuratively guidance → نُور; radiance, the sun's light — for ar-Rāġib more specific than نُور (and Allah's scriptures too are ضِيَاء, 21:48) → ضِيَاء.

نُور light

43× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, spread light that helps seeing; in this world it is either seen with the eye or seen with insight — like the light of reason and of the Quran; there is also the light of the hereafter.

ضِيَاء radiance, illumination

3× in the Quran · Noun page →

Three times in the Quran. Tafsir on 10:5 often explains ḍiyāʾ as a stronger light than nūr — hence the sun is ḍiyāʾ and the moon nūr.

Sources

  • ar-Rāġib al-Iṣfahānī, Mufradāt alfāẓ al-Qurʾān — entries نور, ضوأ
  • Tafsir on 10:5 (the stronger light), unattributed
