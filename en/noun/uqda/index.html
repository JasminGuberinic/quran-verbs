What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعُقْدَةُ

    the knot (čvor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعُقْدَةَ

    the knot (čvor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعُقْدَةِ

    in the knot (na čvoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعُقْدَةِ

    from the knot (iz čvora) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    شَكْلُ الْعُقْدَةِ

    the shape of the knot (oblik čvora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عُقْدَتُهُ

    his/its knot (njegov čvor) · Bosnian nominative
