What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الطُّلُوعُ

    the rising (izlazak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الطُّلُوعَ

    the rising (izlazak) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الطُّلُوعِ

    in the rising (na izlasku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الطُّلُوعِ

    from the rising (od izlaska) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَقْتُ الطُّلُوعِ

    the time of the rising (vrijeme izlaska) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طُلُوعُهُ

    his/its rising (njegov izlazak) · Bosnian nominative
