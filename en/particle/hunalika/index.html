Particles · time adverb

هُنَالِكَthere; then

8× in the Quran

What it does

A demonstrative of place (‘there’), made of هُنَا, the lām of distance and the كَ of address, like ذَٰلِكَ. In the Quran it mostly points to a moment or a scene: ‘then, at that point’. It changes no case or mood. Ibn Hišām lists it among the demonstratives of the far, to which the هَا of attention is not added.

Uses

‘Then, there’

time adverb · corpus: 8×

It points to the scene just told: there, at that moment.
