What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسَّبْتُ

    the Saturday (subota) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    السَّبْتَ

    the Saturday (subotu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسَّبْتِ

    in the Saturday (u suboti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلسَّبْتِ

    from the Saturday (od subote) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    يَوْمُ ٱلسَّبْتِ

    the day of the Saturday (dan subote) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    of their Saturday (njihove subote) · Bosnian genitive
