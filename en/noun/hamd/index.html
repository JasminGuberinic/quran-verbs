What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْحَمْدُ

    the praise (pohvala) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحَمْدَ

    the praise (pohvalu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحَمْدِ

    in the praise (u pohvali) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحَمْدِ

    from the praise (od pohvale) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلِمَاتُ الْحَمْدِ

    the words of the praise (riječi pohvale) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حَمْدُهُ

    his/its praise (njegova pohvala) · Bosnian nominative

    Words of praise are beautiful.
