What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلطَّيْرُ

    the birds (ptice) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلطَّيْرَ

    the birds (ptice) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلطَّيْرِ

    to the birds (do ptica) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلطَّيْرِ

    from the birds (od ptica) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ ٱلطَّيْرِ

    the sound of the birds (glas ptica) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طَيْرُهُ

    his/its birds (njegove ptice) · Bosnian nominative
