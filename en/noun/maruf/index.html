What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَعْرُوفُ

    the kindness (dobro) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَعْرُوفَ

    the kindness (dobro) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمَعْرُوفِ

    from the kindness (od dobra) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمَعْرُوفِ

    in the kindness (u dobru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَهْلُ ٱلْمَعْرُوفِ

    the people of the kindness (ljudi dobra) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَعْرُوفُهُ

    his/its kindness (njegovo dobro) · Bosnian nominative
