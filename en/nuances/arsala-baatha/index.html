Nuances

To send

أَرْسَلَ · بَعَثَ

What they share

Both verbs mean ‘to send’ and both are said of sending messengers; they differ in what else each covers.

How to tell them apart

Letting a thing go forth — people and things alike (winds, rain, punishment) → أَرْسَلَ; rousing and dispatching, and raising from death or sleep → بَعَثَ.

أَرْسَلَ to send

130× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, sending is said both of people and of things, of what is welcome and what is hateful; sometimes by subjection, as with wind and rain, sometimes by sending one who has choice, as with messengers.

بَعَثَ to send, to raise

52× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root sense is to rouse a thing and direct it; the meaning shifts with what it is applied to. In the Quran it is used of sending a messenger, raising the dead and waking the people of the cave.
