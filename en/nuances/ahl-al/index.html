Nuances

Family and household

أَهْل · آل

What they share

Both words name a person's own, those who belong to him. According to ar-Rāġib, آل comes from أَهْل but is used more narrowly.

How to tell them apart

Family, household, but also inhabitants, followers, those who belong to something (People of the Book, people of the towns) → أَهْل; family, house or followers — in the Quran (our observation) always with a person's name (of Abraham, of Lot, of Pharaoh) → آل.

أَهْل family; people of; followers

127× in the Quran · Noun page →

The widest word: household and family, but also the inhabitants of a place, the people of a scripture or of a state (‘the people of the Fire’, أَهْلُ النَّارِ).

آل family, house; followers

26× in the Quran · Noun page →

The family or house of a known person, but also his followers and partisans. In the Quran always with a proper name: the family of Abraham and of Imran, but also Pharaoh's people.
