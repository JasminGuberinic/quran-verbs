What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَيْتُ

    the house (kuća) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَيْتَ

    the house (kuću) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْبَيْتِ

    in the house (u kući) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْبَيْتِ

    from the house (iz kuće) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ ٱلْبَيْتِ

    the door of the house (vrata kuće) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بُيُوتَكُمْ from the Quran

    your houses (vaše kuće) · Bosnian accusative
