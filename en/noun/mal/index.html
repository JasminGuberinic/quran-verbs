What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْمَالُ

    the wealth (imetak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمَالَ

    the wealth (imetak) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلْمَالِ

    with the wealth (imetkom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمَالِ

    from the wealth (od imetka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَاحِبُ ٱلْمَالِ

    the owner of the wealth (vlasnik imetka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ from the Quran

    their riches (njihove imetke) · Bosnian accusative

    صَاحِبُ الْمَالِ كَرِيمٌ

    The owner of the wealth is generous.
