Nuances

Weakness

وَهَنَ · ضَعِيف

What they share

Both words speak of weakness. According to ar-Rāġib, وَهْن is a weakness of the body or of character, while ضَعْف is the general opposite of strength — in soul, body or circumstances.

How to tell them apart

To weaken, to lose heart — to give way in body or spirit (forbidden to the believers: “Faint not”, 3:139) → وَهَنَ; weak, powerless — the opposite of strong → ضَعِيف.

وَهَنَ to weaken, to lose heart

5× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a weakness of body or of character. In the Quran: bones weakened by age (19:4) and a spirit that gives way in hardship — the believers are commanded not to lose heart.

ضَعِيف weak

8× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the opposite of strong — weakness of soul, body or circumstances. Man was “created weak” (4:28) because, he says, he has many needs; and Satan's plot is weak against Allah's servants.
