Nuances

The sea

بَحْر · يَمّ

What they share

Both words mean the sea, a great body of water. What tells them apart (our observation) is where the Quran uses them.

How to tell them apart

The sea in general — salt and fresh, the sea ships sail, the sea as an image of the boundless → بَحْر; the sea (or river) in the story of Moses and Pharaoh — in the Quran only there → يَمّ.

بَحْر sea

41× in the Quran · Noun page →

The general word for the sea and great waters: the sea ships sail, the sweet and salt ‘two seas’, the sea parted before Moses, the sea as a measure of the boundlessness of Allah's words.

يَمّ the sea, the river

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

The sea or a great water — in the Quran (our observation) only in the story of Moses: his mother set the infant upon it, and Pharaoh and his soldiers were drowned in it (tafsir: the Nile in 20:39 and 28:7, the sea in 28:40).
