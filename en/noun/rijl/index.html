What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الرِّجْلُ

    the leg (noga) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الرِّجْلَ

    the leg (nogu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالرِّجْلِ

    with the leg (nogom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الرِّجْلِ

    on the leg (na nozi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَلَمُ الرِّجْلِ

    the pain of the leg (bol noge) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَرْجُلِهِم from the Quran

    of their legs (njihovih nogu) · Bosnian genitive
