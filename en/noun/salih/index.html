What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلصَّٰلِحُ

    the righteous person (pravednik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الصَّالِحَ

    the righteous person (pravednika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الصَّالِحِ

    from the righteous person (od pravednika) · Bosnian genitive

    بِالصَّالِحِ

    the house of the righteous person (kuća pravednika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    صَٰلِحُهُ

    his/its righteous person (njegov pravednik) · Bosnian nominative
