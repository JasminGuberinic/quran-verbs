الَّذِينَwho (pl.)

1080× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The relative pronoun, masculine plural (also for a mixed group): ‘those who’. In the Quran it has the same form in every case. It is the Quran's most frequent relative word: ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ – ‘those who believe’.

Uses

‘Those who…’

relative pronoun · corpus: 1080×

The same form whether it describes a noun, follows a noun in annexation (the path of those who…), or comes after إِلَّا.
