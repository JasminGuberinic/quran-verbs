Particles · condition · negation · particle of the إِنَّ family

إِنْif; not

702× in the Quran

What it does

Two frequent roles. As a condition (‘if’) it puts present-tense verbs of the condition and the answer in the jussive. As a negation it means ‘not’, mostly with إِلَّا: إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا… ‘it is nothing but…’.

Uses

  1. ‘if’: condition
  2. ‘not’: negation
  3. The lightened إِنَّ: ‘indeed’

‘if’: condition

condition · corpus: 578×

A condition that may or may not be fulfilled. A present-tense verb after it is jussive.

The verb after it is jussive.

‘not’: negation

negation · corpus: 115×

It means the same as مَا ‘not’; with إِلَّا it restricts: ‘only’.

The lightened إِنَّ: ‘indeed’

particle of the إِنَّ family · corpus: 9×

إِنْ lightened from إِنَّ (al-muḫaffafa) affirms, and its predicate takes لَـ. According to Sībawayh and most grammarians it is the lightened إِنَّ, and لَـ is the lām of emphasis that also tells affirming from negating; the Kufans say إِنْ is a negation there and لَـ means إِلَّا (so: ‘we were not, except in error’). The sense is the same either way.
