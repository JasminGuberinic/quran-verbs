Nuances

Turning away and preventing

صَدَّ · مَنَعَ

What they share

Both words mean keeping someone from something. According to ar-Rāġib, صَدّ is either turning oneself away from a thing or turning another away, while مَنْع is the opposite of giving (withholding), and also means protection.

How to tell them apart

To turn away from, to turn others away from (a way, guidance) → صَدَّ; to prevent, to withhold, to protect → مَنَعَ.

صَدَّ to avert; to turn away

37× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, two ways: turning away from a thing and refusing it, or turning another away and preventing him. In the Quran it is most often turning people away from the way of Allah.

مَنَعَ to prevent, to withhold

12× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the opposite of giving — withholding a thing from someone — and also protecting. For “What hindered thee” said to Iblis (7:12) he gives ‘what led you’, and reports the view ‘what turned you away (صَدَّكَ)’.
