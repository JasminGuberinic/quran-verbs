What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الصُّنْعُ

    the making (izrada) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الصُّنْعَ

    the making (izradu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الصُّنْعِ

    in the making (u izradi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الصُّنْعِ

    from the making (od izrade) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَرِيقَةُ الصُّنْعِ

    the method of the making (način izrade) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    صُنْعُهُ

    his/its making (njegova izrada) · Bosnian nominative
