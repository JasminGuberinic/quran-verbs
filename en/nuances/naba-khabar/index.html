Nuances

News

نَبَأ · خَبَر

What they share

Both words mean news, a report of something. According to ar-Rāġib, نَبَأ is news of great benefit that brings knowledge or a strong probability.

How to tell them apart

Weighty news of great benefit — accounts of prophets, the unseen and the Last Day → نَبَأ; news, information in general, and what is known about someone → خَبَر.

نَبَأ news, tidings

29× in the Quran · Noun page →

News of weight and benefit. In the Quran most often of the prophets and peoples of old, of the unseen and of the Last Day. Many derive نَبِيّ, prophet, from the same root (others from nabwa, elevation).

خَبَر news, information

5× in the Quran · Noun page →

News, information in general; in the plural also what is known about someone (their deeds and state).
