Nuances

To form, to proportion, to determine

صَوَّرَ · سَوَّى · قَدَّرَ

What they share

All three words in the Quran most often describe Allah's work on what He has created, as befits Him: what a creature receives once created. They differ in what is given: a form, balance, or a measure.

How to tell them apart

To give the form by which a thing is told apart → صَوَّرَ; to make balanced, free of excess and lack → سَوَّى; to give the measure and manner by which a thing becomes what it is meant to be → قَدَّرَ.

صَوَّرَ to form, to give a form

4× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ṣūra is that by which a thing is recognised and set apart from others: a visible form (a man's shape) and a form grasped by the mind (reason and deliberation proper to man). When Allah says He formed people, ar-Rāġib says both are meant.

سَوَّى to proportion, to make balanced

13× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, taswiya is making a thing even, uniform; and sawiyy is what is kept from excess and lack in measure and manner. When Allah says He proportioned man, ar-Rāġib explains: He gave him a make such as wisdom requires.

قَدَّرَ to determine, to measure out

16× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, Allah's determining has two aspects: giving power, and giving a particular measure and manner as wisdom requires — as a date-stone is set to grow a palm, not an apple. Man too ‘measures out’: by reason, which is praised, or by wish and desire, which is blamed.
