What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النِّعْمَةُ

    the blessing (blagodat) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النِّعْمَةَ

    the blessing (blagodat) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ النِّعْمَةِ

    from the blessing (od blagodati) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي النِّعْمَةِ

    in the blessing (u blagodati) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    شُكْرُ النِّعْمَةِ

    the thanks of the blessing (zahvala blagodati) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نِعْمَتَهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its blessing (njegovu blagodat) · Bosnian accusative
