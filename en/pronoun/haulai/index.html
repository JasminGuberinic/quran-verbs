هَٰؤُلَاءِthese

46× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The demonstrative of the near, plural for both genders (هَا + أُولَاءِ). It does not change: in every case it ends in -i.

Uses

‘These …’

demonstrative · corpus: 46×

The same form as a subject and after a preposition or a word in the genitive.
