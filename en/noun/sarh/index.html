What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الصَّرْحُ

    the edifice (zdanje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلصَّرْحَ

    the edifice (zdanje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الصَّرْحِ

    in the edifice (u zdanju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الصَّرْحِ

    from the edifice (iz zdanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ الصَّرْحِ

    the door of the edifice (vrata zdanja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    صَرْحُهُ

    his/its edifice (njegovo zdanje) · Bosnian nominative
