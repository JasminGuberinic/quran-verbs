What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الظُّلَّةُ

    the shelter (nadstrešnica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الظُّلَّةَ

    the shelter (nadstrešnicu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلظُّلَّةِ

    in the shelter (na nadstrešnici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلظُّلَّةِ

    from the shelter (iz nadstrešnice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ظِلُّ ٱلظُّلَّةِ

    the shade of the shelter (hlad nadstrešnice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ظُلَّتُهُ

    his/its shelter (njegova nadstrešnica) · Bosnian nominative
