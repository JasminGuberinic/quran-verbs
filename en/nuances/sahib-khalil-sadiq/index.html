Nuances

Companion and friend

صَاحِب · خَلِيل · صَدِيق

What they share

All three words speak of someone close. They differ in the depth of the bond: from a companion to an intimate friend.

How to tell them apart

Companion, one a person is often with — even without love → صَاحِب; intimate friend, a love that pervades the soul → خَلِيل; true friend, one whose affection is sincere → صَدِيق.

صَاحِب companion

12× in the Quran · Noun page →

A companion one is often with — on the road, in prison, in a cave. It speaks of keeping company, not necessarily of love; the Quran reminds the deniers that the Prophet, ‘your companion’, is one they know well.

خَلِيل intimate friend

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

A friend of the closest love — according to ar-Rāġib, a love that pervades the soul (he records other derivations too, among them ‘need’). At the highest: Allah took Abraham as khalīl — an honour of the highest nearness, nothing like a human friendship; in regret: the sinner rues his bad friend.

صَدِيق true friend

2× in the Quran · Noun page →

A friend whose affection is true (the word is of the root of truth). Twice in the Quran: among those in whose houses one may eat, and as the friend the sinner will not have on the Last Day.
