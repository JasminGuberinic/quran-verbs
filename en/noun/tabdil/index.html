What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    التَّبْدِيلُ

    the replacement (zamjena) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    التَّبْدِيلَ

    the replacement (zamjenu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي التَّبْدِيلِ

    in the replacement (u zamjeni) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ التَّبْدِيلِ

    from the replacement (od zamjene) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ التَّبْدِيلِ

    the reason of the replacement (razlog zamjene) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    تَبْدِيلُهُ

    his/its replacement (njegova zamjena) · Bosnian nominative
