Love and affection

أَحَبَّ · وَدَّ · مَوَدَّة

What they share

All three words speak of love and attachment. According to ar-Rāġib, wudd is loving a thing and wishing it to be, so the word is used in both senses.

How to tell them apart

To love — people loving Allah, and Allah loving those who earn it → أَحَبَّ; to wish (the basic verb وَدَّ always means this in the Quran) → وَدَّ; affection, warmth between people → مَوَدَّة.

أَحَبَّ to love

64× in the Quran · Verb page →

The general word for love: of Allah, of wealth, of people. It is said too of Allah's love for those who do good and fear Him, as befits Him.

وَدَّ to wish

16× in the Quran · Verb page →

Though from the root of affection, the verb in the Quran (our observation) always means ‘to wish’, most often for what will not come true or would harm others.

مَوَدَّة affection

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

Warmth and affection between people — between spouses, friends, even towards an enemy. Allah names it as His sign and gift.
