Particles · preposition

فِيin, on; about; because of

1701× in the Quran

What it does

A preposition: the noun after it is in the genitive (a kasra at the end: ٱلْأَرْضِ, أَرْضٍ). A pronoun is joined to it as a suffix (فِيهِ ‘in it’).

See a noun's cases, the genitive after a preposition among them, on: جَنَّٰتٍ →

Uses

  1. ‘in’: place
  2. ‘in, within’: time
  3. ‘because of’: the cause

‘in’: place

preposition

That in which something is (aẓ-ẓarfiyya).

The noun after it is genitive.

‘in, within’: time

preposition

The time in which something happens.

The noun after it is genitive.

‘because of’: the cause

preposition

The cause (at-taʿlīl); Ibn Hišām gives this ayah among his examples: “for that whereof ye murmured” (24:14), that is, ‘because of’ it. It can also be read as ‘in the matter of’.

  • Ibn Hišām al-Anṣārī, Muġnī al-labīb ʿan kutub al-aʿārīb
