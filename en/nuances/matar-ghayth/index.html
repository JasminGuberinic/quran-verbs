Nuances

Rain

مَطَر · غَيْث

What they share

Both words mean ‘rain’; Quranic usage sets them apart.

How to tell them apart

Rain, in the Quran mostly a rain of punishment (stones on the people of Lot) → مَطَر; rain that helps and revives, relief after despair → غَيْث. A tendency, not a rule (4:102).

مَطَر rain

7× in the Quran · Noun page →

Rain. In the Quran it is mostly mentioned as punishment — the rain of stones on the people of Lot; but also as ordinary rain (4:102).

غَيْث rain (that relieves)

3× in the Quran · Noun page →

Rain that brings help and life: in 42:28 after despair, in 57:20 rain that makes plants grow, and in 31:34 it is Allah who sends it down.
