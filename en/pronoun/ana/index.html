أَنَاI

68× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The personal pronoun of the first person singular, masculine and feminine alike. In the Mushaf it is written with a final alif (أَنَا۠), but in Ḥafṣ's reading the alif is said only when one stops on the word; in connected reading it is ‘ana’ with a short a.

Uses

  1. Subject: ‘I…’
  2. The pronoun of separation (ḍamīr al-faṣl)

Subject: ‘I…’

personal pronoun

Often in the speech of the prophets and in Allah's speech about Himself, and in the speech of others too.

The pronoun of separation (ḍamīr al-faṣl)

personal pronoun

Between a subject and a definite predicate the pronoun says that what follows is the predicate, not an adjective or a follower – hence its name ‘separation’ (faṣl), and also ‘support’ (ʿimād). Ibn Hišām names two more benefits, emphasis and restriction, and az-Zamaḫšarī says all three on 2:5 – ‘they, and they alone’ (of هُمْ in 2:5). It can also stand between the two objects of a verb of thinking or seeing (18:39).
