What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحِمارُ

    the donkey (magarac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحِمارَ

    the donkey (magarca) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْحِمَارِ

    to the donkey (k magarcu) · Bosnian dative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْحِمَارِ

    in the donkey (u magarcu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَاحِبُ ٱلْحِمَارِ

    the owner of the donkey (vlasnik magarca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِمارُهُ

    his/its donkey (njegov magarac) · Bosnian nominative
