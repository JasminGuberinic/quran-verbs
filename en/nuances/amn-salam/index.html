Nuances

Security and peace

أَمْن · سَلَام

What they share

Both words speak of a state in which nothing threatens a person. They differ in what is at the centre: a soul at rest, without fear, or being spared every harm.

How to tell them apart

The soul at rest and fear gone — the opposite of fear → أَمْن; being free of harms, outward and inward, and the greeting that wishes it → سَلَام.

أَمْن security, safety

7× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root of أَمْن is the soul at rest and fear removed. In the Quran it stands against fear (4:83): security is the absence of fear. أَمَنَة in 3:154 he explains as أَمْن; there and in 8:11 it is security from Allah.

سَلَام peace; soundness; the greeting

42× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, سَلَام and سَلَامَة mean being free of harms, outward and inward; true, complete peace, he says, is only in Paradise, where there is lasting without passing away. Salām is also the greeting: from people by word, and from Allah, he says, by act — the giving of that peace of Paradise. As-Salām is also one of Allah's Beautiful Names (59:23); ar-Rāġib notes the view that ‘the abode of peace’ (6:127) is named after it.
