What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الثِّيَابُ

    the garments (odjeća) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الثِّيَابَ

    the garments (odjeću) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الثِّيَابِ

    in the garments (u odjeći) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الثِّيَابِ

    from the garments (iz odjeće) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ الثِّيَابِ

    the color of the garments (boja odjeće) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ثِيَابَهُمْ from the Quran

    their garments (njihovu odjeću) · Bosnian accusative

Practice sentences
