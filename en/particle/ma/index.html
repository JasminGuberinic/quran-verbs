مَاnot; what (that which); what?

2565× in the Quran

What it does

One word with many roles. As a particle it negates (مَا ضَلَّ ‘he has not strayed’), stops إِنَّ from working (إِنَّمَا ‘only’) and turns a verb into a verbal noun. As a noun it means ‘what (that which)’, ‘what?’ or ‘whatever’. Ibn Hišām divides it exactly so: mā the noun and mā the particle.

Uses

  1. ‘not’: negation
  2. ‘what, that which’: relative
  3. ‘what?’: the question
  4. إِنَّمَا ‘only’: it stops إِنَّ
  5. Making a verbal noun: ‘as’
  6. ‘whatever’: condition
  7. Between a preposition and its noun: emphasis
  8. With a condition: إِمَّا، أَيْنَمَا، إِذَا مَا

‘not’: negation

negation · corpus: 705×

It negates a past verb (مَا ضَلَّ) or a nominal sentence (مَا هُوَ ‘he is not’). When it negates a nominal sentence like لَيْسَ, the corpus puts it in the family of كَانَ; the predicate then often comes with بِـ.

‘what, that which’: relative

relative pronoun · corpus: 1475×

A noun: ‘what, that which’. It introduces the clause that describes it. The most frequent use of this word in the Quran, as the corpus counts it.

‘what?’: the question

question word · corpus: 95×

A noun asking ‘what?’. In the Quran it often asks to stress how great something is.

إِنَّمَا ‘only’: it stops إِنَّ

restraining particle · corpus: 162×

Joined to إِنَّ, مَا stops it from working (mā al-kāffa): the noun after it is no longer accusative. Together they mean ‘only, indeed only’.

The word after it is nominative.

Making a verbal noun: ‘as’

conjunction (verbal noun) · corpus: 83×

With the verb after it, it makes a whole that stands as a noun (mā al-maṣdariyya); often in كَمَا ‘as’.

‘whatever’: condition

condition · corpus: 22×

A noun of condition: ‘whatever’. The verbs of the condition and its answer are jussive.

The verb after it is jussive.

Between a preposition and its noun: emphasis

emphasis

A مَا added for emphasis (zāʾida): according to Ibn Hišām it does not cancel the governing, so the noun after the preposition is still genitive: فَبِمَا رَحْمَةٍ ‘it is by mercy’.

The noun after it is genitive.

With a condition: إِمَّا، أَيْنَمَا، إِذَا مَا

emphasis

The added مَا often stands with words of condition and stresses them: إِنْ + مَا = إِمَّا, أَيْنَ + مَا = أَيْنَمَا.
