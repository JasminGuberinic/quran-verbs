What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقَرْضُ

    the loan (zajam) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقَرْضَ

    the loan (zajam) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْقَرْضِ

    in the loan (u zajmu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْقَرْضِ

    from the loan (od zajma) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قِيمَةُ الْقَرْضِ

    the value of the loan (vrijednost zajma) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قَرْضُهُ

    his/its loan (njegov zajam) · Bosnian nominative
