Nuances

Admonishing and advising sincerely

وَعَظَ · نَصَحَ

What they share

Both words mean speech addressed to another for his good. They differ in tone and in what the word stresses: restraint with a warning, or sincere care for the good of the one addressed.

How to tell them apart

An admonition that restrains and warns (in the Quran mostly from Allah or a prophet) → وَعَظَ; sincere striving for another's good; the verb in the Quran always takes ‘for’ someone (نَصَحَ لِـ) → نَصَحَ.

وَعَظَ to admonish

15× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, admonition is restraint joined with a warning; he also quotes al-Ḫalīl: reminding of good in a way that softens the heart. In the Quran it is mostly Allah who admonishes, then the prophets, and Luqmān to his son.

نَصَحَ to advise sincerely

5× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, it is seeking out the deed or word that holds the good of the one advised; the root is either ‘pure, sincere love’ or ‘sewing’ — sincerity or firmness. In the Quran it is mostly a prophet's word to his people.
