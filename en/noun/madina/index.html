What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَدِينَةُ

    the city (grad) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمَدِينَةَ

    the city (grad) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمَدِينَةِ

    in the city (u gradu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْمَدِينَةِ

    to the city (do grada) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سُوقُ ٱلْمَدِينَةِ

    the market of the city (tržnica grada) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَدِينَتُهُ

    his/its city (njegov grad) · Bosnian nominative
