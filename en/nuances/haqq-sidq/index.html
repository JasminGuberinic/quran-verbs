Nuances

Truth and truthfulness

حَقّ · صِدْق

What they share

Both words speak of what is true. According to ar-Rāġib, the root of حَقّ is matching and agreement — what stands as it should; صِدْق, he says, is when a statement matches both what the speaker holds within and the reality it reports.

How to tell them apart

Truth itself — what exists and stands as it should (also Allah's name الْحَقّ) → حَقّ; the truthfulness of one who speaks or acts — word matching heart and reality → صِدْق.

حَقّ truth, the right

247× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what matches and stands as it should. It is said of Allah, who is الْحَقّ for He brings all things into being by wisdom; of what He creates, for His every act is truth; of a belief that matches reality; and of a word or deed that comes as, as much as, and when it should.

صِدْق truthfulness, sincerity

14× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, it is rooted in speech: a word is truthful when it matches both what the speaker holds and the reality. So the hypocrites (63:1) are liars though what they said is true. It is also said of a deed — when one fulfils what he pledged — and every virtuous act, outward and inward, is called صِدْق.
