What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الثَّمَنُ

    the price (cijena) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الثَّمَنَ

    the price (cijenu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الثَّمَنِ

    in the price (u cijeni) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الثَّمَنِ

    from the price (od cijene) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    ارْتِفَاعُ الثَّمَنِ

    the rise of the price (porast cijene) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ثَمَنُهُ

    his/its price (njegova cijena) · Bosnian nominative
