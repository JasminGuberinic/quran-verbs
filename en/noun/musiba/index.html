What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمُصِيبَةُ

    the misfortune (nesreća) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمُصِيبَةَ

    the misfortune (nesreću) · Bosnian accusative

    مِنَ الْمُصِيبَةِ

    from the misfortune (od nesreće) · Bosnian genitive

    فِي الْمُصِيبَةِ

    in the misfortune (u nesreći) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.
