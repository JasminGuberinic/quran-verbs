What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلتَّمَاثِيلُ

    the statues (kipovi) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    التَّمَاثِيلَ

    the statues (kipove) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي التَّمَاثِيلِ

    in the statues (na kipovima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ التَّمَاثِيلِ

    from the statues (od kipova) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    شَكْلُ التَّمَاثِيلِ

    the form of the statues (oblik kipova) · Bosnian genitive
