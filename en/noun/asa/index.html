What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَصَا

    the stick (štap) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعَصَا

    the stick (štap) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْعَصَا

    with the stick (štapom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعَصَا

    in the stick (na štapu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْعَصَا

    the length of the stick (dužina štapa) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَصَاهُ from the Quran

    his/its stick (njegov štap) · Bosnian accusative
