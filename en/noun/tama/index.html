What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الطَّمَعُ

    the greed (pohlepa) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الطَّمَعَ

    the greed (pohlepu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الطَّمَعِ

    in the greed (u pohlepi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الطَّمَعِ

    from the greed (od pohlepe) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سَبَبُ الطَّمَعِ

    the cause of the greed (uzrok pohlepe) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طَمَعُهُ

    his/its greed (njegova pohlepa) · Bosnian nominative
