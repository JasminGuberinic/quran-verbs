Particles · particle of the إِنَّ family

إِنَّindeed, truly

1682× in the Quran

What it does

It strengthens a nominal sentence. Its subject is accusative (إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ) and its predicate stays nominative. It has ‘sisters’ that work the same way: أَنَّ, كَأَنَّ, لَٰكِنَّ, لَيْتَ, لَعَلَّ. After the word ‘say’, when it introduces a quotation, it is إِنَّ, not أَنَّ.

Uses

  1. ‘indeed’: emphasis
  2. After ‘say’: the quotation
