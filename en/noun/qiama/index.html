What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْقِيامَةُ

    the resurrection (proživljenje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْقِيامَةَ

    the resurrection (proživljenje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ

    in the resurrection (u proživljenju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ

    from the resurrection (od proživljenja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    يَوْمُ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ

    the day of the resurrection (dan proživljenja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    قِيامَتُهُ

    his/its resurrection (njegovo proživljenje) · Bosnian nominative
