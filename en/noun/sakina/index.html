What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّكِينَةُ

    the tranquillity (smirenost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسَّكِينَةَ

    the tranquillity (smirenost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّكِينَةِ

    in the tranquillity (u smirenosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّكِينَةِ

    from the tranquillity (od smirenosti) · Bosnian genitive
