What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الطَّرِيقَةُ

    the method (način) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الطَّرِيقَةَ

    the method (način) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلطَّرِيقَةِ

    with the method (načinom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلطَّرِيقَةِ

    on the method (na načinu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سُهُولَةُ ٱلطَّرِيقَةِ

    the ease of the method (lakoća načina) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    طَرِيقَتُهُ

    his/its method (njegov način) · Bosnian nominative
