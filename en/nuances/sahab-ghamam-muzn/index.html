Nuances

Clouds

سَحَاب · غَمَام · مُزْن

What they share

All three words mean cloud. They differ in what the name brings out and in the part the cloud plays in the ayat.

How to tell them apart

The cloud drawn and driven by the winds, usually rain-bearing → سَحَاب; the cloud that covers and shades (from the root ‘to cover’) → غَمَام; the rain cloud — once, as the source of sweet water → مُزْن.

سَحَاب clouds

9× in the Quran · Noun page →

Clouds driven and gathered by the winds, from which rain falls. In the Quran (our observation) often with winds and rain, as a sign of Allah's power and mercy, but also as an image of passing (27:88) and of darkness (24:40).

غَمَام cloud (that shades)

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

The cloud that covers and screens. In the Quran (our observation) never as a rain cloud: the shade Allah gave the Children of Israel, and the clouds of the Last Day.

مُزْن rain clouds

1× in the Quran

Rain clouds; once in the Quran, in a question: who sends down the drinking water from them?
