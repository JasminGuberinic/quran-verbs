What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكاتِبُ

    the writer (pisac) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكاتِبَ

    the writer (pisca) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْكاتِبِ

    in the writer (u piscu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْكاتِبِ

    from the writer (od pisca) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَلَمُ الْكاتِبِ

    the pen of the writer (olovka pisca) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَاتِبُهُ

    his/its writer (njegov pisac) · Bosnian nominative
