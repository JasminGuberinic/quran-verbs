Nuances

Promise and threat

وَعْد · وَعِيد

What they share

Both words come from one root and mean announcing to someone what awaits him. They differ in what is announced.

How to tell them apart

Announcing good or evil → وَعْد; announcing evil only, a threat → وَعِيد.

وَعْد promise

49× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, waʿd is said of good and of evil. Mostly it is a promise of good, but the punishment the deniers hasten is also Allah's waʿd (22:47); and ‘Allah's promise is true’ holds both: the Rising and the requital — good for good, evil for evil.

وَعِيد threat

6× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, waʿīd is for evil only. In the Quran it comes 6 times and is always Allah's threat; three times as ‘My threat’.
