Particles · setting out

أَمَّاas for…

55× in the Quran

What it does

A word of setting out: ‘as for…’. What follows as its answer is regularly bound with فَـ. Sībawayh explains it as ‘whatever be the case, (then)…’ (مَهْمَا يَكُن مِن شَىْءٍ).

Uses

‘As for…, (then)…’

setting out · corpus: 55×

It often comes in pairs: فَأَمَّا… وَأَمَّا… It changes no case itself; the word after it has the case the rest of the sentence gives it.
