Making clear and setting out in detail

بَيَّنَ · فَصَّلَ

What they share

Both verbs mean making a thing understood. According to ar-Rāġib, بَيَان is uncovering a thing, and فَصْل is separating one thing from another so that a gap stays between them — and he ties the Book's تَفْصِيل to its being an explanation of all things.

How to tell them apart

To make clear, to uncover what was hidden or unclear — Allah, a messenger, and people of learning → بَيَّنَ; to set out in detail, separating part from part — in the Quran always Allah's verses, the Book, what is forbidden → فَصَّلَ.

بَيَّنَ to make clear

35× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, giving a thing an explanation that uncovers it; بَيَان is disclosure, wider than speech. In the Quran Allah makes clear (the verses), a messenger makes clear (the revelation to people), and so must the People of the Book, who were told to make it clear and not conceal it.

فَصَّلَ to set out in detail

17× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, فَصْل is separating two things so that a gap stays between them; the Book's تَفْصِيل he ties to its being an explanation of all things. In Quranic usage the one who sets out in detail is always Allah, and what is set out is the verses, the Book, what is forbidden, ‘everything’.
