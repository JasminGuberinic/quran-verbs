What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الزَّوْجُ

    the spouse (supružnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الزَّوْجَ

    the spouse (supružnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الزَّوْجِ

    from the spouse (od supružnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الزَّوْجِ

    on the spouse (na supružniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الزَّوْجِ

    the name of the spouse (ime supružnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    زَوْجَهَا from the Quran

    her/its spouse (njenog supružnika) · Bosnian accusative
