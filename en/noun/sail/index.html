What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السَّائِلُ

    the beggar (prosjak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسَّآئِلَ

    the beggar (prosjaka) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السَّائِلِ

    in the beggar (u prosjaku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السَّائِلِ

    from the beggar (od prosjaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طَلَبُ السَّائِلِ

    the request of the beggar (molba prosjaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سَآئِلُهُ

    his/its beggar (njegov prosjak) · Bosnian nominative
