What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْمُنَٰفِقُونَ

    the hypocrites (licemjeri) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمُنَٰفِقِينَ

    the hypocrites (licemjere) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمُنَٰفِقِينَ

    in the hypocrites (u licemjerima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمُنَٰفِقِينَ

    from the hypocrites (od licemjera) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلَامُ ٱلْمُنَٰفِقِينَ

    the speech of the hypocrites (govor licemjera) · Bosnian genitive
