What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْمَثَلُ

    the example (primjer) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَثَلَ

    the example (primjer) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِالْمَثَلِ

    with the example (primjerom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَثَلِ

    in the example (u primjeru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَاتِبُ الْمَثَلِ

    the writer of the example (pisac primjera) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَثَلُهُمْ from the Quran

    their example (njihov primjer) · Bosnian nominative
