What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخَلِيلُ

    the friend (prijatelj) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَلِيلَ

    the friend (prijatelja) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخَلِيلِ

    in the friend (u prijatelju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْخَلِيلِ

    from the friend (od prijatelja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَيْتُ الْخَلِيلِ

    the house of the friend (kuća prijatelja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَلِيلُهُ

    his/its friend (njegov prijatelj) · Bosnian nominative
