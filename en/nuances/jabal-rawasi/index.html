Nuances

Mountains and firm ones

جَبَل · رَوَاسِي

What they share

Both words speak of mountains. جَبَل is the plain name of the mountain; رَوَاسِي names them by one quality — that they are firmly set.

How to tell them apart

The mountain as such, also in images of firmness and greatness → جَبَل; what is firmly set, the mountains as the earth's anchors → رَوَاسِي.

جَبَل mountain

39× in the Quran · Noun page →

The general word for mountain. Ar-Rāġib says images of firmness (‘a mountain that does not budge’), of innate nature and of greatness are drawn from it. In the Quran this word also describes how the mountains move and crumble on the Last Day.

رَوَاسِي firm ones (firmly set mountains)

10× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, from rasā, to stand firm; رَوَاسِي are firmly set mountains. In the Quran the word comes nine times, always with the earth in which Allah has placed them, three of them with “that it quake not with you” (16:15); the same root also gives “boilers built into the ground” (رَاسِيَات, 34:13).
