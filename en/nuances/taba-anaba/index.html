Nuances

To repent and to turn back

تَابَ · أَنَابَ

What they share

Both verbs mean returning to Allah; they differ in whether leaving the sin or a constant turning to Allah in heart and deed is in view.

How to tell them apart

To repent, leave the sin — and Allah's accepting it (تَابَ عَلَى) → تَابَ; to turn to Allah, return to Him sincerely, even with no sin in view → أَنَابَ.

تَابَ to repent; to accept repentance

63× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, leaving a sin in the finest way. The same verb is said of Allah — تَابَ عَلَيْهِ: He accepted his repentance, turned to him in mercy.

أَنَابَ to turn (to Allah)

11× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, returning to Allah with repentance and sincere deeds. Said also with no sin in view — as a constant turning to Allah; never of Allah (our observation).
