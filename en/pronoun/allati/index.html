الَّتِيwho, which (f.)

68× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The relative pronoun, feminine singular. As with الَّذِي, a sentence follows it with a pronoun returning to it; that pronoun may be left out when it is an object (نِعْمَتِىَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنْعَمْتُ – ‘My favour which I bestowed’).
