What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    السِّرُّ

    the secret (tajna) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسِّرَّ

    the secret (tajnu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي السِّرِّ

    in the secret (u tajni) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ السِّرِّ

    from the secret (od tajne) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَشْفُ السِّرِّ

    the disclosure of the secret (otkrivanje tajne) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سِرَّهُمْ from the Quran

    their secret (njihovu tajnu) · Bosnian accusative

Practice sentences
