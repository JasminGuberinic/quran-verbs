Nuances

Cup, goblets, pitchers

كَأْس · أَكْوَاب · أَبَارِيق

What they share

All three words are vessels for drink, and all three come in the Quran in the description of Paradise. Ar-Rāġib's separate definitions differ by the vessel's shape and by whether drink is in it.

How to tell them apart

The vessel with drink in it, and also the drink itself → كَأْس; a cup with no handle → أَكْوَاب; a pitcher, of which ar-Rāġib says only that it is well known → أَبَارِيق.

كَأْس cup (with drink)

6× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the vessel together with the drink in it; and each of the two alone is also called كَأْس, so one says ‘I drank a cup’, meaning the drink.

أَكْوَاب goblets (handleless cups)

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a كُوب is a cup without a handle; أَكْوَاب is its plural. In the Quran all four times in the description of Paradise.

أَبَارِيق pitchers

1× in the Quran

Ar-Rāġib mentions it under the root of lightning (برق) and says only that the pitcher is well known. In the Quran once, beside the goblets and the cup.
