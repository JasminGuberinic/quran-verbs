What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الذِّراعُ

    the forearm (podlaktica) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الذِّراعَ

    the forearm (podlakticu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الذِّراعِ

    in the forearm (na podlaktici) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الذِّراعِ

    from the forearm (od podlaktice) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الذِّراعِ

    the length of the forearm (dužina podlaktice) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ذِراعُهُ

    his/its forearm (njegova podlaktica) · Bosnian nominative
