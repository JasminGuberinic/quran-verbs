What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَطْنُ

    the belly (stomak) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَطْنَ

    the belly (stomak) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَطْنِ

    in the belly (u stomaku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَطْنِ

    from the belly (iz stomaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَلَمُ الْبَطْنِ

    the pain of the belly (bol stomaka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَطْنُهُ

    his/its belly (njegov stomak) · Bosnian nominative
