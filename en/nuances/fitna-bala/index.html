Nuances

Trial

فِتْنَة · بَلَاء · ابْتَلَى

What they share

All three words speak of a trial by which a person is tested. The Quran says Allah tests with both evil and good (21:35) — not to learn, for He knows all, but to bring out what is in a person.

How to tell them apart

A trial that shows what is in a person — and temptation, discord, persecution → فِتْنَة; a test, a trial — by hardship and by blessing → بَلَاء; to test, to put to trial (the verb) → ابْتَلَى.

فِتْنَة trial; temptation, discord

34× in the Quran · Noun page →

A trial that separates good from bad — according to ar-Rāġib, from putting gold into the fire. The word covers the lure of wealth and children, discord among people, persecution for the faith, and punishment itself (51:14).

بَلَاء test, trial

6× in the Quran · Noun page →

A test that proves a person — by hardship and by blessing. Abraham's command to sacrifice his son is “a clear test” (37:106).

ابْتَلَى to test, to try

8× in the Quran · Verb page →

To put to the test; Allah tests with honour and with want, and a person should take neither as the measure of his worth.
