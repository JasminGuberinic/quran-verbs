What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الرَّحْلُ

    the baggage (prtljag) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الرَّحْلَ

    the baggage (prtljag) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الرَّحْلِ

    in the baggage (u prtljagu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الرَّحْلِ

    from the baggage (iz prtljaga) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    وَزْنُ الرَّحْلِ

    the weight of the baggage (težina prtljaga) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    رَحْلُهُ

    his/its baggage (njegov prtljag) · Bosnian nominative
