What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْاِتِّباعُ

    the following (slijeđenje) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْاِتِّباعَ

    the following (slijeđenje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْاِتِّباعِ

    in the following (u slijeđenju) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْاِتِّباعِ

    from the following (od slijeđenja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَهَمِّيَّةُ الْاِتِّباعِ

    the importance of the following (važnost slijeđenja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ٱتِّبَاعُهُ

    his/its following (njegovo slijeđenje) · Bosnian nominative
