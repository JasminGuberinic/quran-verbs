لَمَّاwhen; not yet; (but)

165× in the Quran

What it does

Three different words of one form. Before a past verb it means ‘when’ (one thing happened because another did). Before a present form it is the negation ‘not yet’ and puts the verb in the jussive, like لَمْ. In a few ayat it means ‘but, only’.

Uses

  1. ‘When…’
  2. ‘Not yet’
  3. ‘But, only’ (86:4)

‘When…’

time adverb · corpus: 154×

According to Sībawayh this لَمَّا is a word saying that one thing happened because another did; Ibn as-Sarrāj and al-Fārisī hold it is an adverb, ‘when’, and the corpus files it as an adverb of time. Ibn Hišām gives both views.

‘Not yet’

negation · corpus: 8×

Like لَمْ it puts the verb in the jussive and negates the past, but according to Ibn Hišām the negation lasts up to the moment of speaking and what is negated is expected to happen.

The verb after it is jussive.

‘But, only’ (86:4)

exception · corpus: 3×

After the negating إِنْ, لَمَّا means إِلَّا: “No human soul but hath a guardian over it” (86:4). So it is in Ḥafṣ's reading (لَمَّا with a shadda); some other readers read لَمَا, with إِنْ lightened from إِنَّ.

Sources

  • Ibn Hišām al-Anṣārī, Muġnī al-labīb ʿan kutub al-aʿārīb
  • Sībawayh, al-Kitāb
