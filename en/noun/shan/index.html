What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الشَّأْنُ

    the matter (stvar) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الشَّأْنَ

    the matter (stvar) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الشَّأْنِ

    in the matter (u stvari) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الشَّأْنِ

    from the matter (od stvari) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَهَمِّيَّةُ الشَّأْنِ

    the importance of the matter (važnost stvari) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    شَأْنِهِمْ from the Quran

    of their matter (njihove stvari) · Bosnian genitive
