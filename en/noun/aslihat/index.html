What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَسْلِحَةُ

    the weapons (oružja) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَسْلِحَةَ

    the weapons (oružja) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْأَسْلِحَةِ

    in the weapons (u oružjima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَسْلِحَةِ

    from the weapons (od oružja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ الْأَسْلِحَةِ

    the sound of the weapons (zvuk oružja) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَسْلِحَتَهُمْ from the Quran

    their weapons (njihova oružja) · Bosnian accusative
