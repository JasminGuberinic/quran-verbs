Nuances

The poor and the needy

فَقِير · مِسْكِين

What they share

Both words denote a person in want and both are among the recipients of zakat (9:60); scholars differ over which of the two is in greater need.

How to tell them apart

The poor, one in need — and man in his need of Allah → فَقِير; the needy, most often named with the orphan and traveller as one to be fed → مِسْكِين; which is worse off, scholars differ.

فَقِير poor, in need

12× in the Quran · Noun page →

One in need, who lacks something. Said too of man's need of Allah: all people are ‘poor’ before Allah, and Moses called himself فَقِير of whatever good Allah sends down.

مِسْكِين needy, destitute

23× in the Quran · Noun page →

One in want; often named with the orphan and the traveller as one to be fed. Scholars differ on whether he is in greater need than the فَقِير.
