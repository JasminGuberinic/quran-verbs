Nuances

Recompense and reward

جَزَاء · أَجْر · ثَوَاب

What they share

All three words mean what one receives for one's deed; they differ in whether it is requital for good and evil alike, a wage for work, or what returns to the doer.

How to tell them apart

Requital for a deed, good or evil → جَزَاء; a wage, reward for effort, only for good → أَجْر; what returns to a person for his deed, as a noun in the Quran always a good reward → ثَوَاب.

جَزَاء recompense, requital

42× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, requital: good for good, evil for evil. So it is said of reward and of punishment alike, and stands where the deed is the measure of what is returned.

أَجْر reward, wage

105× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what one receives for a deed, in this world or the next, as under a contract — and only benefit, never harm. Hence also an ordinary wage, and the bridal due.

ثَوَاب reward

13× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what ‘returns’ to a person for his deeds (the root means return). He says it is used of good and evil but mostly of good; in the Quran the noun is always a reward (our observation).
