Particles · future

سَوْفَwill

42× in the Quran

What it does

A word of the future like سَـ, but standing alone: سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ ‘you will know’. The verb after it stays indicative. It often has the لَـ of emphasis: وَلَسَوْفَ.

Uses

The future

future · corpus: 42×

The Basran grammarians held that the future with سَوْفَ is further off than with سَـ; Ibn Hišām does not accept this (see سَـ).

The verb after it stays indicative.
