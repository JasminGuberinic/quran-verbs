What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْجَوابُ

    the answer (odgovor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْجَوابَ

    the answer (odgovor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْجَوابِ

    in the answer (u odgovoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْجَوابِ

    from the answer (iz odgovora) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سُرْعَةُ الْجَوابِ

    the speed of the answer (brzina odgovora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    جَوابُهُ

    his/its answer (njegov odgovor) · Bosnian nominative
