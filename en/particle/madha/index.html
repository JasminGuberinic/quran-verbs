Particles · question word

مَاذَاwhat?

26× in the Quran

What it does

A question word, ‘what?’, from the مَا of asking and ذَا. Ibn Hišām lists several ways to read it: as one question word, or as مَا ‘what’ and ذَا ‘that which’ (‘what is it that…?’). It changes no case or mood in the word after it; like the مَا of asking it comes first.

Uses

Asking ‘what?’

question word · corpus: 26×

Ibn Hišām shows the difference on 2:219: for those who read the answer ٱلْعَفْوُ in the nominative (Abū ʿAmr), ذَا as ‘that which’ is better – ‘what is the thing they spend? – it is the surplus’; for those who read ٱلْعَفْوَ in the accusative (the others, Ḥafṣ among them), مَاذَا as one word, an object, is better: ‘what are they to spend? – [let them spend] the surplus’. The answer matches the question.
