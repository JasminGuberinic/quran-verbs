What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحِزْبُ

    the party (stranka) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحِزْبَ

    the party (stranku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحِزْبِ

    from the party (od stranke) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحِزْبِ

    in the party (u stranci) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    رَئِيسُ الْحِزْبِ

    the head of the party (predsjednik stranke) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حِزْبَهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its party (njegovu stranku) · Bosnian accusative
