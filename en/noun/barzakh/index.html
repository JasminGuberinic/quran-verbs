What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَرْزَخُ

    the isthmus (prevlaka) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَرْزَخَ

    the isthmus (prevlaku) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَرْزَخِ

    in the isthmus (na prevlaci) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَرْزَخِ

    from the isthmus (od prevlake) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    طُولُ الْبَرْزَخِ

    the length of the isthmus (dužina prevlake) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَرْزَخُهُ

    his/its isthmus (njegova prevlaka) · Bosnian nominative
