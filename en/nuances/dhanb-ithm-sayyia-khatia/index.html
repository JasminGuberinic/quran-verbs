Nuances

Sin

ذَنْب · إِثْم · سَيِّئَة · خَطِيئَة

What they share

All four words name a wrong deed; they differ in the image each root carries.

How to tell them apart

A deed whose consequence is grievous (the deed's ‘tail’) → ذَنْب; a deed that holds back from reward → إِثْم; an ugly deed, the opposite of the good one (حَسَنَة) → سَيِّئَة; a missing of the mark, a turning from the course → خَطِيئَة.

ذَنْب sin, misdeed

37× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, from ḏanab — the tail, a thing's end: any deed whose consequence (end) is grievous. In the Quran mostly with seeking forgiveness.

إِثْم sin

35× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, a name for deeds that slow one (hold one back) from reward. Often with what is harmful and forbidden: wine and gambling, cooperation in aggression, suspicion.

سَيِّئَة evil deed

58× in the Quran · Noun page →

An ugly deed, the opposite of the good deed (حَسَنَة); the Quran often names the two together. According to ar-Rāġib, the root sūʾ is whatever grieves a person.

خَطِيئَة sin, fault

10× in the Quran · Noun page →

From the root ḵ-ṭ-ʾ; according to ar-Rāġib, a turning from the right direction; he says خَطِيئَة is mostly said of a wrong not itself intended, but arising from something that was. In the Quran it is said of the sin that encompasses a person (2:81) and in Abraham's humble hope of forgiveness (26:82).
