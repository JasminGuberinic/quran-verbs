Nuances

Ship and vessel

فُلْك · سَفِينَة

What they share

Both words mean a craft on the water. They differ in the origin ar-Rāġib gives for the word (only for safīna) and in how often the Quran uses them.

How to tell them apart

A ship, said of one or many, the general word for sailing and for Noah's ark → فُلْك; a vessel, named after scraping a surface, in the Quran in the story of Moses and al-Khidr and of Noah's people → سَفِينَة.

فُلْك ship, ships

23× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the ship — one word for one and for many. In the Quran it is the general word for sailing (2:164, 17:66) and for Noah's ark (11:37). The word فَلَك, the course of the stars, is in his view named after its likeness to it.

سَفِينَة vessel

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, named after safn — scraping, planing the surface of a thing; then any easy mount is likened to it. In the Quran only in the story of Moses and the man of knowledge (18:71, 18:79) and of Noah's people (29:15).
