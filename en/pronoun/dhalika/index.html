Pronouns · demonstrative

ذَٰلِكَthat

479× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The demonstrative of the far, masculine singular: ذَا + the lām of distance + the كَ of address. Ibn Hišām says this كَ is a particle of address with no place in the sentence; so it changes with who is addressed (ذَٰلِكُمْ ‘that, to you all’). With the كَـ of likeness it gives كَذَٰلِكَ – ‘thus’. The feminine is تِلْكَ, the plural أُولَٰئِكَ.

Uses

‘That …’, ‘that is …’ and كَذَٰلِكَ ‘thus’

demonstrative · corpus: 479×

It points to something mentioned or present; translators sometimes render it ‘this’ (2:2).
