What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْكَلامُ

    the speech (govor) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْكَلامَ

    the speech (govor) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْكَلامِ

    in the speech (u govoru) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْكَلامِ

    from the speech (od govora) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مَعْنَى الْكَلامِ

    the meaning of the speech (značenje govora) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    كَلامُهُ

    his/its speech (njegov govor) · Bosnian nominative
