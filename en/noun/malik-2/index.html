What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمالِكُ

    the owner (vlasnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمالِكَ

    the owner (vlasnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمالِكِ

    in the owner (u vlasniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمالِكِ

    from the owner (od vlasnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ الْمالِكِ

    the name of the owner (ime vlasnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مالِكُهُ

    his/its owner (njegov vlasnik) · Bosnian nominative
