What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَضَاجِعُ

    the beds (ležaji) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَضَاجِعَ

    the beds (ležaje) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ

    to the beds (do ležaja) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ

    in the beds (na ležajima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عَدَدُ ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ

    the number of the beds (broj ležaja) · Bosnian genitive
