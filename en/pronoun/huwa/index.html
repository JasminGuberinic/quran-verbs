Pronouns · personal pronoun

هُوَhe

481× in the Quran

What it is and what it points to

The personal pronoun of the third person, masculine singular, a word of its own (not a suffix). It points to someone or something masculine, and to Allah. It stands as a subject, and between a subject and its predicate as the pronoun of separation.

Uses

  1. Subject: ‘He is…’
  2. The pronoun of separation (ḍamīr al-faṣl)

Subject: ‘He is…’

personal pronoun

As the subject of a nominal sentence, or before a verb when who does the deed is stressed.

The pronoun of separation (ḍamīr al-faṣl)

personal pronoun

Between a subject and a definite predicate the pronoun says that what follows is the predicate, not an adjective or a follower – hence its name ‘separation’ (faṣl), and also ‘support’ (ʿimād). Ibn Hišām names two more benefits, emphasis and restriction, and az-Zamaḫšarī says all three on 2:5 – ‘they, and they alone’ (of هُمْ in 2:5).
