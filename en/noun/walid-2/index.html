What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْوَلِيدُ

    the child (dijete) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْوَلِيدَ

    the child (dijete) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْوَلِيدِ

    from the child (od djeteta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْوَلِيدِ

    in the child (u djetetu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ الْوَلِيدِ

    the voice of the child (glas djeteta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    وَلِيدُهُ

    his/its child (njegovo dijete) · Bosnian nominative
