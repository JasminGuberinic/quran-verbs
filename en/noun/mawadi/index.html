What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَوَاضِعُ

    the places (mjesta) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْمَوَاضِعَ

    the places (mjesta) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْمَوَاضِعِ

    in the places (na mjestima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْمَوَاضِعِ

    from the places (iz mjesta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    عَدَدُ الْمَوَاضِعِ

    the number of the places (broj mjesta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَوَاضِعِهِۦ from the Quran

    of his/its places (njegovih mjesta) · Bosnian genitive
