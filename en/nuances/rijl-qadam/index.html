Nuances

Leg and foot

رِجْل · قَدَم

What they share

Both words name the lower limb one walks and stands on. They differ in what the Quran brings out with them: the leg as the limb one walks with, which is washed, cut off and bears witness, or the foot as the footing one stands firm on or slips from.

How to tell them apart

The leg as a limb — walking, washing, penalty, witness → رِجْل; the foot as footing — ‘make our feet firm’, ‘lest a foot slip’ → قَدَم.

رِجْل leg

15× in the Quran · Noun page →

Ar-Rāġib describes rijl as the limb proper to most animals; from it comes rājil, one who goes on foot. In the Quran the leg is the limb in motion and action: legs one walks on, which are washed in ablution, cut off as a penalty, and bear witness on the Day of Judgement.

قَدَم foot

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

Ar-Rāġib explains qadam by the word rijl, and by the foot, he says, being ahead and behind (taqaddum) is reckoned; qadam ṣidq (10:2) is, for him, ‘a merit gained beforehand’. In the Quran the foot is mostly footing: Allah makes feet firm (as befits Him), or a foot slips.
