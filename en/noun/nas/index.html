What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلنَّاسُ

    the people (ljudi) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلنَّاسَ

    the people (ljude) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ

    from the people (od ljudi) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ

    on the people (na ljudima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَلَامُ ٱلنَّاسِ

    the speech of the people (govor ljudi) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ناسُهُ

    his/its people (njegovi ljudi) · Bosnian nominative
