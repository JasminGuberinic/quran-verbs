Nuances

Good and the fair

خَيْر · حَسَنَة

What they share

Both words mean good. According to ar-Rāġib, خَيْر is what everyone desires, its opposite evil (شَرّ); حَسَنَة is whatever gladdens — a blessing that reaches a person — and a good deed, its opposite سَيِّئَة.

How to tell them apart

The good all desire, also wealth, and ‘better than’ → خَيْر; what gladdens, good fortune that reaches a person, and a good deed against an evil one → حَسَنَة.

خَيْر good

178× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what everyone desires — reason, justice, virtue, what benefits; its opposite is evil. It is said also of wealth (2:180) and in the sense of ‘better than’. He also reports the view that some good is good in every respect, and some only for one person, like wealth.

حَسَنَة good deed; good fortune

31× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, whatever gladdens of a blessing that reaches a person — in himself, his body and his circumstances; its opposite is سَيِّئَة. He tells two kinds apart: good by reason and the Law — a good deed (6:160) — and what is pleasant to one's nature (7:131).
