Particles · particle of the إِنَّ family

كَأَنْas if

9× in the Quran

What it does

The lightened كَأَنَّ: it means the same, ‘as if’, and stays in the إِنَّ family, but its noun is not spoken – a pronoun is understood – and its predicate is a whole sentence. In the Quran all nine times لَمْ follows it: كَأَن لَّمْ يَغْنَوْا۟ فِيهَا – “As though they had not dwelt there” (11:68).

Uses

‘As if… not…’

particle of the إِنَّ family · corpus: 9×

A comparison with a state that never was: a people wiped out as though they had never dwelt in their homes, a man who turns his back as though the words had never reached his ears.
