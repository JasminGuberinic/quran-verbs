Nuances

Protector and helper

وَلِيّ · نَصِير

What they share

Both words denote one who is on your side and stands up for you; in the Quran they often stand together, and they differ in the kind of support.

How to tell them apart

A close protector, one who takes charge of your affairs, a friend, a kinsman → وَلِيّ; a helper who aids against an enemy and gives victory → نَصِير.

وَلِيّ protector, ally, guardian

86× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, from a nearness with nothing between the two. So وَلِيّ means protector, friend, ally and nearest kinsman — one who takes charge of you.

نَصِير helper

35× in the Quran · Noun page →

One who helps and gives victory (نَصْر) against an enemy or a hardship. It often comes right after وَلِيّ or مَوْلَى.
