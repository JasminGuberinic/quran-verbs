What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبَهِيمَةُ

    the animal (životinja) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْبَهِيمَةَ

    the animal (životinju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْبَهِيمَةِ

    from the animal (od životinje) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْبَهِيمَةِ

    in the animal (na životinji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَحْمُ الْبَهِيمَةِ

    the meat of the animal (meso životinje) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    بَهِيمَتُهُ

    his/its animal (njegova životinja) · Bosnian nominative
