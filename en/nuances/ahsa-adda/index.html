Nuances

Enumerating and counting

أَحْصَى · عَدَّ

What they share

Both words mean to count. According to ar-Rāġib, عَدّ is joining numbers one to another, while إِحْصَاء is to obtain a thing by number, to encompass it fully; the word comes from حَصًى, the pebbles once used for counting.

How to tell them apart

To count, to number; also to reckon someone among → عَدَّ; to enumerate all, to encompass fully by number → أَحْصَى.

أَحْصَى to enumerate fully

10× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, to obtain a thing by number and encompass it. With Allah as subject nothing escapes (72:28); with people, it is mostly what they cannot do — Allah's favours cannot be enumerated (14:34).

عَدَّ to count; to reckon among

7× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, joining numbers one to another. In the Quran it can also mean to reckon someone among (38:62). The count alone does not say a thing is encompassed: people count, but cannot enumerate all (14:34).
