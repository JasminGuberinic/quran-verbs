What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمَسْجِدُ

    the mosque (džamija) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمَسْجِدَ

    the mosque (džamiju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمَسْجِدِ

    in the mosque (u džamiji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمَسْجِدِ

    from the mosque (iz džamije) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    the door of the mosque (vrata džamije) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مَسْجِدُهُ

    his/its mosque (njegova džamija) · Bosnian nominative
