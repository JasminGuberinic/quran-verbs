What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النَّاصِحُ

    the advisor (savjetnik) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النَّاصِحَ

    the advisor (savjetnika) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ النَّاصِحِ

    from the advisor (od savjetnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي النَّاصِحِ

    in the advisor (u savjetniku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    صَوْتُ النَّاصِحِ

    the voice of the advisor (glas savjetnika) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَاصِحُهُ

    his/its advisor (njegov savjetnik) · Bosnian nominative
