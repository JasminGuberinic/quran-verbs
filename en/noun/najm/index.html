What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلنَّجْمُ

    the star (zvijezda) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النَّجْمَ

    the star (zvijezdu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    بِٱلنَّجْمِ

    with the star (zvijezdom) · Bosnian instrumental

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلنَّجْمِ

    in the star (na zvijezdi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    نُورُ ٱلنَّجْمِ

    the light of the star (svjetlost zvijezde) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    نَجْمُهُ

    his/its star (njegova zvijezda) · Bosnian nominative
