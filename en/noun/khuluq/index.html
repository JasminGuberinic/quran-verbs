What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخُلُقُ

    the character (narav) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخُلُقَ

    the character (narav) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْخُلُقِ

    on the character (na naravi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخُلُقِ

    in the character (u naravi) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    أَهَمِّيَّةُ الْخُلُقِ

    the importance of the character (važnost naravi) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خُلُقُهُ

    his/its character (njegova narav) · Bosnian nominative
