Nuances

To give

آتَى · وَهَبَ · أَعْطَى

What they share

All three verbs mean ‘to give’; they differ in what is given and how.

How to tell them apart

The most frequent verb of giving — zakat, Scripture, wisdom, kingship → آتَى; giving as a gift without return, often of children and mercy → وَهَبَ; a rare, general word for giving → أَعْطَى.

آتَى to give

271× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, īṭāʾ is giving, and the Quran expresses the giving of zakat with this very verb. The most frequent verb of giving: Scripture, wisdom, kingship, wealth.

وَهَبَ to grant, to bestow

22× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, hiba is making what is yours another's without return. In the Quran it is often said of the gift of children and of Allah's mercy.

أَعْطَى to give

8× in the Quran · Verb page →

A general word for giving, rare in the Quran. Said both of Allah's gift and of human giving.
