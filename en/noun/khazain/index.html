What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْخَزَائِنُ

    the storehouses (riznice) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْخَزَائِنَ

    the storehouses (riznice) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى الْخَزَائِنِ

    on the storehouses (na riznicama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْخَزَائِنِ

    in the storehouses (u riznicama) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    مِفْتَاحُ الْخَزَائِنِ

    the key of the storehouses (ključ riznica) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    خَزَآئِنُهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its storehouses (njegove riznice) · Bosnian nominative
