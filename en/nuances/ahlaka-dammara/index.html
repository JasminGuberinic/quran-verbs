Nuances

To destroy

أَهْلَكَ · دَمَّرَ

What they share

Both verbs mean ‘to destroy’; they differ in breadth: perishing in any form, or utter destruction.

How to tell them apart

To destroy, cause to perish — peoples, but also crops and wealth → أَهْلَكَ; to wipe out, raze to the ground → دَمَّرَ.

أَهْلَكَ to destroy, to cause to perish

51× in the Quran · Verb page →

To destroy, bring to ruin. Most often of peoples Allah destroyed for their sins, but also of a man who ruins crops or squanders wealth.

دَمَّرَ to destroy utterly

8× in the Quran · Verb page →

To destroy completely, leaving nothing. In the Quran (our observation) almost always of Allah's punishment of peoples and their works; once of the wind that destroys “by commandment of its Lord” (46:25).
