What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعِظَامُ

    the bones (kosti) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْعِظَٰمَ

    the bones (kosti) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْعِظَامِ

    to the bones (do kostiju) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْعِظَامِ

    in the bones (u kostima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ ٱلْعِظَامِ

    the strength of the bones (snaga kostiju) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عِظَامَهُۥ from the Quran

    his/its bones (njegove kosti) · Bosnian accusative
