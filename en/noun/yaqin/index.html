What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْيَقِينُ

    the certainty (sigurnost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْيَقِينَ

    the certainty (sigurnost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْيَقِينِ

    in the certainty (u sigurnosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ

    from the certainty (od sigurnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    دَرَجَةُ ٱلْيَقِينِ

    the degree of the certainty (stepen sigurnosti) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    يَقِينُهُ

    his/its certainty (njegova sigurnost) · Bosnian nominative
