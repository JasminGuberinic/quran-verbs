Nuances

To call and to call out

دَعَا · نَادَى

What they share

Both verbs mean ‘to call’; they differ in whether a raised voice or a request and an address is in view.

How to tell them apart

To call, to pray, to invoke — Allah too, in supplication → دَعَا; to call out aloud so as to be heard, often from afar → نَادَى.

دَعَا to call, to invoke

170× in the Quran · Verb page →

To call someone by name, to invite, to pray; hence دُعَاء, supplication. It is said of praying to Allah, but also of invoking false gods and of inviting people to something.

نَادَى to call out

44× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, nidāʾ is raising the voice so it is heard. Hence نَادَى often stands where someone calls from afar or aloud — the people of Paradise call out to the people of the Fire.
