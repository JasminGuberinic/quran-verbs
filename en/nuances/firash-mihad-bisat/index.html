Nuances

Bed, resting-place and carpet

فِرَاش · مِهَاد · بِسَاط

What they share

All three words describe the earth as something to rest and walk on — a favour from Allah. They differ in the image: a bed that is spread and made tractable, a resting-place prepared and smoothed like a child's cradle, or a carpet spread wide.

How to tell them apart

Spread to rest on, the earth made ‘tractable’ — and the couches of the Garden → فِرَاش; a prepared, smoothed resting-place — the earth, but mostly the ‘evil resting-place’ of Hell → مِهَاد; something spread out wide → بِسَاط.

فِرَاش bed

3× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, farš is spreading out cloth, and what is spread is called firāš. On 2:22 he says Allah made the earth tractable, not so remote that one could not settle on it. The plural فُرُش is the couches of the Garden (55:54, 56:34).

مِهَاد resting-place (made smooth)

7× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, mahd is what is prepared for a child (a cradle), and mahd and mihād are a place made smooth and trodden; he sets ‘We made the earth a resting-place’ beside 2:22 (a bed). In the Quran the earth is mihād only in 78:6; in the other six places it is Hell, five times ‘an evil resting-place’ — our observation.

بِسَاط carpet, spread

1× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, basṭ is spreading a thing out and widening it; bisāṭ is the name for anything spread out, and wide land is also called so. In the Quran only once, in Noah's words to his people (71:19).
