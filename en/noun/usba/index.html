What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعُصْبَةُ

    the group (skupina) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعُصْبَةَ

    the group (skupinu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْعُصْبَةِ

    in the group (u skupini) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْعُصْبَةِ

    from the group (iz skupine) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَائِدُ ٱلْعُصْبَةِ

    the leader of the group (vođa skupine) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عُصْبَتُهُ

    his/its group (njegova skupina) · Bosnian nominative
