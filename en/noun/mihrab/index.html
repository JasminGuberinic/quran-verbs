What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْمِحْرابُ

    the mihrab (mihrab) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْمِحْرَابَ

    the mihrab (mihrab) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْمِحْرَابِ

    in the mihrab (u mihrabu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْمِحْرَابِ

    from the mihrab (iz mihraba) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    بَابُ ٱلْمِحْرَابِ

    the door of the mihrab (vrata mihraba) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    مِحْرابُهُ

    his/its mihrab (njegov mihrab) · Bosnian nominative
