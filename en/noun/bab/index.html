What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْبابُ

    the door (kapija) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْبَابَ

    the door (kapiju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْبَابِ

    from the door (iz kapije) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْبَابِ

    in the door (na kapiji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    لَوْنُ ٱلْبَابِ

    the colour of the door (boja kapije) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَبْوَٰبُهَا from the Quran

    her/its doors (njene kapije) · Bosnian nominative
