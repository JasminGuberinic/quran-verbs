Nuances

To know and to recognise

عَلِمَ · عَرَفَ

What they share

Both verbs mean ‘to know’; they differ in how the knowledge is reached and in who is said to know.

How to tell them apart

Knowledge in general, and Allah's knowledge → عَلِمَ; recognising what is reached by reflection and by its traces, the opposite of denial → عَرَفَ.

عَلِمَ to know

382× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ‘grasping a thing in its reality’. The general word for knowledge; it is used of Allah's knowledge, which encompasses the hidden and the open.

عَرَفَ to know, to recognise

20× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, knowledge reached by reflecting on a thing's traces; narrower than ʿilm, and its opposite is denial (inkār). Hence, he says, a person is said to ‘recognise’ Allah, while Allah is said to ‘know’.
