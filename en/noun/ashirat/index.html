What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعَشِيرَةُ

    the clan (klan) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعَشِيرَةَ

    the clan (klan) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْعَشِيرَةِ

    in the clan (u klanu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْعَشِيرَةِ

    from the clan (iz klana) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    كَبِيرُ الْعَشِيرَةِ

    the elder of the clan (starješina klana) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    عَشِيرَتَكَ from the Quran

    your clan (tvoj klan) · Bosnian accusative
