Great

كَبِير · عَظِيم

What they share

Both words mean ‘great’ and both are names of Allah (الْكَبِير, الْعَظِيم); they differ in what makes the greatness.

How to tell them apart

Great, often in relation to something else — in size, age, rank → كَبِير; great in itself, magnificent (from ʿaẓm, bone) → عَظِيم.

كَبِير great, big; old

40× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, ‘great’ and ‘small’ are relative terms: a thing is great compared with another. It is also said of age and of rank.

عَظِيم great, magnificent

107× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, at root: big of bone (ʿaẓm), then said of every great thing, perceptible or intelligible. Often with what inspires awe: the Throne, punishment, the Prophet's character.

Sources

  • ar-Rāġib al-Iṣfahānī, Mufradāt alfāẓ al-Qurʾān — entries كبر, عظم
