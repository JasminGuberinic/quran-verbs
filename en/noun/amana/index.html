What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْأَمانَةُ

    the trust (amanet) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْأَمانَةَ

    the trust (amanet) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْأَمانَةِ

    in the trust (u amanetu) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْأَمانَةِ

    from the trust (od amaneta) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    حِفْظُ الْأَمانَةِ

    the keeping of the trust (čuvanje amaneta) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    أَمَٰنَٰتِكُمْ from the Quran

    your trusts (vaše amanete) · Bosnian accusative
