Nuances

The Fire

نَار · جَحِيم · سَعِير

What they share

All three words denote fire and in the Quran most often the punishment of the hereafter; they differ in whether fire in general or its fierceness and blaze is in view.

How to tell them apart

Fire in general — of this world and of the hereafter → نَار; a fierce, blazing fire → جَحِيم; a kindled flame, a blaze → سَعِير.

نَار fire

145× in the Quran · Noun page →

The general word for fire: the fire Moses saw, the fire Ibrāhīm was thrown into, and the Fire of the hereafter, when ‘the Fire’ stands alone.

جَحِيم blazing fire, Hellfire

26× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, from a word for the fierce blazing of fire. In the Quran a name for the Fire of the hereafter, and once for the fire prepared for Ibrāhīm.

سَعِير blaze

16× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, from a verb meaning to kindle a fire: a flame that is stoked. In the Quran always of the hereafter (our observation), often in ‘the punishment of the Blaze’.
