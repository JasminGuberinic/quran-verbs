What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلْفِتْنَةُ

    the trial (smutnja) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلْفِتْنَةَ

    the trial (smutnju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    إِلَى ٱلْفِتْنَةِ

    to the trial (do smutnje) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلْفِتْنَةِ

    in the trial (u smutnji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    زَمَنُ ٱلْفِتْنَةِ

    the time of the trial (vrijeme smutnje) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    فِتْنَتُهُ

    his/its trial (njegova smutnja) · Bosnian nominative
