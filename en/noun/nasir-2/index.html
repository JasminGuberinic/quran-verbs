What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    النَّاصِرُ

    the supporter (pomagač) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    النَّاصِرَ

    the supporter (pomagača) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ النَّاصِرِ

    from the supporter (od pomagača) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي النَّاصِرِ

    in the supporter (u pomagaču) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    اسْمُ النَّاصِرِ

    the name of the supporter (ime pomagača) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    ناصِرُهُ

    his/its supporter (njegov pomagač) · Bosnian nominative
