Nuances

Forgetting and heedlessness

نَسِيَ · غَافِل

What they share

Both words speak of a person not keeping in mind what he should. According to ar-Rāġib, heedlessness (غَفْلَة) is one of the causes of forgetting: one forgets from weakness, from heedlessness, or on purpose.

How to tell them apart

To lose hold of what one was entrusted with — excused when not one's own doing, blamed when its cause lies in the person himself → نَسِيَ; to be heedless, unaware, off one's guard → غَافِل.

نَسِيَ to forget

35× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, losing from the heart what was entrusted to it — from weakness, from heedlessness, or on purpose. Allah blames only the forgetting a person brought on himself; said of Allah it means He leaves them, in return for what they left (the Quran itself says Allah does not forget, 19:64).

غَافِل heedless, unaware

27× in the Quran · Adjective page →

According to ar-Rāġib, غَفْلَة is an oversight that comes over a person from too little care and alertness. The Quran often says Allah is not غَافِل (unaware) of what people do, and warns against being among the heedless.
