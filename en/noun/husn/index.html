What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْحُسْنُ

    the beauty (ljepota) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْحُسْنَ

    the beauty (ljepotu) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الْحُسْنِ

    in the beauty (u ljepoti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الْحُسْنِ

    from the beauty (od ljepote) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    سِرُّ الْحُسْنِ

    the secret of the beauty (tajna ljepote) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    حُسْنُهُنَّ from the Quran

    their beauty (njihova ljepota) · Bosnian nominative
