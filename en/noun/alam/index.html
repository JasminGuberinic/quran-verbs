What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الْعالَمُونَ

    the worlds (svjetovi) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الْعالَمِينَ

    the worlds (svjetove) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    عَلَى ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

    on the worlds (na svjetovima) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

    from the worlds (od svjetova) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    خَرِيطَةُ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

    the map of the worlds (karta svjetova) · Bosnian genitive
