Nuances

Dust, clay and dry clay

تُرَاب · طِين · صَلْصَال · حَمَأ

What they share

All four are matter from which, according to the Quran, man was created. According to ar-Rāġib, طِين is earth (تُرَاب) mixed with water, صَلْصَال is dried clay, and حَمَأ is black, foul-smelling mud. Many mufassirun see them as stages of one and the same matter.

How to tell them apart

Dry earth, dust → تُرَاب; earth mixed with water → طِين; dried clay that rings → صَلْصَال; black, altered mud → حَمَأ.

تُرَاب dust

17× in the Quran · Noun page →

Dry earth; according to ar-Rāġib, تُرَاب is the earth itself. In the Quran it is the beginning of man's creation (30:20), and in eight ayat the deniers ask whether they will be brought back once they have become dust, voicing disbelief in the resurrection.

طِين moist clay (earth mixed with water)

12× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, earth mixed with water; it may keep the name even when the water's strength has gone from it. In the Quran mostly the matter of Adam's creation, but also the birds Jesus shapes by Allah's permission and the bricks Pharaoh has fired (28:38).

صَلْصَال dry ringing clay

4× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, the root is the echoing sound of a dry thing, so dried clay is called صَلْصَال. He also reports a view that it is foul, spoilt clay. Only four times in the Quran, always of man's creation.

حَمَأ black mud

3× in the Quran

According to ar-Rāġib, black clay with a foul smell. Three times in the Quran, always in the same phrase with صَلْصَال (15:26, 28, 33).
