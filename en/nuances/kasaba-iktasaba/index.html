Nuances

To earn and to acquire

كَسَبَ · اِكْتَسَبَ

What they share

Both verbs mean ‘to earn, to gain’ — good deeds and bad; they differ in the effort involved and in for whom the gain is made.

How to tell them apart

To earn, gain in general — wealth, good or evil → كَسَبَ; to acquire for oneself (tafsir: with effort) → اِكْتَسَبَ.

كَسَبَ to earn, to gain

62× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, what a person seeks to gain benefit and a share by. The general word: earned wealth, good deeds and sins — all that a person ‘earns’.

اِكْتَسَبَ to acquire, to earn for oneself

5× in the Quran · Verb page →

According to ar-Rāġib, said only of what a person gains for himself. Many mufassirun add that Form VIII implies effort and explain 2:286 by it, though not all accept this, and 4:32 uses it for the lawful earnings of men and women.
