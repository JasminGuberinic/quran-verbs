What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الشَّاهِدُ

    the witness (svjedok) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الشَّاهِدَ

    the witness (svjedoka) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الشَّاهِدِ

    from the witness (od svjedoka) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الشَّاهِدِ

    in the witness (u svjedoku) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قَوْلُ الشَّاهِدِ

    the statement of the witness (izjava svjedoka) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    شَاهِدُهُ

    his/its witness (njegov svjedok) · Bosnian nominative
