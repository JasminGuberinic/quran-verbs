Righteousness and doing good

بِرّ · إِحْسَان

What they share

Both words speak of the good a person does. According to ar-Rāġib, بِرّ is breadth in doing good, while إِحْسَان is giving more than one owes — above justice.

How to tell them apart

Righteousness, breadth in goodness — in belief and in deeds (2:177) → بِرّ; doing good to others and doing things well — more than what is due (beside justice in 16:90) → إِحْسَان.

بِرّ righteousness

8× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, from بَرّ (land, wide space): breadth in doing good — in belief and in deeds, as 2:177 lists them. Said of Allah (الْبَرّ, 52:28) it means His reward, as from the servant it means obedience.

إِحْسَان doing good, excellence

12× in the Quran · Noun page →

According to ar-Rāġib, in two ways: doing good to another, and doing one's own work well. It is above justice: justice is giving what one owes and taking what one is owed, إِحْسَان giving more and taking less. The Quran uses it for how one treats parents.
