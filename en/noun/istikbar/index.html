What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    الِاسْتِكْبَارُ

    the arrogance (oholost) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    الِاسْتِكْبَارَ

    the arrogance (oholost) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي الِاسْتِكْبَارِ

    in the arrogance (u oholosti) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ الِاسْتِكْبَارِ

    from the arrogance (od oholosti) · Bosnian genitive
