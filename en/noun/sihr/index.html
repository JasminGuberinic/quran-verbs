What each case does

Arabic has three cases; Bosnian seven. This is how they match, shown with this noun.

  • Nominative: the subject — who or what does or is something.

    ٱلسِّحْرُ

    the magic (magija) · Bosnian nominative

  • Accusative: the object — whom or what.

    ٱلسِّحْرَ

    the magic (magiju) · Bosnian accusative

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    مِنَ ٱلسِّحْرِ

    from the magic (iz magije) · Bosnian genitive

  • Genitive after a preposition — the preposition chooses the Bosnian case.

    فِي ٱلسِّحْرِ

    in the magic (u magiji) · Bosnian locative

  • Genitive as the possessor (iḍāfa): “the boy's book”.

    قُوَّةُ ٱلسِّحْرِ

    the strength of the magic (snaga magije) · Bosnian genitive

  • A possessive suffix: “his, her, their…”.

    سِحْرُهُ

    his/its magic (njegova magija) · Bosnian nominative
